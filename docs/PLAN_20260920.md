## 2. The contradiction that Stage A must resolve first

Two records name different blocking rows for the same cases.

  frozen at 1.52e-01 from pass 12 for `scalar_x0.01/HeH2.13`, the energy row

Cell 1 is the base. Cells 245 and 248 are in the wind. A base row and a wind
row are different problems with different admissible methods, so the plan
cannot proceed until it is settled which measurement describes the state that
is on disk now and under which binary each was taken. This is the first
deliverable and it is cheap: both numbers come from a certification report on
a stored state, not from a solve.

The D9fix evidence (READ, `lhs1140b_stationary_D9fix_20260919.md` section 6)
is about the **certificates of the parent generations**, not about these
rows: under the accepted contact law the base face flux of the three parents
stands at 38.4, 82.2 and 177.8 times the window mean, against 0.99998 under
the blended face that D2b removed. That is why the old certificates were

## 4. Stage A: does the residual repeat at fixed inputs

Until the residual is a function of the state alone, no condition number,
spectrum or step length measured on it means anything.

1. Reload each state and evaluate the stationary residual twice in the same
   process and once in a fresh process, single threaded. Require bitwise
   equality of the row norms across all three.
2. Restore and record the full evaluation context, not the state pair alone:
   the radiation context, the elimination state, the branch decisions, the
   column and row scalings, the pseudo-time shift and the reconstruction
   state. Any of these carried over from a previous iteration makes the
   residual path dependent.
3. Repeat at `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` and at the thread count the campaign used.
   A difference here is a reduction-order effect and sets a floor for
   everything measured afterwards.

Instruments that exist: `EXHALE_RESIDUAL=1`, `EXHALE_RESID_ROW_CELLS`,
`EXHALE_RESID_BRANCH_REPORT=1` (stops the run and prints the branch
decisions), `EXHALE_JUDGED_ROWS`, `EXHALE_JUDGED_MERIT` (VERIFIED present in
`src/modules/time_step/steady_newton.f90`).

**Acceptance**: the row that blocks, its cell, and its value repeat exactly.
**If it fails**: stop. The finding is that the residual is not a function of
the state, and the repair is in the evaluation, not in the solver.

## 5. Stage B: the resolved interval of perturbation lengths

A forward difference carries a truncation error proportional to the step and
an evaluation noise divided by it, so there is an interval of steps in which
the difference describes the derivative, and outside it the difference
describes nothing. A condition number taken outside that interval is not a
measurement.

1. Sweep the probe length over decades in the direction the solver actually
   takes, with `EXHALE_JV_PROBE_ARC=<x>` and with the probe taken on the
   column scales (`EXHALE_JV_COLUMN_SCALE=1`), and plot the response against
   the step. Identify the plateau, or report that there is none.
2. Check that the direction crosses no active bound and no contact branch
   over that interval, with `EXHALE_RESID_JUMP_SCAN=1`, which samples the
   residual densely along one direction and names the term and the operator
   where it jumps.
3. Measure additivity of the matrix-free action across directions with
   `EXHALE_JV_ADDITIVITY=1`. A nonadditive action is not a linear operator
   and no Krylov method converges on it.

**Acceptance**: a plateau of at least one decade in which the directional
difference is stable to a stated fraction, and no jump inside it.
**If there is no plateau**: the finding is a noise floor above the signal, and
the admissible responses are listed in section 9.

## 6. Stage C: four operators, measured apart

The name "the Jacobian" covers four different objects here, and the failure
of the solve is a statement about one of them.

1. The unshifted scaled Jacobian action.
2. The pseudo-time shifted system that is actually solved.
3. The preconditioned operator the Krylov iteration sees:
   `EXHALE_PRECOND_SPECTRUM=1` gives its Ritz values on the whole operator and
   on its compressions onto the species rows and the hydrodynamic rows, and
   `EXHALE_BAND_DIFFERENCE=1` gives the difference between the full action and
   the banded one.
4. The true linear residual of the Krylov solve against the nonlinear trial
   residual: `EXHALE_GM_TRUE_RESIDUAL=1`, `EXHALE_GM_HISTORY`,
   `EXHALE_KRYLOV_SIZE_SCAN=1` (the same cycle repeated at 40, 80, 160 and 320
   products, and the last two with the basis orthogonalized twice, adopting
   none of the steps).

Attribute the blocking row term by term with `EXHALE_FRONT_ROW=1` and read its
conditioning with `EXHALE_RESID_ROW_CONDITION=1`.

**What separates the cases**: if the linear residual is small and the
nonlinear trial residual does not fall, the linearization is right and the
step is outside its trust radius. If the linear residual itself stalls, the
operator or its preconditioner is the problem. These require different
methods, which is the point of measuring them apart.

## 7. Stage D: the record of accepted steps

For every outer pass: the accepted step length, the change in the state and in
the merit, every bound, limiter and branch event, and every reason a counter
was reset. A stall that is really a sequence of steps cut at the same bound is
a different finding from a stall in which full steps are taken and the merit
does not fall. `EXHALE_TR_TRACE`, `EXHALE_SPECIES_BOUND_HOLD`,
`EXHALE_SPECIES_BOUND_PROJECT` and the PTC ramp reports carry this.

## 9. What each outcome admits

The stages are designed so that each possible finding names its own method.
None of these is adopted by this plan.

| finding | reading | admissible method |
|---|---|---|
| the residual does not repeat (A) | the evaluation carries state between calls | repair the evaluation. No solver question exists until then |
| no resolved interval, action nonadditive (B) | the action is dominated by evaluation noise | raise the evaluation accuracy of the rows that carry the noise, or ask the Krylov cycle for an absolute tolerance above the floor (`EXHALE_KRYLOV_TOL_ABS`), or supply the row analytically |
| the residual jumps inside the interval (B) | a branch or a limiter inside the step | the nonsmooth term, not the solver, is the object. Either the branch is smoothed with a stated width or the step is confined below it |
| linear residual small, nonlinear trial residual flat (C) | the step leaves the region where the model holds | the scaled trust region (`EXHALE_TRUST_REGION=1`, `EXHALE_TR_DELTA0`), the choice of shifted or unshifted leg (`EXHALE_TR_SHIFTED_LEG`), truncation on the ball (`EXHALE_KRYLOV_ON_THE_BALL`) |
| the linear residual itself stalls, spectrum spread over decades (C) | row scaling, not step control | row equilibration of the linear model (`EXHALE_MODEL_ROW_EQUIL`, `EXHALE_JUDGED_ROWS`), band factorization of the preconditioner (`EXHALE_PRECON_EQUIL`) |
| one row at one cell stands while all others fall, and equilibration does not move it (C, D) | the state and that equation are incompatible where it stands | no step control helps. Go to Stage F |
| steps are cut at the same bound every pass (D) | the active set, not the smooth problem | the treatment of the bound (hold against project), or a formulation that carries the constraint |

## 10. Stage F, on a further decision only: continuation

If Stage C and Stage D leave one row standing at one cell against every
scaling, the question is no longer how to take a step but whether a stationary
solution exists at these parameters that the present seed can reach. The
instrument for that is continuation in the XUV scale, and the ladder is
already built in one direction.

MEASURED, `LHS1140b/models/*/state_index.json`, 2026-09-20: the scalar
`kzz1e9` family is certified at x1, x0.15 and x0.10 and open at x0.01; the
photochem `kzzprofile` family is certified at x0.33, x0.30, x0.25, x0.20, x0.15
and x0.10 and open at x0.01 (its fiducial x1 directory carries no state index
at all, MEASURED). **There is no rung between 0.10 and 0.01**,
a factor of ten in one step, which is where the ladder stops.

The design, if it is opened: descend from the certified x0.10 in factors small
enough that each rung certifies, seeding each from the rung above, and record
at every rung the blocking row, its cell and its margin. Three outcomes, all
of them informative:

- The rungs certify down to 0.01. The finding is that the solve needed a path,
  not a control, and the three states close.
- The rungs certify to some scale and then refuse, with the margin of one row
  growing smoothly along the way. The finding is a limit, and the row and the
  cell say which physics sets it. This is a physical result about very weak
  winds at this base, not a solver failure.
- The rungs refuse immediately below 0.10 while x0.10 itself remains
  certified. The finding sits with the step between rungs, and it returns the
  question to Stage C with a much cheaper reproduction.

## 11. What no stage here can decide

Whether a stationary solution exists at 0.01 of the fiducial XUV. No residual,
spectrum or trust radius measured on a state proves existence or its absence.
The strongest statement available at the end of Stage F is a limit: the scale
below which every attempt refuses and the row that refuses it. If that is
where it ends, the honest report is that the code finds no stationary solution
below that scale at this base, with the row named, and that a time dependent
state may be the physical answer for those winds. That sentence, with its
evidence, is an acceptable end of this plan.

## 14. M1: the molecular models whose chemistry rows refuse

### 14.1 Why they are not P2

P2 is a signature, not a chemistry: the hydrodynamic rows stand still between
passes while the composition relaxes, and more budget changes nothing. The
four molecular models below do not show it. MEASURED, the newest
`certification.txt` of each case and the case's own `run.log`:

| case | `du` | `norm(R)` | hydrodynamic rows | what refuses, gated at r >= 1.20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH0.083` | 4.879e-12 | 1.369e-08 | all within | carrier H2 8.150e-04 at cell 499, elemental He/H 2.503e-04 at cell 280 |
| `molecular_scalar_gj1132_wellmixed/HeH0.083` | 6.592e-12 | 2.701e-08 | all within | carrier H2 7.371e-02 at cell 500 |
| `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH0.55` | 1.408e-02 | 1.954e-01 | energy 4.634e-01 at cell 257 | carrier H2 8.922e-03 at cell 261 |
| `molecular_scalar_gj1132_wellmixed/HeH0.55` | 9.086e-13 | 2.893e-04 | energy 1.000e+00 at cell 246 | carrier H2 2.363e-02 at cell 306 |

The two `HeH0.083` models are solved: every hydrodynamic row is inside its
tolerance and the stationary residual norm stands at 1e-08. Nothing about
their solve is stalled. The two `HeH0.55` models carry a refusing energy row
as well, which makes them a different question, kept apart below.

### 14.2 The finding that reframes the item

MEASURED, `src/modules/files_IO/input_read.f90` (the key documentation at the
head of the file and the parse at the `Coupled carrier solve` label):
the key is three valued and **defaults to False**, and False means the route
is an alternation of a hydrodynamic solve with fixed-wind transport
relaxations. The H2 carrier density and the diffused element fractions are
then **not Newton unknowns**. READ, the input files: none of the four sets the
key to True.

This explains the table without any appeal to a solver defect. The residual
norm measures the rows the Newton solves, so `norm(R) = 1.4e-08` and a carrier
row at 8.2e-04 are consistent statements about two different systems. Nothing
in those solves was ever driving the row that refuses them.

**The item is therefore not "why does the solver stall" but "does the
alternation have the joint fixed point, and if not, what reaches it".**

### 14.3 What to measure

1. **Is the alternation approaching a joint fixed point?** Record the carrier
   and elemental gated row measures pass by pass, on all four cases. A
   geometric fall is a budget statement. A flat sequence says the alternation
   has a fixed point that is not the joint one, and the joint route is the
   answer rather than more passes.
2. **Where the rows refuse.** MEASURED, the radii of the refusing cells of
   these states: cell 280 is r = 1.584, cell 306 is r = 1.919, and cells 499
   and 500 are r = 27.6 and 28.1, the **two outermost cells of the 500**. A
   row that stands only in the last cells is a statement about the outer
   condition of the carrier, not about the wind. Compare the row measure over
   cells 490 to 500 with the interior, and repeat with the outer radius moved.
   If it follows the boundary, it is the boundary.
3. **Run the route the code already carries.** `Coupled carrier solve: True`
   solves n(H2) and the diffused element fractions as Newton unknowns beside
   the three hydrodynamic ones; `On stall` starts from the alternation and
   switches at the pass where it stops approaching a joint fixed point. Run
   both on the two `HeH0.083` states, which start from a converged
   hydrodynamic solution, and judge by the certification of the refreshed
   state. This is the existing method for exactly this failure and it costs
   one solve per case.

### 14.4 The two `HeH0.55` models, kept apart

Their energy row measure is 1.000 at cell 246 and 4.634e-01 at cell 257, that
is r = 1.322 and r = 1.390 (MEASURED from the states). A row measure of one
means the residual equals the row's own largest term: that cell's energy
equation is not approximately satisfied, it is not satisfied at all. Both
cells sit just above the certification's wind gate at r = 1.20, in the band
where H2 is still dissociating.

A candidate that the records already name, to be checked first because it is
cheap: MEASURED from the same reports, the out-of-domain closure activations
are 334 for `kzz1e9/HeH0.083` (H3+ collider density below the table 272,
outside the non-LTE range 62), 428 for `kzz1e9/HeH0.55` (396 and 32), and 526
for `wellmixed/HeH0.55` (474 below the table, 24 **above** the temperature
fits, 28 outside the non-LTE range). The worst energy row belongs to the case
with the most activations and it is the only one with activations above the
temperature fits. Establish whether the refusing cells are the cells where the
H3+ cooling closure is evaluated outside its table. If they are, the item is
the cooling closure's domain and its behavior at the edge, not the solver.

### 14.5 What each outcome means

- The joint route certifies the `HeH0.083` states. The finding is that the
  alternation, not the physics, held them, and the catalog gains two models.
- The rows fall geometrically and only the budget was short. Same conclusion,
  cheaper method.
- The carrier row follows the outer boundary. The item moves to the carrier's
  outer condition, and the row measure there stops being evidence about the
  wind.
- The energy row coincides with the out-of-domain H3+ evaluations. The item
  moves to the cooling closure's domain, which is a physics repair with its
  own validity range to be written at the code.

## 15. M2: the atomic-to-molecular conversion of `molecular_photochem_gj1132_kzzprofile/HeH9`

This model has never been given the treatment P2 describes. MEASURED, its own
`not_solved.md` (2026-09-18, binary 3146d11b): **one** outer pass at each of
two pseudo-time starts, hydrodynamic `info = 2` in both, and the ending was
that the element composition relaxation of that first pass found no admissible
advance and restored its entry composition. The rows at that point were the
mass row 2.254e-01 at cell 2 against a tolerance of 2.2e-11, the energy row
3.554e-01 at cell 43, and the carrier balance H2 at 1.000 at cell 432
(r = 9.568).

The seed is the origin of all of it: the state was built by converting this
case's own certified **atomic** wind, the closure iterate
`atomic_photochem_gj1132_kzzprofile/HeH9/k04`, into a molecular one. The
conversion leaves the hydrodynamic rows at the base two decades outside their
tolerances, and the first relaxation then has nothing admissible to do.

The plan:

1. **Measure the conversion itself**, before any solve. Evaluate the
   hydrodynamic rows of the converted state cell by cell and compare with the
   atomic state it came from. The atomic state is certified, so every decade
   the conversion adds is the conversion's own.
2. **Name what the conversion does not carry.** The atomic state states one
   composition; the molecular one states H2, H2+, H3+ and HeH+ and their
   elemental reservoirs. Establish which quantity is reconstructed rather than
   carried, and whether the base level of the profile is preserved through it.
   A conversion that changes the base level changes the hydrostatic balance of
   cell 1, which is where the refusal is.
3. **Try the other seeds.** The certified molecular scalar solution of the
   nearest reservoir is refused by `load_IC` because a `scalar` case is metal
   free while this column carries C/H, N/H and O/H (READ, the same memo).
   That refusal is correct. What remains is a seed built at this case's own
   composition, for instance by relaxing the molecular chemistry at fixed
   hydrodynamics from the atomic state before any hydrodynamic pass.
4. Only then a solve, and with the M1 question already settled, since this
   case also runs with the alternation.

Until step 1 is done, nothing about this model is evidence about the solver.

## 16. Order and cost

1. Section 2, the contradiction. Certification reports on the three stored
   states. Minutes.
2. Stage A. Two evaluations per case in two processes. Minutes.
3. **M1 step 3 and M2 step 1 can run beside Stage A**: they are independent of
   P2 and of each other, and M1 step 3 is one solve per case on a state that
   is already hydrodynamically converged.
4. Stage B. Decades of probe lengths, the jump scan and the additivity test on
   one outer iteration per case, and the same on the certified x0.10 control.
   The additivity test costs about twenty residual evaluations at each of
   three outer iterations and the jump scan about seventy at one, so this is
   the first stage with a real cost.
5. Stage C, on one outer iteration per case, with the Krylov size scan.
6. Stage D, over the passes already recorded where they suffice, and over a
   short bounded solve where they do not.
7. M1 sections 14.3 and 14.4, and M2 steps 2 and 3.
8. Report, with the decision table of section 9 filled in and the M1 outcomes
   of section 14.5 named.

Stages A to D need no new solve to completion, so the wall ceilings that
stopped the campaign do not bind this work. M1 step 3 does need one solve per
case, from a converged state. Only Stage F needs a campaign, and Stage F is a
separate decision.
