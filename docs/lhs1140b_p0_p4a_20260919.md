**What was done.** `models/status.py` takes a subcommand:

    python3 models/status.py inventory --write

It walks the tree, judges each index by one written criterion, and writes
`models/CASE_INVENTORY.md` with `models/CASE_INVENTORY.json` beside it: the
selection rule in words and the name of the function that IS the rule, the
instant it was taken, the binary of record with its md5 and its source list
(both MEASURED at the moment of writing), the criterion for certified, the
totals by kind of directory, and one row per index carrying its path, its
kind, `latest_complete` and its ending class, `latest_certified`, the md5 of
the binary that certified it, and whether that certificate is stale.

**The selection rule.** One row per state index: every `state_index.json`
under `models/`, walked recursively, hidden directories included. An index is
what a reader resolves to get a state (`LHS1140b/MODELS.md` section 9.5), so
the count of indexes is the count of states a reader can be handed. Nothing
is excluded: closure rungs `k<NN>/`, `.L*` study directories,
`diffusion_check/`, preserved trees (`.stopped/`, `.ab/`) and archives
(`pre_*/`) are each one row, with a `kind` column. A narrower count is taken
by reading that column, never by walking the tree again with another rule.

**The criterion for certified.** The index names a `latest_certified`
generation and that generation's index entry carries no `stale_under` (nor
`certificate_stale_under`), which is the rule the publisher applies when it
moves the reference after a refused re-evaluation (section 9.8).

## 2. P4a, the termination classification

**What was wrong** (READ, and reproduced below on synthetic logs).
`classify_ending` of `models/run_case.sh` returned `solved` on `INFO = 0`
before it tested that a complete finite state existed, and it took `INFO` from
the last inner ` (JFNK|PTC) done info=` line when the outer route printed no
verdict. `solver_from_log` of `models/publish_state.py` repeated the same
fallback in its own code, so a manifest could carry a verdict no route gave.

**The repair.** Four facts are now separate and all recorded: the process
ended and by which end marker of the binary, the route stated its own outcome
and what it stated, a complete finite state of that pass is on disk, and the
state as written certified. The route is read first, from the run's own
`Restart intent:` echo, and only that route's terminal event is its verdict:
`the stationary solve returned info = N` for the stationary route, the
`measured` line for the evaluate route (which solves nothing and states no
solver verdict at all), and the marching loop's own `-> converged:` or
`-> stopped:` line for the marching route. An inner solver line is one pass
of the route and is carried as `inner_info`, as evidence.

The state is tested before any verdict is honored, so `state_missing`,
`nonfinite_state` and the new `stale_state` are reached from `info = 0` as
well. `stale_state` is a state OLDER than the pass the log records; the runner
already touches a mark when a pass begins and now hands it to
`classify_ending`, and without a mark the freshness is `unknown` and is never
assumed. A certificate counts as this state's only where the block is the
block of the state AS WRITTEN; a block a pass left behind is a verdict on a
state the run then moved away from.

Conflicting evidence is stated and not reconciled. The new class
`conflicting_evidence` is a route that claims a stationary solution whose
certification of the state as written refuses it, `info = 0` with a refusing
certificate being that case; the conflict is in the reason sentence and in
`ending.evidence.conflicts` of the manifest. The new class `marching_stop` is
a marching route that stopped without a stationary claim (a relaxation
snapshot), which `no_verdict` used to cover although `no_verdict` says
"nothing says the run ended rather than died", and the new class
`no_certificate` is a route that stated a verdict over a log that carries no
certification of the state it wrote.

**One rule, both readers.** `solver_verdict` and `classify_ending` of
`run_case.sh` are the one statement of the rule. `publish_state.py` reaches
them through a new `report_ending` with `RUN_CASE_POLICY_ONLY=1`
(`run_case_policy`, and `solver_from_log`, `ending_evidence` and
`classify_with_run_case` built on it) and carries no reading of its own;
`status.py`'s `solver_verdict` now delegates to the publisher's. A manifest
records the facts under `ending.evidence` and the index entry carries
`ending_conflicts`.

## 5. Noticed outside the scope of these two items, and not fixed

- **A record binds its log by PATH, and that path is reused.** A generation
  copies the certification block but names its log as
  `<case>/run.log`, which the next run of the case overwrites. Five records
  above are already in that position. The block copied into the generation is
  what makes the binding checkable at all, and it is what the measurement of
  section 4 used; the record itself would have to carry the log, or its md5,
  for the binding to be recoverable.
- **`ending.source` is sometimes a path and sometimes a sentence**
  ("classified from pp.log by classify_ending ..."), so a reader that wants
  the log has to parse prose. It is one field carrying two kinds of thing.
- **The state's own `run=` stamp is not compared with the run** anywhere in
  the classification. It is recorded now (`state_written`), which is what a
  later binding rule would need.


## Addendum, 2026-09-19 (advisor)

The marching route's `-> stopped:` line covers six different endings
(`EXHALE_main.f90` near line 4192): a NaN in the conserved state, the JFNK
finish, the flux-spread criterion, a state that stopped changing, a du
plateau, and a run that requested no time integration. Classifying all of
them as `marching_stop`, "a relaxation snapshot", is wrong in two of the six:
a state the march never advanced is not a snapshot of a march, and a NaN stop
is not a state to carry forward. The rule now reads the binary's own reason
and gives `no_integration` and `nonfinite_state` their own classes, carrying
the reason text into the record for the rest.

Re-measured with that correction, over the same 398 records: 44 of the 306
bound records still classify differently, and all 44 are `no_verdict`
becoming `no_integration`, not `marching_stop`. Those are the 37 closure
rungs, the 4 `diffusion_check` cases and the 3 generations of
`molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH0.55` the first text lists: each of their
logs carries `-> stopped: "Do only PP" -- no time integration was requested`.

`termination_classification.sh` 14/14 and every other catalog fixture pass on
the corrected rule.
