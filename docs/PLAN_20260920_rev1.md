1. **The opening table.** It was labeled as READ from the indexes and the
   inventory, and no number in it came from either. Every value came from the
   live `run.log` of a 2026-09-20 run that the 30 minute wall ceiling killed
   and that published no state. Two of the three rows additionally mix two
   different solves of that log.
2. **The contradiction of section 2.** There was no contradiction to resolve.
   The cell 1 and cell 2 rows belong to the stored generations, written under

   `..._ghost_fixed_point_seed_reservoir_row_v3`, seeded from the x0.10 cases.
   No single operator ever reported both.
3. **"Nothing was driving the row" (section 14.2).** False. The default route
   is an alternation, and the fixed-wind relaxation drives exactly the rows
   that refuse.
4. **Stage C instruments.** Four of the six named keys cannot produce output

   merit.
5. **`EXHALE_KRYLOV_TOL_ABS` in the decision table.** It tightens the forcing
   term. It cannot stop a Krylov cycle above a noise floor.

   not follow.
7. **"Two decades" in M2**, and the sentence putting the M2 refusal at cell 1.
8. **"It never needed a solve"** about the newly certified molecular
   `kzz1e9/HeH2.13`. What the record supports is narrower: the re-evaluated
   state satisfies every active equation of the contract, under the wind gate
   the contract states.

## 5. The revised stages

Two groups, named on every run and never mixed in one report:

- **Observation**: the production route unchanged, only output added.
- **Numerical variant**: a named change of scaling, termination, projection,
  step control or route, run as a labeled comparison against the same
  checkpoint.

The first plan promised that stages A to E change nothing. That promise holds
only for the observation group, and section 1 says which keys leave it.

### 5.1 Stage A, repeatability

Use `EXHALE_RESID_DETERMINISM` (`EXHALE_main.f90:1378`). It already does what
the first plan proposed to build: it evaluates the control twice back to back,
runs three trial states, returns to the control, performs a deliberately
discarded evaluation, takes a Jacobian-vector probe, and evaluates the control
once more, reporting which entries of the full residual vector moved, with
cell and slot, against a floor taken from the control.

Additions rev1 requires:

1. Compare every component, not the row maxima. Report the number of differing
   entries, the largest absolute and scaled differences, and the cells.
2. Fix BLAS threading as well as OpenMP threading, and repeat at each thread
   count before comparing counts.
3. Do not call a difference between thread counts a reduction-order effect.
   Reduction order, shared mutable state, a race, initialization and a
   branch-sensitive chemical iteration all produce it and need different
   repairs.
4. Failure here is a reason to distrust derivative estimates. It is not proof
   that the physical model lacks a state function, and a discontinuous closure
   can still be a deterministic function of the state.

### 5.2 Stage B, the accuracy of the action

Unchanged in intent, corrected in method.

1. Sweep the probe length with `EXHALE_JV_PROBE_ARC` and, where the comparison
   calls for it, `EXHALE_JV_COLUMN_SCALE`. Both change the production action,
   so a sweep runs on a frozen state, never during a solve that is being
   judged.
2. Record for each direction: the requested `h`, the actual displacement after
   the bound treatment, `F(Y + h v) - F(Y)`, an independently measured
   evaluation uncertainty, and the forward and central differences in the same
   units.
3. Add the Taylor remainder test the review proposes:
   `E(alpha) = || Dr^-1 [F(Y + alpha s) - F(Y) - alpha J_ref s] ||`.
   A quadratic regime before the uncertainty floor is the evidence that the
   reference action is right; a linear contribution is the evidence that it is
   not. Measure the hydrodynamic, carrier and element blocks separately.
4. Measure additivity AND homogeneity, and compare their defects with the
   linear reduction actually being asked for. A nonzero additivity defect at
   finite step length is expected and does not prove that no Krylov method can
   converge. This replaces the first plan's categorical sentence.
5. A missing plateau has several possible causes: a crossed bound or branch, a
   badly scaled direction, different closures at the two endpoints, or too
   narrow a sweep. Report which were excluded.

### 5.3 Stage C, the linear model and its correctness, on the route P2 uses

The first plan's instrument list is replaced.

**Available without changing the route**: `EXHALE_LINEAR_ROWS`
(`steady_newton.f90:17424-17446`), which evaluates the action of the direction
actually returned, forms `r_lin = -F - Jv - idtau*dY`, and prints the
hydrodynamic rows entering the step and the linear residual left over each row
of `F`; and, after the step, the rows of the new state.

**Not available without changing the route**: the Ritz values, the band
difference, the Krylov size scan and the term-by-term binding row. Reaching
them needs `EXHALE_TRUST_REGION=1`, which replaces the pseudo-transient line
search by the scaled trust region and moves the merit onto the model row
scaling. If those quantities are wanted, they are a numerical variant with its
own control, not an observation of P2's solve.

Order of inference, replacing the first plan's shortcut:

1. Verify the action over a resolved interval (Stage B).
2. Measure the residual of the returned direction against that same frozen
   action, with the actual shift and scaling, and report the shift
   contribution separately.
3. Compare the model's prediction with the nonlinear change over progressively
   smaller feasible steps.
4. Only then classify: model inaccuracy, insufficient linear reduction,
   globalization failure, or a changed active branch.

Where a spectrum is reported, report the Ritz residuals and the scaling
convention with it, and state that the species and hydrodynamic compressions
are not Schur complements, so a well-conditioned block says nothing about the
coupled system.

### 5.4 Stage D, the ledger

As before, with the review's additions: name the outer composition pass and
the inner Newton iteration separately; carry the state identity, the active
unknowns, the unscaled row measures AND the tolerance-normalized distances,
the actual scaled displacement, the step length or trust radius, the predicted
and observed reduction under fixed weights, the reduced Krylov residual and
the independently evaluated residual of its returned step, bound and limiter
events with the projection displacement, and a termination status that
distinguishes an inner return, a completed outer route and an external
interruption. Note that `du` in the JFNK line is the flux spread, not the norm
of the state update.

Scheduling is fixed in the code and must be named rather than assumed:
the additivity hook fires at Newton iterations 1, 20 and the configured cap
(`steady_newton.f90:16918`), the jump scan at the first iteration (`:16923`),
and `elem_diag_here` at iterations 1, 2 and `elem_diag_third` (`:16911`).

### 5.5 Stage E, the control

Re-evaluate the x0.10 states with the same operator, binary and conventions as
the x0.01 test, and say so. A certified state may exit before any Krylov work,
so it is not a control for step acceptance or preconditioning; for those, use a
controlled feasible perturbation of the certified state, which supplies a
nonzero residual without pretending the root exercises a full solve. The
chemistry and the radiation differ with the XUV as well as the Mach number, so
nothing may be attributed to one variable.

## 7. Stage F, continuation

Unchanged in design, corrected in what it may conclude.

Descend in `log(XUV)` from the certified x0.10 by moderate increments, reduce
the increment where a correction fails, use a predictor from the previous
successful states, keep the base model, composition, grid policy and band
definition fixed, and archive the spectrum of every point. Record the branch
markers, the smallest successful increment, the residual structure and the
reason each correction ended. If a turning point is suspected, an augmented
continuation equation is the instrument, not a smaller step.

**Result language.** The outcome is "the lowest certified XUV on the tested
branch, with this grid, boundary law and numerical method". The first plan's
second outcome called a smoothly growing failing row a physical limit. It is
not: a fold, an unresolved branch, a discretization artifact or a numerical
failure produce the same picture. A statement about physical stationarity
needs a branch and stability investigation and a check of closure validity,
and time dependence can only be established by an accurately integrated
physical time calculation, never by a failing pseudo-time iteration.

## 8. M1, rewritten

### 8.1 What the default route actually does

INSPECTED, `src/EXHALE_main.f90`, the outer pass loop from `:7005`:

- `:7368-7373` when element diffusion is on, `relax_element_composition` runs
  at the face mass flux of the current state.
- `:7430-7438` when transported rows exist and the block is off, which is the
  default, `relax_photochemical_composition` runs with a movement bound.
- `:7408-7412` and `:7445-7449` refresh the species densities, the temperature
  from the pressure, and the ionization chemistry around those updates.
- `:7140-7168` certifies the refreshed state jointly.
- `:6977-6980` makes the pass cap larger than one exactly when one of those
  relaxations is active.

So the route is a block Gauss-Seidel alternation: the wind is solved with the
composition held, the composition is relaxed with the wind held, and the row
that refuses is the row the relaxation drives. The first plan's claim that
nothing drove it is withdrawn.

The correct statement of the four cases is therefore: `norm(R) = 1.4e-08` says
the hydrodynamic subsystem is within tolerance at the inspected state, and the
carrier or elemental row above tolerance says the ALTERNATION has not reached
a joint fixed point. The two `HeH0.083` cases are "hydrodynamically within
tolerance at the inspected state", not "solved".

### 8.2 The measurements, revised

1. **Is the alternation approaching a joint fixed point?** Record, pass by
   pass: the actual composition displacement AND the displacement the
   relaxation requested before its movement bound, the inner residual and the
   ending token of each relaxation, the joint residual after the refresh, and
   whether successive state vectors converge or cycle. A flat maximum is not
   proof of a non-joint fixed point: an inexact inner solve, a movement bound,
   rejected updates, a cycle, or a moving worst cell all produce it.
2. **Where the rows refuse.** The outer cells 499 and 500 of 500 are
   r = 27.6 and 28.1. The carrier's outer closure is explicit:
   `carrier_face_mass_fraction` continues the last physical value into the
   ghost faces (`diffusive_photochemistry.f90:4167-4169`) and
   `carrier_outflow_ghost` does the same for the state (`:4181-4188`), which
   the header justifies as an outflow face of a transported scalar whose
   hydrodynamic closure also holds no composition. Test it with a term-by-term
   budget, which needs NO new code: `EXHALE_CARRIER_ROW_TERMS=1` writes
   `output/carrier_row_terms.txt` with, per cell and per solved carrier, the
   advective and diffusive divergences, the four faces separately (both
   diffusive and both advective, each carrying its area and the cell volume),
   the face mass fluxes, chemical production, loss and its radiative part, the
   row, its floor and the physical scale the certification divides by, and for
   H2 the thirteen channels one by one.
   For a radius experiment, hold the inner mesh and extend the domain outward
   rather than rebuilding a different grid everywhere, and compare fluxes,
   inner profiles, integrated budgets and the radiation column, not the index
   of the worst cell. A residual that follows the boundary localizes the
   question. It does not by itself say which boundary law is right, and it
   never justifies dropping those rows from certification.
3. **The joint route, as a bounded named variant.** `Coupled carrier solve:
   True` changes the unknown set AND switches the globalization to the scaled
   trust region (`EXHALE_main.f90:7024` then `steady_newton.f90:16661`), so it
   is a two-factor change and needs its unchanged control recorded beside it.
   Run it on ONE promising case first, after its coupled action has passed
   Stage B, not on both `HeH0.083` states at once.
4. **`On stall` is not an available comparison.** See section 10.1: as written
   the handover cannot fire. Until that is repaired, its absence is not
   evidence about the coupled method.

### 8.3 The two `HeH0.55` models

Their energy rows stand at 1.000 at cell 246 (r = 1.322) and 4.634E-01 at cell
257 (r = 1.390), just above the wind gate at r = 1.20, where H2 is still
dissociating. A row measure of one means the residual equals the row's largest
term.

The first plan proposed the H3+ out-of-domain counts as the lead. **That lead
is withdrawn**: the counters are cumulative call counts with no production
reset, a cell can increment several categories, rejected trials increment
them, and the certified molecular `kzz1e9/HeH2.13` carries 1320 activations
with 219 above the fit temperature range while the refusing states carry 334,
428 and 526 (READ). The count cannot rank failure.

What replaces it: at the failing cells record `T`, `n(H2)`, `n(H3+)`, the H3+
energy term, every competing energy term, which fit or interpolation branch
was taken, and the directional derivatives. Ask whether the state is outside a
validated domain and whether a join or a clamp actually affects the attempted
correction. Co-location is not causation. The converse also holds: an
expression without physical support must be repaired or restricted even where
its numerical contribution is small.

### 9.2 What the conversion promises, and what it does not

INSPECTED, `src/modules/init/molecular_seed_from_atomic_state.f90`:

- Two thermodynamic invariants, `:638-643`. `invariant=p` is the default
  (`:169`, `:256-273`, `EXHALE_MOLECULAR_SEED_INVARIANT`): keep rho, v and p,
  and let T follow from the new particle count. `invariant=T` keeps T and
  reconstructs p.
- It DOES check budgets, `:610-633`: the H nuclei, the He nuclei and the mass
  are compared before and after, a negative H I or H2 fraction stops the run,
  and a violation of the closure tolerance stops the run with the message that
  the transfer did not conserve what 2 H to H2 conserves.
- It DOES close the converted energy with the production equation of state,
  `:651-657`, and records the round-trip pressure difference.
- The H2 fraction of the seed is chosen by `EXHALE_MOLECULAR_SEED_X2`:
  absent means `handoff` (the default, the base value extended over the
  column), `local` takes the smaller of the thermochemical fit and
  `2 n_root / n_H` from `carrier_h2_chemical_root` after one ionization sweep
  whose result is then discarded, and a real in [0,1] states the fraction.

Therefore: neither invariant claims to preserve the source model's chemical or
energy equilibrium, and a certified ATOMIC state is not a certified MOLECULAR
state. Comparing the two residuals changes both the state and the operator, so
a larger residual after conversion is not by itself a conversion defect. The
first plan's sentence that the seed is the origin of everything is withdrawn.

### 9.3 The revised M2 work

1. **Test the promised invariants first**, on the converted state: the
   invariant displacement it records, composition admissibility, nucleus,
   charge and mass conservation, the equation-of-state round trip, the level
   and reservoir metadata, and the base closure the target operator
   reconstructs. A failed invariant is a conversion defect; a correctly
   converted but off-equilibrium state is an initialization problem. They lead
   to different work.
2. **Decompose the target molecular residual into its physical terms** at the
   refusing cells, in particular cell 2, where the mass row stands.
3. **Compare the existing seed modes** (`handoff`, `local`, a stated
   fraction) and say which produced each historical seed, before proposing
   anything new. If a chemistry relaxation at fixed hydrodynamics is proposed,
   state its thermodynamic invariant: fixed conserved energy, fixed pressure
   and fixed temperature are three different experiments once the heat
   capacity changes.
4. Refusing the metal-free scalar restart for a metal-bearing photochemical
   column is correct behavior, not an obstacle. Any conservative remapping
   would be a new, named experiment that inherits no certificate.

M2's read-only audit does not depend on M1 and can run beside it.

## 10. Defects found during the verification

These are code defects, not investigation steps. They are recorded here with
their evidence and a proposed repair; none has been applied.

### 10.1 The `On stall` handover cannot fire

INSPECTED, `src/EXHALE_main.f90`, all inside one iteration of the pass loop
that opens at `:7005`:

- `:7323` is the only assignment of `outer_ending = outer_no_progress`.
- `:7355` resets `carrier_outcome = carrier_relax_nothing_to_advance`
  unconditionally, AFTER `:7323`.
- `:7356` enters the update block only when `outer_ending .eq. outer_running`,
  so on the pass where the stall is declared no relaxation runs and
  `carrier_outcome` is not written again.
- `:7876-7885` requires both `outer_ending .eq. outer_no_progress` and
  `carrier_outcome .eq. carrier_relax_movement_bound`.
- A pass that ends `outer_no_progress` returns before any later pass.

The two conjuncts cannot hold together, so the branch is unreachable as
written. Proposed repair: test the ending of the LAST relaxation that actually
ran, held in a saved copy, rather than the current pass's reset value.
`n_bound_endings` (`:7455-7457`) already carries that history correctly.

### 10.2 Valid endpoints are counted as outside the H3+ domain

INSPECTED, `src/modules/lower_atmosphere/h3p_cooling.f90:180-183` and
`:245-248`: `.not. (tt .gt. T_fit_lo)` with `T_fit_lo = 30`, and
`.not. (tt .gt. sT(1))` with `sT(1) = 300`. At exactly 30 K and exactly 300 K
the fit and the table row are on their valid endpoint, and both are counted as
outside; the upper tests are strict, so the asymmetry is a NaN-safety idiom
rather than a physical statement. The review's own probe reproduces both
counts. Proposed repair: separate the NaN guard from interval membership,
count only strict excursions, and classify nonfinite inputs separately.

### 10.3 Evaluation history is reported as final-state validity

INSPECTED: the counters increment once per call with no production reset (the
only `h3p_reset_domain_records` callers are in `src/tests/physics_probe/`),
and `certification.f90:2069-2076` sums them into the certificate of the final
state. A cell can increment several categories, and rejected trials increment
them. Proposed repair: keep the process history under a name that says so, and
compute a separate final-state domain map in one controlled evaluation, with
unique cells distinguished from evaluations and the analytic low-density limit
distinguished from an unsupported continuation.

### 10.4 An exact float equality in the coupled-block test

INSPECTED: the production carrier residual is formed by multiplying by
`tscale_code` (`steady_newton.f90:4373-4374`) and the diagnostic compares
`F0(i)/tscale_code .eq. cres_again(...)` (`:6839-6843`, `:6849-6853`), counted
by `src/tests/coupled_block_jacobian/run.sh:189-202`. A multiplication
followed by a division need not return the same double, MEASURED:
`(0.1*3)/3 .eq. 0.1` is false, difference 1.39e-17. Proposed repair: compare
before the conversion, or apply the identical conversion to both sides, or
state a rounding bound. Do not loosen the other derivative assertions on
account of this one.

## 11. Additions adopted from the review's section 16

1. **Closure bias, before trusting Stage B.** `eval_residual` either runs the
   eliminated chemistry and radiation update to a convergence test or for a
   prescribed number of sweeps, and the Newton loop forms its Jacobian base
   point with a FIXED count (`steady_newton.f90:4175-4183`, `:17037-17038`,
   `n_eq_sweeps_model = 1` at `:8246`). A bitwise replay therefore measures
   the same finite-iteration map, not the converged closure. At one frozen
   state, hold seed, scalings, reconstruction and branch, raise the sweep
   count in a doubling sequence, and repeat the probe ladder at each count.
   If the derivative stabilizes with step length but moves with sweep count,
   the inner map is the problem and no finite-difference rule repairs it.
   Report `|| Dr^-1 (F - F_jac) ||` and its row distribution at the actual
   iterate.
2. **An independent discrete conservation audit.** Reconstruct the
   finite-volume identity from the exported faces and sources rather than by
   summing the same residual twice, using the operator's own volumes, areas,
   units and signs. Report the signed defect, the sum of absolute local
   defects and the largest local defect, and repeat over subdomains ending at
   the failing cells. Weight species by nucleus counts for elemental budgets:
   H2 alone is not conserved through a dissociation front. State the energy
   convention actually implemented before adding any term to the budget.
3. **A hierarchy of smaller controlled problems** (a compatible hydrostatic
   state, an inert carrier in a prescribed wind, a local reacting parcel, then
   the coupled checkpoint), reusing the existing drivers
   `src/tests/carrier_outer_boundary`, `carrier_boundary_jacobian`,
   `carrier_returned_state_acceptance` and `h2_level_ladder` where they match,
   and never publishing an inert or hydrostatic test as a physical solution.
4. **Whether eliminating a chemical variable is justified.**
   `chemical_decay_against_the_transport_times`
   (`steady_newton.f90:9177`) is the starting point, read with its
   limitations: an undefined advective or diffusive time prints as zero
   (`:9240-9245`), and `measure_molecular_decay_rates`
   (`ionization_equilibrium.f90:4894-4898`) summarizes by `abs(wr(i))`, which
   discards the sign of the real part and skips exactly zero. Keep the signed
   real parts before drawing any conclusion about fast chemistry.
5. **A physics-based derivative and preconditioner**, only after the action
   and closure experiments give a resolved map:
   `dF/dY = F_Y - F_z C_z^-1 C_Y` for the eliminated closure `C(Y,z) = 0`, and
   a composition-block preconditioner approximating the feedback in
   `D - C A^-1 B`. Both are proposals; neither block is assembled today.
   Optical depth couples cells, so a local chemical derivative is only part of
   the action.
6. **Three claims kept apart**: that the state satisfies the implemented
   discrete equations, that those equations are applicable, and that the
   scientific quantities are resolved. A certificate addresses only the first,
   under its stated gate.

| phase | minimum useful work | gate before proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| A0 identity | the two tables of section 3, for every checkpoint the work will use; decide whether the three P2 cases must be re-run under the current binary and boundary | no row mixes a stored state with a log iterate |
| A repeatability | `EXHALE_RESID_DETERMINISM` on each checkpoint, full vector, fresh process, fixed BLAS and OpenMP threading | a reproducible operator, or a quantified and explained evaluation uncertainty |
| M1 local audit, M2 read-only audit | `EXHALE_CARRIER_ROW_TERMS=1` budgets at the outer cells; the conversion invariants of section 9.3 step 1 | know whether a movement bound, an inner accuracy, an outer closure or a domain problem is present |

| closure bias | the sweep-count experiment of section 11 item 1 | the derivative is stable in both step length and sweep count |
| C linear model | `EXHALE_LINEAR_ROWS` on the unchanged route; anything needing the trust region runs as a named variant with its control | action error separated from linear-solve failure |
| D, E | the ledger; a matched control with a nonzero residual | a mechanism, not a correlation |
| M1 coupled trial | ONE case, `Coupled carrier solve: True`, with its unchanged control | full refreshed certification, or a localized recorded failure |
| F continuation | separate authorization | report the lowest certified XUV on the branch, not nonexistence |

Predeclare limits as residual evaluations, Krylov products, inner iterations
and outer passes, not only as wall clock, and estimate them from one measured
frozen evaluation on the actual checkpoint. The first plan's "minutes"
estimates are withdrawn: they were not measured, and the eliminated chemistry
and the spectral diagnostics dominate the cost.

Treat "no diagnostic output", "timeout before the checkpoint" and
"measurement unresolved" as three distinct results. None of them is a pass.
