\begin{quote}\small\itshape
This memo is the complete record of two tightly linked developments in
\code{EXHALE}: (i) the discovery that the historical convergence
criterion ($du<10^{-3}$ on the mass-flux spread) can stop runs at
\emph{premature, non-steady} snapshots --- the WASP-121b reference used
throughout earlier work was such a snapshot, low by $\sim$0.08~dex in
$\dot M$ --- together with the new convergence diagnostics that fix this;
and (ii) the construction, debugging, and first \emph{converged} run of a
direct steady-state solver (pseudo-transient continuation + Jacobian-free
Newton--Krylov), which reaches residual levels unattainable by time
marching at $\sim$1/200 of the cost. Every code change is opt-in: with all
new options at their defaults, the marching code is byte-identical to the
pre-existing behavior (verified by the regression harness after every
increment). Statements of physical interpretation below are tentative
unless explicitly verified.
\end{quote}

\section{The convergence-criterion problem}\label{sec:conv}

\subsection{What $du$ measures, and what it misses}

ATES declares steady state when
\begin{equation}
du \;=\; \frac{\max_j |\rho v r^2|_j - \min_j |\rho v r^2|_j}
              {\min_j |\rho v r^2|_j}\Big|_{j\in[j_{\min},N]} \;<\; du_{\rm th},
\qquad du_{\rm th}=10^{-3},
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the \emph{spatial spread} of the mass flux over the wind window
($r\ge r_{\rm esc}$). Two blind spots were demonstrated this session:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $du$ cannot see a \emph{uniform level drift}: the whole $\rho v r^2$
  profile can rise or fall while staying flat.
\item $du$ is \emph{oscillatory} on WASP-121b: it dips below $10^{-3}$
  transiently long before the wind settles.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The premature-dip discovery (WASP-121b)}\label{sec:dip}

The single-thread regression golden (He\,$2^3$S + metals, two-stage
PLM$\to$WENO3, stopped by $du<10^{-3}$ at step 7{,}288) has raw
$\log_{10} 4\pi\rho v r^2(r_{\max}) = 13.631$. Suppressing the $du$ stop and
marching on, the flux level rises \emph{monotonically} (the windowed level
change \code{lev\_rel} falls $0.23\to2.7\times10^{-3}$) toward
$\log_{10}\dot M \simeq 13.71$:
at step 35{,}085 the state reaches 13.707 and is still creeping upward,
with $T_{\max}$ falling from 11{,}021\,K to 10{,}767\,K. Three independent
paths agree on the deeper state (long cold marching $\to$13.707; warm
restarts $\to$13.716; the naive local-$dt$ experiment $\to$13.716 in 1{,}702
steps). \textbf{The old golden was a premature $du$-dip snapshot, not the
steady state} --- approximately $0.08$~dex ($\sim$20\%) low in $\dot M$.

Two earlier conclusions were corrected as a result. First, naive per-cell
pseudo-time stepping (``\code{Time stepping: Local}'') had been judged
``answer-shifting''; in fact it reaches the \emph{correct deeper} state
$\sim$20$\times$ faster --- the judgment was an artifact of comparing
against the premature golden. (The LTS state does carry larger per-cell
hydro residuals, \S\ref{sec:resid}, so it is ``fast but needs polishing.'')
Second, the apparent ``path-dependent quasi-steady states'' largely
collapse to one true state plus premature snapshots along the way.

\subsection{New diagnostics and stops (all opt-in)}\label{sec:diag}

\paragraph{Level-stability gate (\code{Level tol:}).} A converged/stalled
stop additionally requires the mean $|\rho v r^2|$ over $[j_{\min},N]$ to
change by less than \code{lev\_th} (relative) across the last
$N_{\rm stall}$ steps (ring buffer in \code{EXHALE\_main}; the relative level
change is now the 4th column of the per-step stdout). This correctly
refuses the premature dip (the cold run marches past step 7{,}288), but a
practical tolerance cannot discriminate ultra-slow drifts; it is necessary,
not sufficient. Default off.

\paragraph{Steady-residual diagnostic (\code{ATES\_RESIDUAL=1}).}
The finite-volume steady residual
\begin{equation}
\Rres_j \;=\;
\begin{cases}
(\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{F})_j - S_j, & \text{mass, momentum},\\[2pt]
(\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{F}_E)_j - S_{E,j} - (\mathcal{H}_j-\mathcal{C}_j),
& \text{energy},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:resid}
\end{equation}
evaluated once on a loaded state (one \code{Reconstruct}+\code{RK\_rhs}
pass; heating/cooling from \code{ioniz\_eq}; reported as
$\max_j|\Rres|/\max_j|u|$ per component over the window). This is
\emph{independent} of $du$/$dtu$/stall and ranks candidate states:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
state & $R_{\rm mass}$ & $R_{\rm mom}$ & $R_{\rm energy}$\\
\midrule
golden 13.631 ($du<10^{-3}$ stop) & $9.9\times10^{-2}$ & $4.7\times10^{-1}$
 & $\mathbf{3.1\times10^{1}}$\\
warm 13.716 (local-$dt$ + cont.) & 2.4 & 2.8 & $7.4\times10^{-1}$\\
cold 13.707 (35k steps) & $\mathbf{2.1\times10^{-2}}$ &
 $\mathbf{1.6\times10^{-2}}$ & $\mathbf{1.5\times10^{-2}}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The premature golden has an energy residual of $\sim$30: heating and
cooling are grossly out of balance, which $du$ cannot detect. (The energy
value $1.5\times10^{-2}$ for the cold state corresponds to the consistent
ghost-handling order, \S\ref{sec:newtonres}.)

\paragraph{Residual-based stop (\code{Resid tol:}).} In stage 2 the run
converges on $\max_k \max_j|\Rres_{j,k}|/\max_j|u_k| <$ \code{resid\_th}
(evaluated every \code{N\_resid}=500 steps; cheap), \emph{replacing} the
$du$ test. Validation: a run with \code{resid\_th}$=10^{-2}$ reached
$du=8.6\times10^{-4}$ (below the old stop) at step 32k and correctly kept
marching because $R_{\max}$ was still $3.1\times10^{-2}$ and falling
monotonically. Default off.

\section{The steady-state solver}\label{sec:newton}

The marching cost to a genuinely converged wind ($>$35k steps for
WASP-121b; $>10^6$ steps for HD189733b without reaching it) motivates
solving the steady residual directly: $F(Y)=0$.

\subsection{Formulation}\label{sec:newtonres}

\textbf{Unknowns} $Y\in\mathbb{R}^{3N}$: the conserved hydro variables
$(\rho,\rho v,E)$ on the $N$ physical cells. Ghost cells are not unknowns;
\code{Apply\_BC} fills them from the interior on every residual evaluation
(lower boundary: pinned $\rho_{\rm bc}$, $p=1+dp_{\rm bc}$ and the one-way
velocity valve; upper: extrapolation). \textbf{Local elimination}: given
$Y$, the ionization equilibrium is re-solved (\code{ioniz\_eq}) and the
resulting heating/cooling enter the energy residual --- so $F$ is a
pure-hydro residual of size $3N$ (``method A'' of the SUNDIALS plan), and
the non-local radiation (column densities) is inside $F$. The residual
construction is shared with the diagnostics
(\code{steady\_residual.f90}: \code{assemble\_residual}); the vector form
lives in \code{steady\_newton.f90} (\code{pack\_U}/\code{unpack\_U}/
\code{eval\_residual}), validated to reproduce the \code{ATES\_RESIDUAL}
numbers to all digits. One ordering subtlety: \code{Apply\_BC} must run
\emph{before} the chemistry/heating step so that $F$ depends only on the
interior $Y$; the diagnostic hook was aligned to the same order.

\subsection{Jacobian and linear algebra}

\textbf{Banded approximation.} With WENO3's $\pm$2-cell stencil and 3
coupled variables per cell, $\partial F_i/\partial Y_j$ has half-bandwidths
$k_l=k_u=8$ in the flat ordering. A graph coloring with stride
$k_l{+}k_u{+}1=17$ recovers all band entries of a \emph{local} residual
from 17 finite-difference probes. The banded matrix is factored with
LAPACK \code{dgbtrf}/\code{dgbtrs} (\code{LDLIBS=-llapack}; ATLAS LAPACK
3.7.1 on this machine, link-tested). A unit test
(\code{ATES\_JAC\_TEST=1}) checks $J r$ against a directional finite
difference: relative mismatch $1.3\times10^{-4}$, i.e.\ the band layout is
correct (an error would be $O(1)$).

\textbf{JFNK.} Because the radiation makes $F$ weakly non-local, the
banded matrix is used as a \emph{preconditioner} while the true
Jacobian-vector product is matrix-free,
$Jv \simeq [F(Y+\varepsilon v)-F(Y)]/\varepsilon$
(\code{jv\_product}), inside a right-preconditioned GMRES($m$)
(\code{pgmres}, modified Gram--Schmidt + Givens; inexact-Newton forcing
$10^{-1}$). The outer loop is pseudo-transient continuation,
\begin{equation}
\Big(\tfrac{1}{\Delta\tau} I + J\Big)\,\delta Y = -F(Y),
\qquad \Delta\tau \leftarrow \Delta\tau\,
\max(\lambda,0.1)\,\frac{\|F\|_{\rm old}}{\|F\|_{\rm new}}
\quad(\text{SER ramp}),
\end{equation}
with a backtracking line search and positivity guards ($\rho,p>0$).

\subsection{The five necessary ingredients (each found by a stall)}
\label{sec:ingredients}

The path to convergence was a sequence of well-localized stalls; each fix
was verified by its own probe (warm start from the cold-35k state,
$\Delta\tau_0=1$ unless noted). Removing any one ingredient reproduces the
corresponding stall.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Smooth line-search merit.} The first driver used the
  component-wise max-relative residual as the merit; it is non-smooth and
  rejected every step ($\lambda\to10^{-6}$). Fix: merit $=\|F\|_2$ (later
  $\|D^{-1}F\|_2$). After the fix the banded-PTC driver descends, but only
  linearly ($\sim$2\%/iteration) --- and a $\Delta\tau_0=1$ probe showed a
  textbook Newton step (residual halved in one step) before stalling,
  proving the machinery and isolating the next blocker.
\item \textbf{Matrix-free $Jv$ (JFNK).} The banded Jacobian omits the
  non-local radiation response; with exact $Jv$ via GMRES the merit fell
  $4.5\times$ in two steps (vs.\ 2\%/step), $\Delta\tau$ grew. Stall again
  --- now localized (whole-domain worst-cell diagnostic) to the
  \emph{base} cells $j=1$--2 (an earlier ``sonic point at $r_{\rm esc}$''
  reading was an artifact of searching only $[j_{\min},N]$; the sonic
  point is actually at $r=1.61$ and $r_{\rm esc}$ enters no physics).
  Freezing base cells with anchor rows does \emph{not} help: the worst
  cell simply follows the slab edge and the anchor interface itself
  introduces a kink.
\item \textbf{Diagonal scaling.} The unknowns span $\sim$7 decades (base
  $\rho\sim1$ vs.\ wind $\sim10^{-7}$ in code units); FD steps with
  absolute floors give wind cells 15--60\% relative perturbations and
  corrupt exactly the columns where the residual lives after the first
  base-scale corrections. With
  $D_i=\max(|Y_i|,\,10^{-6}\max_j|Y^{(k)}|)$, the scaled system
  $D^{-1}(\tfrac{1}{\Delta\tau}I+J)D$, D-relative FD steps, and merit
  $\|D^{-1}F\|_2$: GMRES needs 2--6 vectors (was 9) and the merit falls
  $24\times$ before stalling --- at the base again.
\item \textbf{Smooth base valve.} The lower BC ghost velocity is
  $\max(v_1,0)$, and the steady state hugs the kink
  ($v_1\simeq+4.5\times10^{-3}c_s$): Newton trial steps crossing $v_1=0$
  are non-differentiable. Opt-in replacement (\code{Valve eps:}):
  $v_g=\tfrac12\big(v+\sqrt{v^2+\varepsilon_v^2}\big)$, smooth, $\to0$ for
  $v\ll-\varepsilon_v$, $\to v$ for $v\gg\varepsilon_v$ (an earlier
  variant with a $-\varepsilon_v/2$ shift leaked inflow and was fixed).
  With $\varepsilon_v=10^{-2}$ (too large, it perturbs the state) the
  solver descends $10\times$ \emph{through} the base --- differentiability
  is what Newton needed --- but $\varepsilon_v=10^{-4}$ still stalled at
  the base: the valve was not the only kink.
\item \textbf{Frozen WENO weights + non-monotone line search.} The ESWENO3
  smoothness factors $S_{0,1}=1+\tau^2/\beta_{0,1}$ are smooth but
  strongly nonlinear; freezing them at the outer iterate
  (\code{weno\_mode}: store on a mode-1 pass, reuse on mode-2 passes for
  all Jacobian probes, $Jv$ products, and line-search trials --- the
  standard lagged-weights remedy) removes their response from the inner
  problem. Independently, the monotone Armijo test fails once the required
  decrease $10^{-4}\lambda\,\|F\|$ falls below the iterative-chemistry
  noise floor of the residual; a \emph{non-monotone} (Grippo, memory 5)
  acceptance --- improve on the worst of the last five accepted merits ---
  is the standard remedy.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Result: first converged steady solve}\label{sec:converged}

With all five ingredients (warm start from the cold-35k state,
$\Delta\tau_0=1$, $\varepsilon_v=10^{-4}$):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
target & iterations & final $\|R\|$ \\
\midrule
\code{Resid tol} $=10^{-3}$ & \textbf{8} (all $\lambda=1$, GMRES 2--3) &
 $9.5\times10^{-4}$\\
\code{Resid tol} $=10^{-4}$ & 22 & $6.0\times10^{-5}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Each outer iteration costs $\sim$25 residual evaluations (17 for the
banded preconditioner + GMRES + line search), so the $10^{-3}$ solve costs
the equivalent of $\sim$200 marching steps --- versus $>$35{,}000 marching
steps that never reached this residual level. Convergence is linear, not
quadratic (the frozen weights are refreshed per outer iteration --- a
Picard--Newton hybrid --- and the chemistry noise floor remains); every
step is a full Newton step.

\textbf{Physical validation} (vs.\ the marching references):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
state & $\log_{10}\dot M_{\rm raw}$ & spread($r\!\ge\!1.5$) & $T_{\max}$\\
\midrule
\textbf{Newton (8 its)} & \textbf{13.7111} & $\mathbf{5.0\times10^{-4}}$ &
 10{,}747\,K\\
cold marching (35k steps) & 13.7066 (rising) & $1.2\times10^{-3}$ &
 10{,}767\,K\\
old golden (premature) & 13.6309 & $1.8\times10^{-3}$ & 11{,}021\,K\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The Newton solution sits on the marching asymptote ($\simeq$13.71), is the
flattest state produced to date, and agrees with the deep-marching
reference to 0.005~dex in $\dot M$ and 0.2\% in $T_{\max}$.

\section{Hardening results (2026-06-11, after first convergence)}
\label{sec:hardening}

\paragraph{HD189733b — 33 s vs.\ $>$3 h.} Warm-started from the deepest
marching state available (1.16M steps, $du=6.6\times10^{-3}$; starting
residual $(2.4,\,5.2,\,2.4)\times10^{-3}$), the JFNK solver converged to
$\|R\|=9.5\times10^{-4}$ in 129 iterations / \textbf{33 seconds wall time}.
The converged state has $\log_{10}\dot M_{\rm raw}=10.298$ vs.\ 10.218
(marching) and 10.223 (pristine \code{ATES-Code-main}) --- both still
drifting upward --- and is the flattest state produced
(spread $7.6\times10^{-3}$ vs.\ $1.25\times10^{-2}$). Tentatively, the true
HD189733b $\dot M$ is $\sim$20\% above the marching estimates, the same
premature-snapshot pattern quantified for WASP-121b. Tightening to
$10^{-4}$ grinds: the frozen-weight refresh jumps are large at the
breathing base and progress floors at $\|R\|\sim3.4\times10^{-4}$ ---
the practical floor for this planet with the current settings.

\paragraph{Valve-$\varepsilon$ insensitivity.} Two full Newton solves of
WASP-121b (tol $10^{-4}$) with $\varepsilon_v=10^{-4}$ and $10^{-5}$ give
$\log_{10}\dot M = 13.7110$ and $T_{\max}=10750.2$\,K \emph{identically} to
the digits quoted: the valve smoothing is a pure numerical regularization.

\paragraph{\code{Solver: Newton} (production wiring) and the switch
criterion.} The warm-up$\to$Newton hand-off was wired into the main loop.
Two failure modes were found and fixed along the way, both reiterating the
central lesson: (i) a $du$-based switch fails --- at $du<10^{-2}$ during the
early transient the residual is still $\|R\|\approx4.8$, far outside
Newton's basin; (ii) with the switch fixed, the marching's own $du<10^{-3}$
stop fired first, returning the premature state. The final design:
\emph{the switch and the warm-up termination are both residual-based}
(periodic monitor; switch at $\max\|R\|<5\times10^{-2}$, $du$-stops
suspended in this mode). End-to-end validation from a \emph{cold
isothermal IC} (WASP-121b, single input line \code{Solver: Newton}):
two-stage warm-up to step 27{,}000 ($\|R\|=4.85\times10^{-2}$) $\to$ JFNK
16 iterations $\to$ $\|R\|=5.4\times10^{-4}$ $\to$ standard outputs and
post-processing; final $\log_{10}\dot M_{\rm raw}=13.7111$, spread
$5.1\times10^{-4}$, $T_{\max}=10{,}748$\,K --- identical to the
warm-started reference. Wall time 16 min (4 threads), dominated by the
warm-up.

\paragraph{Transmission spectra of the Newton solutions.} \code{EXHALE\_transit.py}
was run on both converged solutions (after PP-only runs generated the
\code{*\_adv} profiles): He\,10830 / Ly$\alpha$ / H$\alpha$ / H$\beta$
spectra for both planets plus the metal lines for WASP-121b
(Mg\,II 2796: 3.7\%, Ca\,II K: 3.8\%, Na\,I D2: 1.7\% line-center).
Peak line-center absorptions: WASP-121b He 3.7\%, H$\alpha$ 3.7\%;
HD189733b He 11.9\%, Ly$\alpha$ 46\%, H$\alpha$ 1.8\% (consistent in
magnitude with the Jensen et al.\ 2012 H$\alpha$ detection). Figures and
numbers are collected in
\code{regression/steady\_solver\_results.ipynb}; usage is documented in
the updated user manual (\S\,TPM).

\paragraph{Failure containment (a genuine JFNK failure, and two fixes).}
Re-running the manual's four figure runs through the production pipeline
exposed a real failure mode on the spherical, metals-off tutorial planet
(a generic hot Jupiter, domain to $10\,R_p$). The solve nearly converged
($\|R\|=6.0\times10^{-3}$ at iteration~2), but the non-monotone reference
still held the huge \emph{initial} merit ($\|F_s\|_2\simeq10^4$), so a
blow-up step was ``accepted''; the search then stalled at
$\lambda\simeq10^{-6}$, accepting zero-length steps against the
now-flat reference for $\sim$470 iterations, and --- worst of all --- the
main loop accepted the final unconverged state ($\|R\|=0.42$) as
converged and wrote it out. Two fixes (both in the default-off Newton
path; legacy byte-identity re-verified): (i)~\emph{best-iterate tracking
and fail-fast} in \code{solve\_steady\_jfnk} --- the best residual state
is stored, and the solve aborts with \code{info=2} after 15 outer
iterations without a new best, returning the best iterate; (ii)~in
\code{EXHALE\_main}, \code{info}$\ne$0 is treated as a failure: the run
prints \code{JFNK failed}, keeps the best iterate, disables the Newton
mode, and \emph{resumes plain time-marching} with the $du$-based stops
re-armed, so an unconverged Newton state can never masquerade as the
final answer. Re-run with the fixes, the same configuration aborts after
15 stuck iterations, returns the best iterate
($\|R\|=5.97\times10^{-3}$), and the resumed marching stops one step
later ($du=3.4\times10^{-4}$, already below threshold): the run ends on
an honest, near-converged state instead of a diverged one. The three
other figure runs converged cleanly through the pipeline (tutorial
metals-on $\|R\|=5.6\times10^{-4}$, WASP-121b spherical Case~A
$4.1\times10^{-4}$, Roche Case~B $5.8\times10^{-4}$, all \code{info=0}).

\paragraph{Can JFNK start cold? (basin-boundary experiments,
2026-06-11).} Three probes on WASP-121b (all with the fail-fast guard, so
each failure is cheap and the run still finishes honestly via the
marching fallback):
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
start of JFNK & start $\|R\|$ & JFNK outcome & final answer \\
\midrule
cold isothermal IC      & $1.4\times10^{4}$ & 1 iter $\to$ 11, hard stall
  at the base & --- (abort) \\
step 6{,}500 (\code{R\_switch}=0.5) & 0.43 & $\to$ 0.245, stall &
  fallback $du$-stop, $\dot M$ 13.34 (premature) \\
step 12{,}500 (\code{R\_switch}=0.1) & 0.091 & first step \emph{worsens}
  (0.148), stall & fallback $du$-stop, $\dot M$ 13.38 (premature) \\
step 27{,}000 (default $5\times10^{-2}$) & 0.0485 & 16 iters $\to$
  $5.4\times10^{-4}$ & $\dot M$ 13.7111 \checkmark \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The Newton basin boundary on this planet therefore lies between
$\|R\|\simeq0.09$ and $0.049$ --- the default switch is calibrated
essentially at the edge, and raising it buys nothing: every stall pins to
the first base cells ($j=1$--$2$, momentum), where the valve/ionization
coupling makes the frozen-weight local model invalid far from the fixed
point. PTC's small-$\Delta\tau$ limit is just implicit time stepping at
$\sim$20--60$\times$ the cost per unit pseudo-time of an explicit RK
step, so a cold-start PTC could not beat the marching warm-up
economically even where it survives. Practical caveat: when a
\code{Solver: Newton} run prints \code{JFNK failed}, the fallback
finishes on the $du$ stop, which can be a premature state (13.34/13.38
vs.\ 13.71 here) --- re-check such runs with \code{ATES\_RESIDUAL=1}.
Probes preserved in \code{regression/\{jfnk\_cold, newton\_rsw05,
newton\_rsw01\}}.

\section{Remaining open items}\label{sec:next}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{HD189733b residual floor.} Progress floors at
  $\|R\|\sim3.4\times10^{-4}$ (frozen-weight refresh jumps at the
  breathing base); an under-relaxed weight refresh between outer
  iterations is the natural next step.
\item \textbf{Non-monotone reference poisoning.} The tutorial-planet
  failure above was \emph{contained}, not prevented: the 5-entry Grippo
  memory can retain a huge early merit and thereby accept a blow-up step.
  Capping the reference at a multiple of the current merit (or flushing
  the history after a large drop) should prevent it outright.
\item \textbf{Chemistry noise floor.} The non-monotone search works around
  the \code{ioniz\_eq} iteration noise; tightening the inner solve (or
  smoothing its switch points) would restore certifiable descent and
  possibly quadratic tails.
\item \textbf{Quasi-steady physics.} The deep states still show slow
  residual decay rather than an exact fixed point (base breathing);
  whether an exact steady state of the discrete scheme exists with the
  hard valve remains open --- the smooth-valve fixed point found here is
  the natural regularized answer.
\end{enumerate}
