\begin{quote}\small\itshape
This memo is the complete record of two tightly linked developments in
\code{EXHALE}: (i) the discovery that the historical convergence
criterion ($du<10^{-3}$ on the mass-flux spread) can stop runs at
\emph{premature, non-steady} snapshots (the WASP-121b reference used
throughout earlier work was such a snapshot, low by $\sim$0.08~dex in
$\dot M$) together with the new convergence diagnostics that fix this;
and (ii) the construction, debugging, and first \emph{converged} run of a
direct steady-state solver (pseudo-transient continuation + Jacobian-free
Newton--Krylov), which reaches residual levels unattainable by time
marching at $\sim$1/200 of the cost. Every code change is opt-in: with all
new options at their defaults, the marching code is byte-identical to the
pre-existing behavior (verified by the regression harness after every
increment). Statements of physical interpretation below are tentative
unless explicitly verified.

\medskip
\textbf{Note added 2026-08-10.} This is a work log of 2026-06-10/11 and is kept
as written. Three of the solver constants it records have since been replaced:
the diagonal scaling, the stagnation watchdog, and the warm-up hand-off
criterion. Section~\ref{sec:postscript} lists what changed and what it means for
the stall diagnoses recorded here, in short, the repeated finding that
``every stall pins to the base cells'' was an artifact of the old scaling and of
a worst-cell print that normalized momentum by the base cell's own $|\rho v|$.
\end{quote}

\section{The convergence-criterion problem}\label{sec:conv}

\subsection{What $du$ measures, and what it misses}

ATES declares steady state when
\begin{equation}
du \;=\; \frac{\max_j |\rho v r^2|_j - \min_j |\rho v r^2|_j}
              {\min_j |\rho v r^2|_j}\Big|_{j\in[j_{\min},N]} \;<\; du_{\rm th},
\qquad du_{\rm th}=10^{-3},
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the \emph{spatial spread} of the mass flux over the wind window
($r\ge r_{\rm esc}$). Two blind spots were demonstrated this session:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $du$ cannot see a \emph{uniform level drift}: the whole $\rho v r^2$
  profile can rise or fall while staying flat.
\item $du$ is \emph{oscillatory} on WASP-121b: it dips below $10^{-3}$
  transiently long before the wind settles.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The premature-dip discovery (WASP-121b)}\label{sec:dip}

The single-thread regression golden (He\,$2^3$S + metals, two-stage
PLM$\to$WENO3, stopped by $du<10^{-3}$ at step 7{,}288) has raw
$\log_{10} 4\pi\rho v r^2(r_{\max}) = 13.631$. Suppressing the $du$ stop and
marching on, the flux level rises \emph{monotonically} (the windowed level
change \code{lev\_rel} falls $0.23\to2.7\times10^{-3}$) toward
$\log_{10}\dot M \simeq 13.71$:
at step 35{,}085 the state reaches 13.707 and is still creeping upward,
with $T_{\max}$ falling from 11{,}021\,K to 10{,}767\,K. Three independent
paths agree on the deeper state (long cold marching $\to$13.707; warm
restarts $\to$13.716; the naive local-$dt$ experiment $\to$13.716 in 1{,}702
steps). \textbf{The old golden was a premature $du$-dip snapshot, not the
steady state}: approximately $0.08$~dex ($\sim$20\%) low in $\dot M$.

Two earlier conclusions were corrected as a result. First, naive cell-by-cell
pseudo-time stepping (``\code{Time stepping: Local}'') had been judged
``answer-shifting''; in fact it reaches the \emph{correct deeper} state
$\sim$20$\times$ faster: the judgment was an artifact of comparing
against the premature golden. (The LTS state does carry larger
hydro residuals in each cell, \S\ref{sec:diag}, so it is ``fast but needs polishing.'')
Second, the apparent ``path-dependent quasi-steady states'' largely
collapse to one true state plus premature snapshots along the way.

\subsection{New diagnostics and stops (all opt-in)}\label{sec:diag}

\paragraph{Level-stability gate (\code{Level tol:}).} A converged/stalled
stop additionally requires the mean $|\rho v r^2|$ over $[j_{\min},N]$ to
change by less than \code{lev\_th} (relative) across the last
$N_{\rm stall}$ steps (ring buffer in \code{EXHALE\_main}; the relative level
change is now the 4th column of the stdout at each step). This correctly
refuses the premature dip (the cold run marches past step 7{,}288), but a
practical tolerance cannot discriminate ultra-slow drifts; it is necessary,
not sufficient. Default off.

\paragraph{Steady-residual diagnostic (\code{EXHALE\_RESIDUAL=1}).}
The finite-volume steady residual
\begin{equation}
\Rres_j \;=\;
\begin{cases}
(\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{F})_j - S_j, & \text{mass, momentum},\\[2pt]
(\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{F}_E)_j - S_{E,j} - (\mathcal{H}_j-\mathcal{C}_j),
& \text{energy},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:resid}
\end{equation}
evaluated once on a loaded state (one \code{Reconstruct}+\code{RK\_rhs}
pass; heating/cooling from \code{ioniz\_eq}; reported as
$\max_j|\Rres|/\max_j|u|$ per component over the window). This is
\emph{independent} of $du$/$dtu$/stall and ranks candidate states:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
state & $R_{\rm mass}$ & $R_{\rm mom}$ & $R_{\rm energy}$\\
\midrule
golden 13.631 ($du<10^{-3}$ stop) & $9.9\times10^{-2}$ & $4.7\times10^{-1}$
 & $\mathbf{3.1\times10^{1}}$\\
warm 13.716 (local-$dt$ + cont.) & 2.4 & 2.8 & $7.4\times10^{-1}$\\
cold 13.707 (35k steps) & $\mathbf{2.1\times10^{-2}}$ &
 $\mathbf{1.6\times10^{-2}}$ & $\mathbf{1.5\times10^{-2}}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The premature golden has an energy residual of $\sim$30: heating and
cooling are grossly out of balance, which $du$ cannot detect. (The energy
value $1.5\times10^{-2}$ for the cold state corresponds to the consistent
ghost-handling order, \S\ref{sec:newtonres}.)

\paragraph{Residual-based stop (\code{Resid tol:}).} In stage 2 the run
converges on $\max_k \max_j|\Rres_{j,k}|/\max_j|u_k| <$ \code{resid\_th}
(evaluated every \code{N\_resid}=500 steps; cheap), \emph{replacing} the
$du$ test. Validation: a run with \code{resid\_th}$=10^{-2}$ reached
$du=8.6\times10^{-4}$ (below the old stop) at step 32k and correctly kept
marching because $R_{\max}$ was still $3.1\times10^{-2}$ and falling
monotonically. Default off.

\section{The steady-state solver}\label{sec:newton}

The marching cost to a genuinely converged wind ($>$35k steps for
WASP-121b; $>10^6$ steps for HD189733b without reaching it) motivates
solving the steady residual directly: $F(Y)=0$.

\subsection{Formulation}\label{sec:newtonres}

\textbf{Unknowns} $Y\in\mathbb{R}^{3N}$: the conserved hydro variables
$(\rho,\rho v,E)$ on the $N$ physical cells. Ghost cells are not unknowns;
\code{Apply\_BC} fills them from the interior on every residual evaluation
(lower boundary: pinned $\rho_{\rm bc}$, $p=1+dp_{\rm bc}$ and the one-way
velocity valve; upper: extrapolation). \textbf{Local elimination}: given
$Y$, the ionization equilibrium is re-solved (\code{ioniz\_eq}) and the
resulting heating/cooling enter the energy residual, so $F$ is a
pure-hydro residual of size $3N$ (``method A'' of the SUNDIALS plan), and
the non-local radiation (column densities) is inside $F$. The residual
construction is shared with the diagnostics
(\code{steady\_residual.f90}: \code{assemble\_residual}); the vector form
lives in \code{steady\_newton.f90} (\code{pack\_U}/\code{unpack\_U}/
\code{eval\_residual}), validated to reproduce the \code{EXHALE\_RESIDUAL}
numbers to all digits. One ordering subtlety: \code{Apply\_BC} must run
\emph{before} the chemistry/heating step so that $F$ depends only on the
interior $Y$; the diagnostic hook was aligned to the same order.

\subsection{Jacobian and linear algebra}

\textbf{Banded approximation.} With WENO3's $\pm$2-cell stencil and 3
coupled variables per cell, $\partial F_i/\partial Y_j$ has half-bandwidths
$k_l=k_u=8$ in the flat ordering. A graph coloring with stride
$k_l{+}k_u{+}1=17$ recovers all band entries of a \emph{local} residual
from 17 finite-difference probes. The banded matrix is factored with
LAPACK \code{dgbtrf}/\code{dgbtrs} (\code{LDLIBS=-llapack}; ATLAS LAPACK
3.7.1 on this machine, link-tested). A unit test
(\code{EXHALE\_JAC\_TEST=1}) checks $J r$ against a directional finite
difference: relative mismatch $1.3\times10^{-4}$, i.e.\ the band layout is
correct (an error would be $O(1)$).

\textbf{JFNK.} Because the radiation makes $F$ weakly non-local, the
banded matrix is used as a \emph{preconditioner} while the true
Jacobian-vector product is matrix-free,
$Jv \simeq [F(Y+\varepsilon v)-F(Y)]/\varepsilon$
(\code{jv\_product}), inside a right-preconditioned GMRES($m$)
(\code{pgmres}, modified Gram--Schmidt + Givens; inexact-Newton forcing
$10^{-1}$). The outer loop is pseudo-transient continuation,
\begin{equation}
\Big(\tfrac{1}{\Delta\tau} I + J\Big)\,\delta Y = -F(Y),
\qquad \Delta\tau \leftarrow \Delta\tau\,
\max(\lambda,0.1)\,\frac{\|F\|_{\rm old}}{\|F\|_{\rm new}}
\quad(\text{SER ramp}),
\end{equation}
with a backtracking line search and positivity guards ($\rho,p>0$).

\subsection{The five necessary ingredients (each found by a stall)}
\label{sec:ingredients}

The path to convergence was a sequence of well-localized stalls; each fix
was verified by its own probe (warm start from the cold-35k state,
$\Delta\tau_0=1$ unless noted). Removing any one ingredient reproduces the
corresponding stall.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Smooth line-search merit.} The first driver used the
  component-wise max-relative residual as the merit; it is non-smooth and
  rejected every step ($\lambda\to10^{-6}$). Fix: merit $=\|F\|_2$ (later
  $\|D^{-1}F\|_2$). After the fix the banded-PTC driver descends, but only
  linearly ($\sim$2\%/iteration), and a $\Delta\tau_0=1$ probe showed a
  textbook Newton step (residual halved in one step) before stalling,
  proving the approach and isolating the next blocker.
\item \textbf{Matrix-free $Jv$ (JFNK).} The banded Jacobian omits the
  non-local radiation response; with exact $Jv$ via GMRES the merit fell
  $4.5\times$ in two steps (vs.\ 2\%/step), $\Delta\tau$ grew. Stall again:
  now localized (whole-domain worst-cell diagnostic) to the
  \emph{base} cells $j=1$--2 (an earlier ``sonic point at $r_{\rm esc}$''
  reading was an artifact of searching only $[j_{\min},N]$; the sonic
  point is actually at $r=1.61$ and $r_{\rm esc}$ enters no physics).
  Freezing base cells with anchor rows does \emph{not} help: the worst
  cell simply follows the slab edge and the anchor interface itself
  introduces a kink.
\item \textbf{Diagonal scaling.} The unknowns span $\sim$7 decades (base
  $\rho\sim1$ vs.\ wind $\sim10^{-7}$ in code units); FD steps with
  absolute floors give wind cells 15--60\% relative perturbations and
  corrupt exactly the columns where the residual lives after the first
  base-scale corrections. With
  $D_i=\max(|Y_i|,\,10^{-6}\max_j|Y^{(k)}|)$ \emph{(the 2026-06 form;
  replaced 2026-08-10, \S\ref{sec:postscript})}, the scaled system
  $D^{-1}(\tfrac{1}{\Delta\tau}I+J)D$, D-relative FD steps, and merit
  $\|D^{-1}F\|_2$: GMRES needs 2--6 vectors (was 9) and the merit falls
  $24\times$ before stalling, at the base again.
\item \textbf{Smooth base valve.} The lower BC ghost velocity is
  $\max(v_1,0)$, and the steady state hugs the kink
  ($v_1\simeq+4.5\times10^{-3}c_s$): Newton trial steps crossing $v_1=0$
  are non-differentiable. Opt-in replacement (\code{Valve eps:}):
  $v_g=\tfrac12\big(v+\sqrt{v^2+\varepsilon_v^2}\big)$, smooth, $\to0$ for
  $v\ll-\varepsilon_v$, $\to v$ for $v\gg\varepsilon_v$ (an earlier
  variant with a $-\varepsilon_v/2$ shift leaked inflow and was fixed).
  With $\varepsilon_v=10^{-2}$ (too large, it perturbs the state) the
  solver descends $10\times$ \emph{through} the base (differentiability
  is what Newton needed), but $\varepsilon_v=10^{-4}$ still stalled at
  the base: the valve was not the only kink.
\item \textbf{Frozen WENO weights + non-monotone line search.} The ESWENO3
  smoothness factors $S_{0,1}=1+\tau^2/\beta_{0,1}$ are smooth but
  strongly nonlinear; freezing them at the outer iterate
  (\code{weno\_mode}: store on a mode-1 pass, reuse on mode-2 passes for
  all Jacobian probes, $Jv$ products, and line-search trials, the
  standard lagged-weights remedy) removes their response from the inner
  problem. Independently, the monotone Armijo test fails once the required
  decrease $10^{-4}\lambda\,\|F\|$ falls below the iterative-chemistry
  noise floor of the residual; a \emph{non-monotone} (Grippo, memory 5)
  acceptance (improve on the worst of the last five accepted merits)
  is the standard remedy.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Result: first converged steady solve}\label{sec:converged}

With all five ingredients (warm start from the cold-35k state,
$\Delta\tau_0=1$, $\varepsilon_v=10^{-4}$):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
target & iterations & final $\|R\|$ \\
\midrule
\code{Resid tol} $=10^{-3}$ & \textbf{8} (all $\lambda=1$, GMRES 2--3) &
 $9.5\times10^{-4}$\\
\code{Resid tol} $=10^{-4}$ & 22 & $6.0\times10^{-5}$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Each outer iteration costs $\sim$25 residual evaluations (17 for the
banded preconditioner + GMRES + line search), so the $10^{-3}$ solve costs
the equivalent of $\sim$200 marching steps, versus $>$35{,}000 marching
steps that never reached this residual level. Convergence is linear, not
quadratic (the frozen weights are refreshed per outer iteration (a
Picard--Newton hybrid), and the chemistry noise floor remains); every
step is a full Newton step.

\textbf{Physical validation} (vs.\ the marching references):

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
state & $\log_{10}\dot M_{\rm raw}$ & spread($r\!\ge\!1.5$) & $T_{\max}$\\
\midrule
\textbf{Newton (8 its)} & \textbf{13.7111} & $\mathbf{5.0\times10^{-4}}$ &
 10{,}747\,K\\
cold marching (35k steps) & 13.7066 (rising) & $1.2\times10^{-3}$ &
 10{,}767\,K\\
old golden (premature) & 13.6309 & $1.8\times10^{-3}$ & 11{,}021\,K\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The Newton solution sits on the marching asymptote ($\simeq$13.71), is the
flattest state produced to date, and agrees with the deep-marching
reference to 0.005~dex in $\dot M$ and 0.2\% in $T_{\max}$.

\section{Hardening results (2026-06-11, after first convergence)}
\label{sec:hardening}

\paragraph{HD189733b: 33 s vs.\ $>$3 h.} Warm-started from the deepest
marching state available (1.16M steps, $du=6.6\times10^{-3}$; starting
residual $(2.4,\,5.2,\,2.4)\times10^{-3}$), the JFNK solver converged to
$\|R\|=9.5\times10^{-4}$ in 129 iterations / \textbf{33 seconds wall time}.
The converged state has $\log_{10}\dot M_{\rm raw}=10.298$ vs.\ 10.218
(marching) and 10.223 (pristine \code{ATES-Code-main}) (both still
drifting upward), and is the flattest state produced
(spread $7.6\times10^{-3}$ vs.\ $1.25\times10^{-2}$). Tentatively, the true
HD189733b $\dot M$ is $\sim$20\% above the marching estimates, the same
premature-snapshot pattern quantified for WASP-121b. Tightening to
$10^{-4}$ grinds: the frozen-weight refresh jumps are large at the
breathing base and progress floors at $\|R\|\sim3.4\times10^{-4}$,
the practical floor for this planet with the current settings.

\paragraph{Valve-$\varepsilon$ insensitivity.} Two full Newton solves of
WASP-121b (tol $10^{-4}$) with $\varepsilon_v=10^{-4}$ and $10^{-5}$ give
$\log_{10}\dot M = 13.7110$ and $T_{\max}=10750.2$\,K \emph{identically} to
the digits quoted: the valve smoothing is a pure numerical regularization.

\paragraph{\code{Solver: Newton} (production wiring) and the switch
criterion.} The warm-up$\to$Newton hand-off was wired into the main loop.
Two failure modes were found and fixed along the way, both reiterating the
central lesson: (i) a $du$-based switch fails: at $du<10^{-2}$ during the
early transient the residual is still $\|R\|\approx4.8$, far outside
Newton's basin; (ii) with the switch fixed, the marching's own $du<10^{-3}$
stop fired first, returning the premature state. The design adopted at
the time: \emph{the switch and the warm-up termination are both residual-based}
(periodic monitor; switch at $\max\|R\|<5\times10^{-2}$, $du$-stops
suspended in this mode). \emph{This was later reverted to a flux-keyed
hand-off; see \S\ref{sec:postscript}.} End-to-end validation from a \emph{cold
isothermal IC} (WASP-121b, single input line \code{Solver: Newton}):
two-stage warm-up to step 27{,}000 ($\|R\|=4.85\times10^{-2}$) $\to$ JFNK
16 iterations $\to$ $\|R\|=5.4\times10^{-4}$ $\to$ standard outputs and
post-processing; final $\log_{10}\dot M_{\rm raw}=13.7111$, spread
$5.1\times10^{-4}$, $T_{\max}=10{,}748$\,K, identical to the
warm-started reference. Wall time 16 min (4 threads), dominated by the
warm-up.

\paragraph{Transmission spectra of the Newton solutions.} \code{EXHALE\_transit.py}
was run on both converged solutions (after PP-only runs generated the
\code{*\_adv} profiles): He\,10830 / Ly$\alpha$ / H$\alpha$ / H$\beta$
spectra for both planets plus the metal lines for WASP-121b
(Mg\,II 2796: 3.7\%, Ca\,II K: 3.8\%, Na\,I D2: 1.7\% line-center).
Peak line-center absorptions: WASP-121b He 3.7\%, H$\alpha$ 3.7\%;
HD189733b He 11.9\%, Ly$\alpha$ 46\%, H$\alpha$ 1.8\% (consistent in
magnitude with the Jensen et al.\ 2012 H$\alpha$ detection). Figures and
numbers are collected in
\code{backup/regression/steady\_solver\_results.ipynb}; usage is documented in
the updated user manual (\S\,\code{EXHALE\_transit.py}).

\paragraph{Failure containment (a genuine JFNK failure, and two fixes).}
Re-running the manual's four figure runs through the production pipeline
exposed a real failure mode on the spherical, metals-off tutorial planet
(a generic hot Jupiter, domain to $10\,R_p$). The solve nearly converged
($\|R\|=6.0\times10^{-3}$ at iteration~2), but the non-monotone reference
still held the huge \emph{initial} merit ($\|F_s\|_2\simeq10^4$), so a
blow-up step was ``accepted''; the search then stalled at
$\lambda\simeq10^{-6}$, accepting zero-length steps against the
now-flat reference for $\sim$470 iterations, and (worst of all) the
main loop accepted the final unconverged state ($\|R\|=0.42$) as
converged and wrote it out. Two fixes (both in the default-off Newton
path; legacy byte-identity re-verified): (i)~\emph{best-iterate tracking
and fail-fast} in \code{solve\_steady\_jfnk}: the best residual state
is stored, and the solve aborts with \code{info=2} after 15 outer
iterations without a new best, returning the best iterate \emph{(this rule was
replaced 2026-08-10, \S\ref{sec:postscript})}; (ii)~in
\code{EXHALE\_main}, \code{info}$\ne$0 is treated as a failure: the run
prints \code{JFNK failed}, keeps the best iterate, disables the Newton
mode, and \emph{resumes plain time-marching} with the $du$-based stops
re-armed, so an unconverged Newton state can never masquerade as the
final answer. Re-run with the fixes, the same configuration aborts after
15 stuck iterations, returns the best iterate
($\|R\|=5.97\times10^{-3}$), and the resumed marching stops one step
later ($du=3.4\times10^{-4}$, already below threshold): the run ends on
an honest, near-converged state instead of a diverged one. The three
other figure runs converged cleanly through the pipeline (tutorial
metals-on $\|R\|=5.6\times10^{-4}$, WASP-121b spherical Case~A
$4.1\times10^{-4}$, Roche Case~B $5.8\times10^{-4}$, all \code{info=0}).

\section{Postscript: what changed after this log (2026-08-10/11)}
\label{sec:postscript}
%====================================================================

Four things recorded above are no longer what the code does.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The hand-off is flux-keyed, not residual-keyed.} The
  \code{Solver: Newton} key hands off when the flux metric $du$ (the radial
  spread of $\rho v r^2$) falls below \code{newton\_du\_switch}, default
  $10^{-2}$, settable as \code{Solver: Newton <du\_switch>}. The
  \code{R\_switch} keys of the tables above no longer exist.

\item \textbf{The diagonal scaling is local and parameter-free.}
  \code{build\_scaling} was replaced by \code{cell\_state\_scales}, which builds
  every scale from the cell's own state: $\rho$, $\rho(|v|+c_s)$, $E$. The old
  momentum floor $10^{-6}\max_j|\rho v|$ was set by the base cell, which holds
  the global maximum of $|\rho v|$ while carrying no wind, so cells where the
  flow reverses were divided by a number unrelated to their own state.

\item \textbf{The stagnation watchdog counts failed line searches.} It aborts
  after 12 consecutive outer iterations in which the line search found no
  acceptable step, instead of after 15 iterations without a new best $\|R\|$.
  Every solve measured needs 27--38 iterations before it first improves on the
  warm-start $\|R\|$, so the old rule fired during the opening pseudo-transient
  in normal operation.

\item \textbf{The line search is tested against the true residual}
  (2026-08-11). The trial states of the backtracking search in
  \code{solve\_steady\_jfnk} are evaluated with \code{weno\_mode = 0}, i.e.\
  with the WENO smoothness weights recomputed at the trial state, so what
  decides acceptance is the residual the solve is driving to zero rather than
  the frozen-weight model of the current outer iteration. On the
  HD~189733\,b hand-off state the two measures differ by a median factor 2.2
  at $\lambda=1$, and 7 of the 14 steps the frozen test accepted \emph{raised}
  the true residual, by up to $9\times$. This is the change that attacks the
  HD~189733\,b residual floor of item~1 of \S\ref{sec:next} from the other
  side. Numbers: \code{docs/newton\_scaling\_and\_base\_wall.md} \S10.
\end{enumerate}

The consequence for this memo is that its recurring diagnosis (that every
stall lands on the base cells $j=1$--2, momentum) does not survive
re-measurement. The base momentum row is satisfiable: its residual is the
remainder of a four-order cancellation of gravity against the pressure gradient,
$6.5\times10^{-5}$ of the gravity term, and a 2.9\,ppm change of the ghost
pressure drives it to zero. What actually dominated the line-search merit were
the momentum rows around the stagnation point, mis-scaled by the floor above.
Configurations that this memo records as stalling now converge with
\code{info}$=0$. Full account and numbers:
\code{docs/newton\_scaling\_and\_base\_wall.md}.

\section{The stationary solve as it stands (2026-09)}
\label{sec:stage2}
%====================================================================

The solver of \S\ref{sec:newton} solved three unknowns per cell. Since
2026-09 it also solves the SPECIES ROWS: one row per transported element and
one per molecular carrier, four to eleven unknowns per cell depending on the
configuration. Everything in this section is that route; the three-unknown
route is untouched by construction and every configuration that used it,
\code{wasp\_full\_newton} included, is bitwise across the whole series.

This section replaces the description of the outer loop in
\S\ref{sec:newtonres}--\S\ref{sec:ingredients} for the species-row route: the
backtracking line search of the pseudo-transient continuation is no longer
what decides the step there. It is a trust region.

\subsection{The merit reads every row on the scale it will be judged by}
\label{sec:meritscale}

There is one denominator for a species row,
\code{merit\_row\_scale\_from\_certification}, and the acceptance test, the
merit of the line search and the certification all divide by it. The
hydrodynamic rows keep \code{residual\_\allowbreak row\_scale(k,j,u)}, the element rows
take their certification scale, the carrier rows take the term sum the
certification divides by, and the base cells of element rows keep the
unknown's own scale, since the base is the Dirichlet reservoir and carries no
equation. MEASURED on both species-row fixtures: every element row's largest
scaled merit row now EQUALS its certification measure at the same cell, where
before the two stood a factor $2.5$ apart at the first iteration and $112$
apart at the sixtieth.

The measured consequence was not free, and is recorded rather than hidden:
reconciling the hydrodynamic rows onto the same footing improved the carrier
fixture by $2.6\times$ and made the atomic element fixture stop solving
altogether, because the certification row scales span $11.4$ decades against
$8.3$ before. The cause turned out not to be the spread but one exactly empty
Jacobian column (\S\ref{sec:precond}).

\subsection{The trust region, and the linear solve under it}
\label{sec:tr}

The step is a dogleg inside a trust region on the scaled coordinates, and
every part of it now has a name that is printed.

\paragraph{Named linear outcomes.} \code{pgmres} returns one of seven:

\begin{center}\small
\code{gm\_tolerance\_reached}, \code{gm\_subspace\_exhausted},
\code{gm\_no\_direction\_sampled}, \code{gm\_jacobian\_action\_unusable},\\
\code{gm\_preconditioner\_failed}, \code{gm\_reduced\_system\_singular},
\code{gm\_nonfinite}.
\end{center}

\noindent A zero right-hand side returns before any
factorization; the rotations use \code{hypot}; the rank of the reduced system
is tested before the triangular division and the leading nonsingular block is
the one solved; the LAPACK status and finiteness are checked after every
preconditioner apply; at a breakdown the true scaled residual is verified
with one extra product and the cycle exits.

\paragraph{The radius is initialized from a direction that exists, and has a
floor.} \code{initial\_trust\_region\_radius} takes its first radius from a
finite Krylov direction with the active bounds held, and the floor
\code{tr\_delta\_min\_dimensionless}\,$\sqrt{n_{\rm eq}}$ $= 10^{-6}\sqrt{n_{\rm eq}}$ in scaled coordinates stops
a radius of exactly zero from being an absorbing state; the ceiling
\code{tr\_dmax} follows the radius and cannot pass
$10^{3}\sqrt{n_{\rm eq}}$. ``No feasible descent'' is \code{info = 3}.

This is the fix of a real failure, and its mechanism is worth recording
because it looked like a solver defect and was a registration defect: on the
atomic element reload $1500$ unknowns sat on a bound and the first cycle took
zero products, the radius became zero and stayed there. The $1500$ were the
three elements \code{metals.inp} does not carry (Si, K, S over $500$ cells),
registered with an identity row and sitting exactly at zero, so the
fraction-to-the-boundary rule annihilated any direction with a component
there. Absent elements, those with reservoir abundance $\le 0$, are no longer
registered at all. MEASURED: unknowns on a bound $1500 \rightarrow 0$, first
radius $0 \rightarrow 1.909\times10^{-2}$, first step accepted at ratio
$0.995$.

\paragraph{The length along the direction used, and the leg that is dropped.}
The step length along the approximate-gradient leg is
$\tau = (r \cdot Ag)/\|Ag\|^{2}$, the interior minimizer along the leg the
step is actually taken on. That leg is DROPPED from the step and from its
model image on two tests: by length, when it is shorter than
\code{cauchy\_leg\_min\_fraction} $= 10^{-3}$ of the radius, and by image
share, when it supplies more than \code{cauchy\_leg\_image\_share\_max} $=
0.5$ of the model image while being less than
\code{cauchy\_leg\_step\_share\_max} $= 0.1$ of the step. It is dropped and
the dogleg rebuilt at no extra product, not rescaled: rescaling would either
shorten $A s_U$ without $s_U$, which is not a step of the operator, or move
the step off the dogleg path. The reason for the second test is that the
first one is a proxy: a leg that is $0.7\%$ of the step can supply ALL of its
image. Honest impact: on the fixtures as they now run, the length test
reaches every image-dominant leg first ($117$ length drops, $0$ image drops),
so the image rule fires zero times and all three runs are bitwise. The rule
is the right criterion nonetheless and is kept; it is not claimed to improve
either fixture.

\paragraph{The ray test has a measured floor and a sound-ratio rule.} The
decision is \code{ray\_slope\_verdict}: the model slope along the step is
compared with the slope measured along the ray, and the floor under the
comparison is
$\max(\code{eq\_sweep\_reltol}\,|f_2|,\ \code{ray\_floor\_factor}\times
\text{measured reproducibility})$, the reproducibility measured once per
outer iteration by two evaluations of the merit at one state (cost: one
residual evaluation per outer iteration, $+1.5\%$ on the carrier fixture).
MEASURED, the measurement refutes what the diagnostic before it claimed: the
reproducibility is $1.8\times10^{-15}$ at a merit of $14.95$ and $0$ to
$4\times10^{-19}$ at $2.7\times10^{-5}$, four to seven decades below the
assumed part, which therefore governs everywhere, and the refused trials'
$|f_{2}^{+} - f_{2}^{-}|$ stood two to three decades above the floor, so they
were real slope differences and not a probe at its cancellation floor.
Within \code{ratio\_sound\_band} $= 10^{-2}$ of the acceptance ratio the ray
verdict is formed and printed but not acted on.

\paragraph{The face-write hold.} A trial whose species unknown would be
written onto a face of its box is a move of the state that no model of the
step contains, and those were exactly the trials whose predicted decrease
stopped scaling with the step below $\|s\| \sim 10^{-7}$ (MEASURED: $11$ of
$11$ such trials refused at a fixed ascent of $3\times10^{-3}$ over five
decades of step; $10$ of $10$ others accepted at ratio $1.000$).
\code{hold\_the\_step\_where\_it\_leaves\_the\_species\_box} zeroes the step
at such an unknown, so the trial stays where the operator can be sampled.
\code{probe\_length\_of\_the\_jacobian\_action} is now the one definition of
the probe arc. Atomic fixture at a cap of $300$, before $\rightarrow$ after:
$227 \rightarrow 113$ iterations, $170/55 \rightarrow 91/19$
accepted/refused, negative ratios $49 \rightarrow 14$, best $\|R\|$
$2.353\times10^{-2} \rightarrow 1.333\times10^{-2}$; the period-two cycle is
gone. The carrier fixture and \code{wasp\_full\_newton} are bitwise (the
branch never fires there).

\subsection{The element constraint rows}
\label{sec:elemrows}

The coordinate box that used to limit the species step had one side per
carrier, which is the wrong feasible set by stoichiometry: two carriers of
one element each spend the whole budget, and a cell at both of its
hydrogen-bearing ceilings breaches the shared hydrogen budget by exactly
$1.0$ of the available hydrogen. The step is now limited by the shared rows
themselves,
$2n({\rm H_2}) + n({\rm H^+}) + n({\rm OH}) + 2n({\rm H_2O}) \le
n_{\rm H}^{\rm avail}$ and its oxygen and carbon counterparts, each of the
old corners being implied by the row, with a nonlinear feasibility screen by
magnitude at every candidate and a restoration phase for an infeasible start.
The derivatives of the constraint come from the same map that states it
(\code{element\_constraint\_derivatives} of
\code{lower\_atmosphere/element\_inventory.f90}).

The write-back of each iterate returns the element totals it was handed. That
is where the improvement came from, and it is worth separating from the
constraint itself: on the carrier fixture the He/H nucleus ratio stays at
$1.39\times10^{-11}$ of its reservoir at every one of $68$ iterates where the
old text drifted to $2.016\times10^{-1}$ over $79$; best $\|R\|$
$0.1513 \rightarrow 0.0899$ at $14\%$ fewer residual evaluations.
\textbf{Stated, not claimed}: on both fixtures the shared hydrogen row is
never within a probe step of being tight, so the active-set PROJECTION onto
the constraint hyperplane, the part the route is named for, fired on ZERO
iterations of either run; its correctness rests on the suite rows, and the
measured gain comes from the write-back, from the budget leaving the
coordinate box, and from the shared step limit. Switches:
\code{EXHALE\_ELEMENT\_CONSTRAINT\_ROWS=0} restores the five corners,
\code{EXHALE\_ELEMENT\_WRITE\_BACK} the write-back.

\subsection{The column floor and the equilibrated preconditioner}
\label{sec:precond}

The collapse of \S\ref{sec:meritscale} was one exactly empty Jacobian column.
At iteration $11$ of the atomic fixture the carbon unknown of cell $500$ had
been driven to zero, its column scale sat on the absolute floor $10^{-20}$,
the probe step $\sqrt{\epsilon}\times10^{-20}$ underflowed the residual
difference, the column was exactly zero and the banded preconditioner
singular (\code{dgbcon} $3.98\times10^{-27}$). Two repairs, with the model
left untouched to the last bit ($r_0 = F/D_{\rm row}$ and $A$ unchanged,
verified by the scaled column norms):

\begin{enumerate}
\item \code{element\_unknown\_column\_scale}
  $= \max(|x|,\,10^{-4}|{\rm reservoir}|,\,10^{-20})$, so the column stays
  resolvable while the probe step stands far above
  $\epsilon\,X_{\rm reservoir}$;
\item \code{equilibrate\_the\_preconditioner}, a two-sided equilibration in
  powers of two of the band that is FACTORIZED only,
  \code{banded\_preconditioner\_solve} applying $C\,\code{dgbtrs}(Rv)$.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent Attribution on the first solve (accepted/refused; empty columns at
iteration 11; \code{dgbcon} there): entry $14/16$, $1$,
$4\times10^{-27}$; floor alone $69/16$, $0$, $4.9\times10^{-11}$; both
$114/36$, $0$, $1.1\times10^{-9}$. With a cap of $150$ the atomic fixture goes
from an abort at $\|R\| = 1.777$ to $3.800\times10^{-2}$ still descending, and
the carrier fixture from $0.446$ to $0.1513$. The model's OWN column
equilibration (\code{EXHALE\_COLUMN\_EQUIL=1}) was built, measured and NOT
adopted: it moves the trust-region coordinates, the radius runs to the cap
and trials ascend by $4\times10^{4}$ times the predicted decrease.

Two further pieces of the same work: the first-cell rows are declared once
(\code{species\_row\_base\_equation}: an element row is the Dirichlet
reservoir, a carrier row the control-volume balance of the operator's own
$j = 1$ branch), and the tolerance the composition elimination is asked for
is separated from what the outer residual may keep
(\code{eq\_sweep\_reltol} = \code{outer\_residual\_closure\_target} divided by
the measured amplification of the closure map at the base).

\subsection{What limits the species-row solves today}
\label{sec:limit}

Not the step control, and this is measured rather than argued. At the
binding iterate of the atomic fixture: zero unknowns held, zero refused
samples, zero empty columns, \code{dgbcon} $1.25\times10^{-6}$, step ratio
$0.9998$, merit reproducibility exactly zero, the trust region inert. What
fails is the linear model. The cycle spends $40$ of $40$ products on a
direction whose true relative residual is $0.58$ against the $0.1$ asked
(and $0.9917$ at the later binding iterate of the same run), with the Arnoldi
image $21\%$ away from the operator, and $\|s_N\| = 8.6\times10^{2}$ against a
radius $5.9\times10^{-2}$, so the region uses $7\times10^{-5}$ of an
inaccurate direction.

That $21\%$ gap is NOT a loss of orthogonality and not a stale cache. Shown
positively: every one of the $40$ Arnoldi columns matches its own product to
$1$--$3\times10^{-16}$, two products of one direction agree exactly, the
basis's orthogonality loss is $4.4\times10^{-13}$, and yet the action of
$V_1 + V_2$ differs from the sum of the two actions by $1.1\times10^{-4}$. It
is the NONLINEARITY of the finite-difference action across directions, a
curvature term over the fixed probe arc $\sqrt{\epsilon}(1 + \|Y\|)$. (One
later measurement does not fit a gap that is a function of the assembled
length alone, and re-measuring it at three iterates is an open item.)

Doubling the products halves the linear residual and makes the outer solve
WORSE on both fixtures, so more of the same direction is not the answer
either. The untried freedom is the COLUMN preconditioner; the row scaling is
fixed by \S\ref{sec:meritscale}.

\paragraph{And the element reload is chaotic at the level of the last bit.} The atomic
element fixture is not a reproducible quantity. A control that multiplies
three ghost quantities by $1 + 2\times10^{-16}$, changing no equation and
posing no boundary, moves $\|R\|$ at iteration $40$ from
$3.212\times10^{-2}$ to $1.125$; a physically correct refill of the same
ghosts moves it to $1.199$; the three builds part at outer $10$--$11$ and from
each other. The amplifier is the Krylov leg at its one-digit tolerance: the
relative residual reached differs by $0.3\%$ at outer $10$, the promise by
$0.4\%$, then $2.7\times$ at outer $11$, and at outer $12$ the three take
different branches of the acceptance ($0.874$ accepted, $-1.98$ refused,
$85$ accepted).

The consequence is a rule for anyone measuring this route: ``it reproduces
the previous build'' rejects correct changes as readily as wrong ones, and
$\|R\|$ at iteration $N$ is not what such a comparison can carry. What it can
carry is named outcomes: which row binds, the refusal counts by name, the
flux spread, the mass closure.

\subsection{The residual contract}
\label{sec:rescontract}

Three statements the residual is now held to, and the state of each.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Determinism.} $F(Y)$ is a function of $Y$, so an evaluation at
  a point the caller discards (a Jacobian column, a Krylov product, a point
  on a trust-region ray, a self-test) must put back what it left in the
  modules it called. The replay registers the species rows and returns
  $0$ of $1500 / 2000 / 2500 / 5500$ entries differing (3, 4, 5 and 11
  unknowns per cell) after three trials, a discarded trust-region trial and
  a \code{jv\_product} probe with the active bounds held. GREEN on four
  configurations. The one part not reachable from the driver is a rejected
  macrostep. This closed a hole in the old determinism suite, which replayed
  the THREE-unknown residual in every configuration and so compared $0$ of
  $1500$ entries.
\item \textbf{Conditional closure convergence.} FAILS, by design, and the
  failure is the standing measurement. The judged row maximum moves, per
  unit relative change of the eliminated partition's seed, by $4.2\times10^{2}$
  carrier tolerances on the carrier fixture and $3.1\times10^{1}$ element
  tolerances on the atomic one; a seed above $2.4\times10^{-3}$ (carrier) or
  $3.3\times10^{-2}$ (atomic) moves the judged row past its tolerance. Two

\item \textbf{Branch discovery.} With H$_2$ transported the two-root H$_2$

\subsection{What a certified state is now, and what refuses one}
\label{sec:certtol}

The stationary certification no longer has one tolerance for the species
rows. \code{cert\_tol\_element\_wind} $=$ \code{cert\_tol\_carrier\_wind}
$= 10^{-5}$, GATING for cells at $r \ge$ \code{cert\_regime\_wind\_r}
$= 1.20$; below that the accessors return
\code{cert\_tol\_reported\_only} $= \code{huge}$, so the row is measured,
printed and does not gate. One reduction,
\code{certification\_species\_row\_gate}, decides a species row over the
gated cells, and a column with no gated cell is refused, never reported as a
satisfied row. The report prints \code{gated at r >= 1.2 against 1e-5:
<measure> at cell <j>}, and a certified state with species rows carries the
one line saying it is certified IN THE WIND with the rows below it reported;
the state file says \code{cert\_reason=certified\_in\_wind}. The
hydrodynamic tolerances ($3\times10^{-12}$, $10^{-8}$, $10^{-6}$) and the
level and closure tolerances did not move. There is no input key: the two
radii are properties of the flow and are constants of
\code{certification.f90}.

The band $1.10 \le r < 1.20$ is reported and does not gate. The decision was
silent on it, and the band holds the candidates' binding cell at $r = 1.153$,
where the discretization error of the discrete equation itself is
$4.6\times10^{-4}$: a $10^{-5}$ gate there would ask the residual to fall
$1.5$ decades below the error of its own equation, and no anchor for the band
exists.

\paragraph{What anchors $10^{-5}$, and what refuses $10^{-8}$.} Five
measurements were taken on the two candidate states. The arithmetic does not
refuse $10^{-8}$: two evaluations of one state differ by exactly zero, the
closure-seed floor is $5\times10^{-19}$, five to eleven decades below it.
What refuses it is the DISCRETIZATION and the ELEMENT FLUX CONSERVATION.
The derivative of the residual has a clean V at the base ($7.6\times10^{-8}$)
but a PLATEAU at $6.6\times10^{-5}$ over four decades of probe length at the
binding cell, so the smallest row a Newton step resolves there is
$3.9\times10^{-8}$ ($6.5\times10^{-9}$ in the wind). A manufactured relaxed
column converges at order $1.589$ and extrapolates to $1.52\times10^{-6}$ at
the production spacing. Grid refinement of the candidate at $r = 1.1515$ takes
the He/H row from $5.93\times10^{-4}$ ($N = 500$) to $2.47\times10^{-4}$
($N = 1000$) with a remap control of $1.5\times10^{-5}$: about $78\%$ of the
row is discretization. And the element flux spread is $9.79$ in the layer
against $2.7\times10^{-2}$ in the wind. The gate is set a decade above the
manufactured column's discretization at the production spacing, and the
layer's rows do not gate until the layer's flux conservation is repaired.
Full account: \code{docs/certification\_tolerance\_anchoring\_20260910.md}.

\textbf{No candidate is certified.} The atomic reload binds on the He/H row of
a WIND cell at $2.896\times10^{-4}$ against $10^{-5}$; the carrier reload on
the H$_2$ row at $7.284\times10^{-2}$. \code{wasp\_full\_newton}, which
carries no species row, is certified as before.
