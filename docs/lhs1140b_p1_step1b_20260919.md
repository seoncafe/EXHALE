## 1. The answer

**Neither state is a root the other is separated from. There is one root, and
the composition sweep does not reach it: the ghost it returns keeps a measured
fraction of the composition it was seeded with.** Three measurements decide it.

1. **The cell solver is not stopping short for want of iterations.** Over six
   decades of its stopping accuracy (`EXHALE_IEQ_TOL` from the default
   1.49e-8 down to 1e-14, section 3) the two states do not approach each
   other by anything. Each stays where it is to 1e-15 relative while they
   remain 4.7e-3 (H II, He II) to 5.5e-2 (H2+) apart, and the cell-1 mass row
   reads 8.33528e-08 and 2.92293e-09 at every rung. At 1e-14 MINPACK's own
   `hybrd1` answers info = 3, "xtol too small, no further improvement
   possible", on 225 of 508 cells, so at the default the returned iterate is
   already pinned to better than 1e-13 relative. Iteration count is not what
   separates the two.

2. **Seeded with the refused ghost, in the refused ghost's own physical
   column, the solve leaves it** (section 6.3). The generation's `_IC` pair
   with its two lower ghost rows replaced by the refused ghost's own rows,
   seeded from them, returns a ghost 5.48e-2 away in H2+ and lands on the
   certified state, and the evaluation then CERTIFIES (row 1.73321e-09). A
   root is a fixed point of the solve that finds it. The refused state is not
   one.

3. **The map from seed to returned ghost has a measured gain that is not
   zero.** Today's rule and the state file's ghost rows are two seeds 9.68e-1
   apart in H II, 9.61e-1 in H3+ and 9.98e-1 in HeH+ (section 6.1); the two
   ghosts they return are 4.74e-3 apart in H II. The perturbed seed of
   experiment 2 stands 9.83e-1 from the state file's seed in H I, and its
   returned ghost 5.30e-5 from the state file seed's. A converged root does
   not remember its starting point; this one keeps 0.5 per cent of a trace-ion
   seed difference and 5e-3 per cent of a neutral-hydrogen one.

The two readings section 1b of the plan left open were "two roots" against
"one root the solve stops short of". It is the second, with the addition that
the shortfall is not an accuracy of the kind a tolerance names: the network
residual at both states is 3e-26, the imposed partition closes to 0.0 at both,
`hybrd1` returns info = 1 at both, and the cell is accepted at both. **Every
measure the sweep applies is satisfied at a state 5 per cent away from the one
it would return from another seed.** The acceptance test is blind in the
direction the base mass row reads.

Two further results of this item bear on the fix.

- **The interior refresh contributes nothing to the base row** (experiment 5,
  section 5). Holding every cell above the two lower ghosts at the composition
  the sweep was given, a declared diagnostic policy added here and default off,
  leaves the cell-1 row at 8.33528e-08 and 2.92293e-09 to every printed digit,
  with both face fluxes unmoved. The whole effect sits in the two ghost rows.
- **The reservoir-row seed is what selects the refused state, and it selects
  it from either branch** (experiment 6, section 6). Reloaded under today's
  rule, both the refused and the certified state return the same refused state
  to 15 digits; reloaded with the ghost rows the state itself carries, both
  return a certified state, at a fixed point after two reloads. The state file
  ghost rows are therefore a fixed point of the reload and the reservoir row is
  not.

## 4. Experiment 4, the temperature closure

The leg D5b-2 left open is the pair the sweep does not iterate: the composition
sets the particle count, the particle count and the boundary's pressure set the
temperature the balance is solved at, and the balance sets the composition
(READ, `lhs1140b_stationary_D5b2_20260918.md` section 12). The evaluate route
solves at the temperature of the loaded state and never returns to it.

The loop this item used is the one already in the code:
`Restart intent: stationary equilibrate` iterates
`f_sp <- ioniz_eq(rho, T)` with `T = p/(n_tot + n_e)` at the file's pressure
until the particle count stops moving, then rebuilds the conserved state from

is a declared diagnostic policy and not the state the file carries: the

## 7. What P1 step 2 must be

**An accuracy contract on the boundary's composition, with the accuracy stated
in the quantities the base row reads, and with the seed named as part of the
boundary model.** Not a branch statement: there is one root, and section 6.3
shows the sweep leaves the other state as soon as it is started from it. Not a
reformulated base row either, at least not first: the row's conditioning is
real and measured (a factor 4e8 to 1e9 floors per relative unit of the ghost
composition, READ, `lhs1140b_stationary_D5b2_20260918.md` section 12; and here
a factor 28.5 in the row for 4.7e-3 in the trace ions), but a row that hid this
difference would hide a ghost that is 5 per cent wrong in its ionization, which
is a property of the boundary and not of the row.

Three things the contract has to carry, each of them a measurement of this
item.

1. **The returned ghost must be a fixed point of the map that produced it, to a
   stated accuracy, and the accuracy must be measured in the trace ions and not
   only in the network residual.** Every test the sweep applies today is passed
   by a state 5 per cent from the one it would return from another seed:
   reaction residual 3e-26, partition residual 0.0, `hybrd1` info = 1,
   acceptance class "converged root". The natural test is the one section 6.2
   measures: re-enter the solve at the state just returned and require it to
   come back within the contract. On this state one such pass costs one sweep
   and moves the ghost by 1.5e-05; two passes reach 4e-13.

2. **The seed is part of the boundary model and must be stated there.** The
   reservoir row with the first physical cell's partition is not a neutral
   starting point: it is 30 times the ghost's own H II (section 6.1), and it is
   the whole of the difference between a state that certifies and the same
   state refused (section 5 removes the interior, section 3 removes the
   stopping accuracy). Whatever the contract chooses, the choice belongs in
   `base_boundary`'s stated model and in its report, not in `load_IC`'s
   handling of rows it drops.

3. **The temperature leg belongs in the contract, and it is cheap here.**
   Closing the pair brings the two seeds to 3e-06 of each other (section 4) and
   costs one sweep on this state. The plan already puts the temperature leg in
   P1 step 2; this item measures what it is worth.

A base row that does not rest on the cancellation remains worth having, and
section 5 is the measurement that says where it would and would not help: the
interior refresh is not in the row, so a reformulation would be about the
ghost's own state and the face areas, not about the interior.
