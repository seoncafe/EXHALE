## 1. Judgment of the review

The review is sound. Every source claim it makes was checked here and holds
(section 2). Where it corrects the advisor's decision document it is right in
each case: the "no He 2^3S bias" claim for the WASP-121 b composite was not
established and is withdrawn; "every input of record is a power law" was
false (`WASP-52b/input.inp` loads a table); D0 section 7.1 contradicted its
own sections 7.2 and 7.3 (the stationary system has an H2 row when the
carrier transport is coupled). Those three statements are corrected in the
documents themselves. The review's separation between an audit of the
implementation and the approved target system is the correct reading of rev
3 section 4.2 and is adopted.

Two points where the plan below is stated more narrowly than the review:

- The review's whole-step controller (save the complete state, run every
  operator as a trial, accept or restore) is the target structure. It is not
  the first increment, because the state it must checkpoint (species
  fractions, carrier backgrounds, excited populations, thermodynamic caches,
  photon columns, rate caches, accumulated counters) is not yet enumerated
  anywhere; enumerating it is the D0 rollback contract (rev 3 section 4.2
  item 8). A carrier-only rejection with a fixed background is therefore the
  first increment, labeled as partial, and the whole-step controller follows
  once the checkpoint list is written and tested.
- On the relaxation amnesty, the review's rejection of a residual cap "of
  order one" as an acceptance tolerance is accepted. The plan does not remove
  class 4 in one step: it first makes a class-4 state incapable of passing
  any final acceptance (the stationary gate and the written state), then
  replaces the amnesty by an explicit initialization/continuation procedure
  that is not part of the physical time integration. Until then the amnesty
  stays reported and counted, never certified.

## 3. Execution order from here

Roles as before: the advisor writes briefs and verifies diffs and tests;
workers implement; the user decides where a row says so. Every code step
carries its test (RED before, GREEN after), its movement measured on the
matrix, and a golden refresh only at the end of a series. Labels: **A**
acceptance contracts, **B** target system and its first increments, **C**
spectra and configurations, **D** validation runs.

### Step A. Repair the acceptance contracts (before any Phase 3 physics)

A1. **Status and classification contracts** (workers, disjoint files).
- Carrier stop: convergence is judged on every unconstrained row, not on
  the worst cell; constraint activity is recorded separately and never
  certifies a failed solve. Test: a constructed column with a large
  constrained residual and a smaller but unacceptable unconstrained one
  must stop.
- Class 5: one meaning of the acceptance classes for every consumer
  (`steady_newton`, the streak logic, the reports); a class-5 root has the
  same validity as a class-1 root; a class-4 state cannot pass a final
  acceptance anywhere. Test: certified states through both solvers get the
  same verdict; a genuine class-4 state is refused by the final gate.
- Gate: byte-identical on the matrix (no default case has a failed carrier
  solve or a class-4 cell at its final state; if one does, that is a
  finding to report, not to hide).

A2. **Independent final gates** (advisor design, one worker).
- Before a state is declared stationary or written as a final result, the
  active equations are re-evaluated on that exact state: chemical root
  validity of every cell, the stationary residual of every transported
  species (H2, and H+ when transported) even when it is not a global Newton
  unknown, the energy balance, the element and charge invariants. The
  last backward-Euler residual is not a substitute for a steady equation.
- The marching caller's `.false., 0.0d0` carrier condition is replaced by
  the same evaluation.
- Gate: the matrix's converged cases pass; the fixed-step snapshots are
  reported as what they are (relaxation states) with the gate values
  printed, not certified.

A3. **Carrier rejection and retry, carrier-only** (decision 1, first
increment).
- On a failed status: restore the carrier state of the step, halve the
  carrier substep with the background held fixed, cover the original
  interval with successful substeps, bounded retries and a dt floor, then
  stop with the failure reason. Accepted-step statistics exclude rejected
  trials. Labeled partial recovery in the code comment and the log.
- Test: injected failure after mutating a trial state; complete
  restoration, correct elapsed time, agreement with an explicitly
  subdivided integration.

### Step B. The target governing system and its first increments (Phase 2 proper)

B1. **Target specification document** (advisor writes, user accepts; the
Phase 2 gate). Separate from the D0 audit. For each C-item of D0 a target
equation, an owner, an acceptance test. Contents required by rev 3 section
4.2, with the review's recommended answers adopted as the starting position:
- Energy variable: thermal plus kinetic remains the evolved variable, with
  an explicit formation/excitation ledger and a conservative coupled
  temperature-composition source solve; the temperature-preserving pressure
  reset (C1) is removed as an independent update.
- Mass: chemistry preserves the density supplied to the local step;
  reconstruction is a check, never a mass source in the stationary equations
  (C2).
- Independent species space and constraints (element totals, charge
  neutrality, mass), the constrained Jacobian on that space.
- Charged transport: a derived ambipolar closure with charge neutrality and
  a stated current condition; the common-velocity approximation only within
  a quantified regime.
- CO: ceiling-active atmospheres excluded from validated results until the
  destruction model is derived; the cumulative record is the observable.
- H3+ emission and the H2 internal-energy convention chosen from the
  published fit definitions, with limits stated.
- Oxygen: held outside the validated set until its energy ledger closes;
  atomic configurations are not blocked by it.
- `_adv` products: same source assembly as the main solution, or the
  unsupported product refused, never a silent mix (C8).
- Lyman-alpha: local energy ownership (rev 3 section 4.2 item 3); the
  excitation loss and the de-excitation return are derived together, not an
  escape-probability multiplier on the existing cooling (C7 rewritten to say
  so).
- Rollback contract: the enumerated checkpoint state for the whole-step
  controller.
- Verification matrix: closed reacting cell (thermal plus chemical energy
  conserved), two-cell radiation exchange, returned-temperature residual,
  species stationary residual, invariants.

B2. **Residual-controlled temperature solve** (first Phase 3 increment,
after B1 acceptance): bracketed or safeguarded iteration on the energy
equation, sources evaluated at the returned temperature, rejection of an
unacceptable update; a temperature floor only as budgeted external heating
or as a rejection. Replaces the two-iteration update of C3. Test: the
returned state's residual below tolerance in every cell; floor hits either
budgeted or absent.

B3. **Coupled species-energy source step** (rev 3 A4 + B3): one solve for
temperature and composition under the chosen energy constraint, the pressure
reset removed, chemistry mass-preserving; then the whole-step controller of
decision 1 with the B1 checkpoint list. The `output_state_consistency`
test turns GREEN here (one state per file). Movement on every case; goldens
refreshed once at the end of this series.

B4. **Stationary equations on the same terms** (rev 3 Phase 5): species
rows for every transported species, the same source assembly as the
marching step, the A2 gates as the acceptance; `Ionization transport` with
`Solver: Newton` becomes a supported configuration or is refused with the
reason stated.

### Step C. Spectra and configurations (independent of A and B, no reruns)

C1. **Loaded-field validation through the consumers** (worker): define
whether table values are point samples or bin averages; test a narrow line,
a sharp threshold, a coarse bin and a steep continuum through the production
photoionization, heating and Balmer consumers; require rate convergence with
resolution; compare band energy and photon number of the histogram against
the source table; include the 10 A upper cutoff in every integrated XUV
comparison. Coverage tests derived from the active threshold inventory
(2600 A metastable, 3647 A H(n=2) when armed), per configuration.

C2. **WASP-121 b candidate C** (worker): solar XUV below 1700 A with the
1.6e6 normalization, the scaled WASP-17 photosphere above, no arbitrary
renormalization of either component to force continuity, the join ratio
reported; header labels "provisional composite; proxy photosphere; absolute
XUV normalization as chosen, not as a Huang reproduction". The He 2^3S
photoionization rate of A, B and C computed through the production
integrand and reported side by side; the transit-depth consequence measured
only in Step D.

C3. **Configuration switches** (advisor, one per configuration): purpose,
reference model, SED file and checksum, coverage, units at the orbit,
distance and normalization assumptions, active absorbers and dilution
recorded in the folder README; previous outputs kept and labeled with their
input. HD 189733 b: Salz file as the Salz-comparison run, Bourrier file
(visit stated, or the B-E mean with variability as an input uncertainty) as
the observed-star model. WASP-121 b: C, labeled provisional. HD 209458 b:
the WHI 2008 file. Hot Uranus: the Koskinen stand-in, with the note that it
covers no H(n=2) band. `VULCAN/atm/stellar_flux/sflux-HD189_B2020.txt`
untouched.

### Step D. Validation runs (instruction-gated, after A, B1 and C3)

D3. Paper and poster figures, last and only on explicit instruction.
