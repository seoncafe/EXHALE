## 2. L15: localize the thread dependence along the failing trajectory

**Evidence.** L4h memo section 5.0: two 8-thread runs of the same
fixture part after about 21 iterations (mass rows 3.08e-2 and 1.20e-2 at
the end of pass 1); one thread bitwise. L15 memo: five repeated
`stationary evaluate` runs of ONE certified atomic state gave no
difference in the compared outputs at 1 and at 8 threads. That is an
observation about those outputs in those trials; it does not exclude a
schedule-sensitive race, the perturbed evaluations `F(U + eps v)` the
Jacobian action makes along a trajectory away from a certified state,
arrays not written, or values below the printed precision. The OpenMP
regions are all inside the residual evaluation (the ionization sweep,
`util_ion_eq`, the constrained chemical equilibrium,
`diffusive_photochemistry`, `RK_rhs`, `PLM_rec`); the Newton and Krylov
code holds none and its inner products are serial intrinsic sums.

**Experiment.**
1. Two 8-thread runs from the failing seed
   (`backup/regression/atomic_elem_newton/IC`, the fixture's `EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0`),
   same binary and BLAS setting (`pool = 1`), with a checksum of U, the
   composition, the scales, the active set and the pseudo-time shift
   written to a side file at each iteration, the instrumentation kept
   outside the numerical reduction order; find the first divergent
   iteration.
2. At the preceding checkpoint dump every mutable quantity the next
   evaluation reads: U, composition, active bounds, the scaling history,
   the cached radiation and chemistry state including thread-local
   caches, the preconditioner input and the shift, in binary. Where a
   cache cannot be serialized reliably, compare a fresh process against
   a restored in-process evaluation.
3. From that checkpoint repeat `F(U)` and the actual perturbed
   evaluations of the Jacobian action at 1 and 8 threads, restoring the
   state between evaluations; also change the evaluation order, to
   expose a dependence on history.
4. Compare, in order: the preconditioner input and output, each Jacobian
   action, the Arnoldi coefficients, the merit. Dump full basis vectors
   only around the first divergence.
5. One molecular configuration after the atomic cause is understood.

**Fix candidates, by what the localization shows.** A shared scratch
array written by more than one thread: give it an owner (threadprivate
or allocated inside the region); a barrier does not repair it. A
mutable cache read across threads: copy per evaluation. An OpenMP
reduction whose result depends on the thread count: a fixed partition
and reduction tree independent of the count, or a serialized sum.
Acceptance: two 8-thread runs of the fixture identical for 200
iterations; the 8-thread run agrees with the 1-thread run bitwise where
the reduction is made count-independent. The cost of the fix is
measured against a fresh timing of the sweep at 1, 8 and 16 threads on
this machine; the recorded 2.07x (memory record, 16 threads, workload
and hardware not recorded) is not an acceptance threshold. The catalog
reproducibility study then uses difficult saved iterates or controlled
admissible perturbations, not the certified end states. Tests: a new
`jfnk_thread_reproducibility` suite. Goldens: none. Effort: one to two
days. Decision needed: annotate the 8-thread REPRODUCE.md records with
"the solve path was not demonstrated repeatable at 8 threads before
L15" (recommended: yes, by `write_reproduce.py`), naming no mechanism.

## 3. L22: the composition movement bound, the progress measures and the relaxation contract

**Evidence.** L7e memo section 23: in the two refusing well-mixed states
every carrier relaxation of every pass ended on the movement bound
(5.0e-3 -> 2.5e-3 -> 1.25e-3 -> floor 1.0e-3). The three profiles are
kept apart: in `wellmixed/HeH0.083` the bound is attained at the H2
front (cell 231, 1.26 Rp) and the refusing cell is 500 (29 Rp, x2 =
7.8e-3); in `wellmixed/HeH0.55` the front again sets the bound and cell
306 (1.95 Rp, x2 = 2.7e-6) refuses; in `kzz1e9/HeH0.083` the far wind
itself (cell 466, 16.5 Rp) sets the bound. The R4 Jacobian corrections
are in and the cases still refuse. `wellmixed/HeH2.13` has no seed,
which is a different statement.

**What the defect is.** The maximum over cells and a bound in every cell
describe the same feasible set; the defect is one update multiplier and
one cumulative excursion budget applied to regions whose responses
differ by decades. The wind's response is not local (pressure coupling,
attenuation), and at fixed conserved thermal energy the pressure
`p = (gamma-1) e_th` does not depend on the particle count at fixed
gamma, while the caloric EOS moves gamma with the composition and the
temperature moves in any case (`pressure_and_temperature_at_fixed_conserved_state`).
So p, T, the sources and the coupled residual are monitored beside the
particle count.

**Step 1, the progress measures, named and decided.** What the code
measures now (verified): the carrier half of `comp_drift` is the
residual of the carrier operator on the returned composition
(`carrier_steady_residual`, `EXHALE_main.f90` lines 6762-6796), on a
scale the bound cannot touch; the element half is the distance to the
endpoint of the inner element relaxation, formed before `omega`, so
undamped, but the endpoint of a finite inner map that can exit on its
step budget rather than a solved fixed point. The work: (a) four
quantities distinguished in names and logs: the carrier residual, the
element inner-map distance, the accepted displacement, and the full
coupled residual; (b) the element measure decided, either the element
transport residual at the returned state (the counterpart of the
carrier's) or the inner-map distance with a fixed inner accuracy and
budget stated; the first is recommended, so both halves are residuals
of their own operators; (c) each half normalized on its own physical
scale before the maximum is taken, the scales independent of the
iteration length, and absolute terms logged beside them so a scale
movement is visible; (d) a missing background (`bg_ready` false) or a
nonfinite residual reported as unavailable and the pass judged
unavailable, not read through the displacement measure (the present
`crc_max .eq. crc_max` fall-through and the `carrier_close = carrier_drift`
default are the two places). Test rows in `carrier_retry` and the element
suite.

**Step 2, the causal comparisons.** On the frozen `wellmixed/HeH0.083`
and `kzz1e9/HeH0.083` states, single-threaded. The mask is not an
abundance threshold (one target state never has x2 below 1e-2, so the
complement can be empty) but the set of bound-controlling cells read
from the rejected trial (the cell that attains the bound and its
neighbors within the front's width), frozen for the comparison and
listed in the log. Three interval-selection sets are compared: the full
grid, the grid without the narrow mask, and a separate far-wind set. If
a set is empty the run stops with that as its outcome; no set is ever
excluded from the certification, only from the interval choice. The
question each answers: does the refusing cell descend when the
front's cells no longer choose the multiplier? If in neither case the
far cell descends, the obstruction is elsewhere (outer boundary carrier
treatment, the deferred reconstruction terms of the Jacobian) and no
interval design answers it.

**Step 3, the contract, chosen from step 2.** Cell-local intervals
already exist: `dt_code(j) = min(t_diff, t_adv)/t_scale` per cell
(`diffusive_photochemistry.f90` lines 5883-5890), multiplied by one
`grow`. The new work is the control of the multiplier and of the
acceptance, not the introduction of local intervals. Two algorithms are
kept apart. (i) Cell-local pseudo-time preconditioning of the UNCHANGED
residual: `M_j dU_j/dtau_j + R_j(U) = 0` with one common physical face
flux; positive `dtau_j` do not move the root; intermediate iterates need
not conserve an inventory; the requirements are admissibility and the
vanishing of the unmodified conservative residual. Tested first. Its
exit at an active cumulative bound is defined now: when a cell reaches
the excursion boundary referenced to `nsum0` at entry, no shorter
interval enlarges the feasible set, so the relaxation returns to the
hydro solve with that cell named, and a repeatedly shrinking interval
is logged as solver exhaustion, never as convergence; a row that stops
updating with a nonzero physical residual fails the pass. (ii)
Conservative physical subcycling: a common synchronization interval,
accumulated face transfers, integrated sources. Written only if (i)
fails AND a measured stiffness comparison shows it beats a coupled
carrier block or a better preconditioner; failure of (i) alone does not
justify it. (iii) The coupled block stays a candidate with the
statement that a linearized pressure derivative at fixed hydro is not
the wind response and that a coupled block still needs positivity,
simplex feasibility and a nonlinear acceptance.

**Acceptance.** For (i): the unmodified carrier and coupled residuals
inside their tolerances, the element and charge identities at
convergence, admissibility, and independence of the certified state
from the iteration controls; no inventory constraint on intermediate
pseudo-time updates. For (ii): the inventory equation with cell volumes,
accumulated boundary transfers and integrated sources over the common
interval, with a physically scaled absolute tolerance (a face flux
mismatch relative to the wind-window mass flux of the state, and a
stated zero case). Comparing two uses of one shared flux object is not
a test. Agreement with the one-bound route on `kzz1e9/HeH2.13` (pass 11)
is a comparison under the same operator, not an oracle. Tests:
`carrier_retry`, `carrier_returned_state_acceptance`. Goldens:
`mol_carrier`. Effort: two to three days. Decision needed: open it
(recommended).

## 5. L25: the low-Mach base of the seven low-XUV atomic cases

**Evidence.** L17 memo: at 0.02 of the fiducial XUV the base is subsonic
to Mach 1e-8 with alternating-sign velocities in cells 1-6, the HLLC
star flux selected by the sign of S* (`Num_Fluxes.f90` lines 178-187)
puts the base cells on a kink; a C1 blend of the two star fluxes
covered 409 of 500 cells and moved Mdot by 2 percent, reverted. After
L21, `x0.10/HeH9.7` and photochem x0.15/0.20/0.25 hold the cell-1 mass
row near ||R|| 1.5 at dtau0 = 1 and 1e8 alike (`lhs1140b_rerun_20260915.md`).

**Premises.** A base at Mach 1e-8 with a large density carries a finite
mass flux; its momentum balance is hydrostatic at leading order only;
the finite-flow equations and the full mass, momentum and energy
certification stay. The transonic solution is selected by the reservoir
entropy, the heating and the subsonic structure together, so a
base-local change may move Mdot; the acceptance is the physics judgment
first and the movement reported after it. A well-balanced, algebraically
equivalent evaluation of the FULL momentum residual remains allowed.

**Step 0.** L26 (section 6) first.

**Step 1, the existing damping option.** `low_mach_dissipation.f90`
adds a Mach-gated Jameson stress (an undivided third difference of v at
each face, a fourth difference in the cell update) with its energy pair
to the momentum flux (`RK_rhs.f90` line 166; the quadruple-precision
diagnostic route refuses it). Measure it on the frozen `x0.10/HeH9.7`
state and on the 0.03 control. Before relying on it, check its discrete
energy quadratic form with the actual volume weights, the nonuniform
grid and the gate: the header's nonnegative-dissipation argument drops
boundary terms of a variable-coefficient integration and is not
established as written. If it fails, the replacement is written as a
dissipative operator with the sign proven: `dv/dt = -M^-1 B^T K B v`
with `M` the positive volume (mass) matrix, `B` a difference operator
and `K` a nonnegative weight, so that `d(v^T M v / 2)/dt = -(Bv)^T K (Bv) <= 0`

## 6. L26: continuity of the wind-window base boundary

**Evidence.** `base_boundary.f90`: `wind_window_mass_flux` (lines
484-526) averages rho v r^2 over `j_flux..N`, forms the relative spread
`(max - min)/|mean|`, and sets `have_F = .false.` at exactly zero mean;
the blend (lines 660-677) is `w = s(d) w_wind(M_wind) + (1 - s(d)) w_i`
with `s` the cubic smoothstep of the spread and `w_wind` the smoothstep
of the window Mach number over the blend width 1e-8.

**The defect, stated precisely.** The discontinuity is directional at a
zero distant window with a local weight `w_i != 1/2`: along
`F = eps (1,...,1)` the spread is zero, `s = 1`, and `w -> 1/2` as
`eps -> 0`; along a nonuniform `F = eps a` whose spread exceeds the
threshold, `s = 0` and `w = w_i` for every eps. Both paths reach the
same zero window with different limits, so no value assigned at the
zero point repairs it (the rev1 repair is withdrawn). At a fully static
base `M_i = 0` gives `w_i = 1/2` and the limits coincide, so a static
initial condition is continuous in value there and still needs
derivative tests in its neighborhood. Beside this, the `max` and `min`
of the spread have kinks in U when the extremal cell changes inside the
blending interval; an RMS in their place would not be differentiable at
its zero vector, would keep the singular division by the mean, and
would change the meaning of the calibrated threshold (one discrepant
cell contributes its departure over sqrt(n) rather than its full
departure), so it is not the repair either.

**Probes first (frozen states, no repair chosen before them).** On the
certified 0.03 state, on a mid-iteration transient of `x0.02`, and on
its hydrostatic initial condition: the boundary density and its
directional derivative along (i) a constant window flux approaching zero
from both signs, (ii) several nonuniform window shapes approaching zero,
(iii) zero mean with nonzero variance, (iv) local and window signs
disagreeing, (v) an extremal cell switching, (vi) a transient with the
base flux differing from the window's, (vii) the fully static state.
A density jump needs, in addition, the local and reservoir entropy
constructions to differ; the probe reports both densities.

**The repair, a design decision after the probes.** Preferred: a base
closure from the physical reservoir and the local characteristic
information alone, with a face reconstruction or a local boundary
solve, which is the contract `restart_contract_design_20260909.md`
section 7 states; the distant window was introduced to read the
direction the odd-even cell-1 velocity hides (P44), and the probes say
whether a local face-flux reading (the P44 rule: the base FACE flux,
not the cell velocity) gives that direction without the window. If a
nonlocal diagnostic is kept for a time, its influence must vanish
smoothly and uniformly as the magnitude of the whole window flux tends
to zero, along every direction and not only the uniform one, which
means an amplitude weight with a stated regularization scale and a
measured sensitivity to that scale; a regularized squared coherence
measure can be studied mathematically but is not a physical
justification for distant control of a transient base. Tests:
`base_branch` rows for (i)-(vii). Goldens: whether the regression bases
stay byte-identical depends on where the new criterion is inactive;
that is verified after the boundary is specified, never used as a
reason to keep the zero-flux branch. Effort: one day for the probes; the
repair after them. Decision needed: open the probes (recommended,
before L25); the repair is chosen with the user on the probe results.

## 7. L7g remainder: the deposit of the H2 association energy

**Evidence.** `molecular_reaction_heat.f90` lines 469-496: the whole
4.478 eV of R15 is multiplied by `h2_vibrational_heat_fraction`, the
Hollenbach & McKee `(1 + n_cr/n)^-1` for v = 1 with one A and the H and
H2 v = 1 quenching fits, helium absent (`h2_vibrational_relaxation.f90`
lines 189-230). The comment at the site states that the fraction is
valid where the collider density is far above n_cr of EVERY level of the
cascade, and supports that with the v = 1 critical density alone
(n(H2) = 4e9 to 5e13 against n_cr(v=1) = 2e6 to 6e6); the all-level
condition is therefore an unverified assertion until the inventory
supplies the higher levels' rates. The ledger closes on the ionization
potential once; R23's 8.10 eV photon leaves the gas. R6 is
`H3+ + e -> H2 + H` (reaction table lines 261-267), so its products
include a molecule.

**What is wrong in the present term.** The three-body energy partitions
as `D0 = E_trans + E_int(H2) + E_int(M)`; only the internal share is
subject to a radiative-or-collisional branching, the translational
share is deposited promptly, which itself assumes that the products'
kinetic energy thermalizes faster than transport and the relevant
reactions (to be verified where the approximation is used).
Multiplying the whole D0 by a vibrational fraction suppresses the
prompt share in the low-density limit, the wrong limit even though it
evaluates to 1 in this layer. Hollenbach & McKee (1979) treats grain
formation and the H- route and gives no product-state distribution for
gas-phase `H + H + M`.

**Plan, the data inventory first (no implementation before it).** What
the IR module holds (`molecular_infrared_data.f90`: level energies and
line data) and whether it is a connected transition network; which
state-resolved formation distributions for three-body association exist
in the published literature (searched through ADS; read in the
published version; requested from the user when not accessible); which
state-resolved collisional rates exist for H, H2 and He colliders
(helium matters in the helium-rich configurations); what the third-body
identity and the destruction channels do to the partition. Then the
model the data support: a prompt translational share plus the
collisionally thermalized share of the internal excitation, cascaded
level by level if the data allow, or an explicitly bounded two-parameter
approximation (translational share, effective internal quenching) whose
bounds come from data or from energy limits and are never adjusted to
recover a base temperature. The same audit runs channel by channel over
R5, R6, R7 and R16 (energy recipients: fragments, an excited molecule,
or a photon).

**Acceptance tests.** The zero- and high-collision limits; normalized
product distributions; energy conservation per event; detailed balance
where the adopted network supports it; the IR band cooling and the
caloric EOS not counting one energy twice. Measured effect on the held
molecular base (`kzz1e9/HeH2.13`): heat per association, base
temperature, x2 at cell 1, the cell-1 mass row; then the certified

`mol_*` cases carry `heat_mol_chem` and will move; refreshed once.
Effort: two days for the inventory; the model's effort depends on it.
Decision needed: open the inventory (recommended); the model after it.
