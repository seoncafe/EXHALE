## 1. The verdicts

not the cells that are evolved.** `eval_dt` took `CFL min_j dr_j/(|v_j| +
c_j)` over `1-Ng:N+Ng`. Two things are wrong with that for the scheme in

## 2. P6b, the derivation

The hydrodynamic row of cell `j` is assembled in `RK_rhs` as the spherical
finite-volume divergence

    dF_j = ( A_{j+1/2} F_{j+1/2} - A_{j-1/2} F_{j-1/2} ) / V_j ,
    A = r^2 ,  V_j = ( r_{j+1/2}^3 - r_{j-1/2}^3 )/3 ,

(READ, `RK_rhs.f90` lines 211 to 222 and `define_grid.f90`
`spherical_cell_volume`), and integrated by SSP-RK3, every stage of which is
a convex combination of forward-Euler steps of the same length, so the
restriction is the forward-Euler one of that divergence.

`F` at a face is a Riemann flux of the two reconstructed states bounding the
face (HLLC, Roe or Rusanov, under PLM or WENO3). Each is built from the
waves of that face's Riemann problem, whose fastest signal is bounded by

    S_{j+1/2} = max( |v| + c )  over the two states bounding the face.

Godunov's condition is that the fan opened at a face has not left the cell it
enters when the step ends. Written with the geometry the divergence above
uses, a face carries signal into cell `j` at the rate `A_f S_f` per unit
volume `V_j`, so

    dt_j = CFL V_j / max( A_{j-1/2} S_{j-1/2} , A_{j+1/2} S_{j+1/2} )

with the Godunov number `CFL <= 1`. In the Cartesian uniform limit
`A_{j-1/2} = A_{j+1/2} = A` and `V_j = A dr` this is `CFL dr/(|v| + c)`, the
convention the `CFL:` key carries (default 0.6, READ `parameters.f90` line
952), so the input number keeps its meaning and no recalibration is implied.
The stricter condition that the two fans of one cell may not meet each other
inside it replaces the `max` by the sum of the two face terms, which is the
same restriction at `CFL/2`.

**The coefficient the scheme needs is therefore the one the code already
carries**; what the scheme needs and did not have is the geometry and the
choice of cells:

- `V_j/A_{j+1/2} < dr_j < V_j/A_{j-1/2}`: the outer face binds, and the
  cell-centered width is not an upper bound of the interval on either face.
  On the production base grids `dr/r` is 2e-4 and the two differ by that
  much; on the stretched outer grid `dr/r` reaches order unity.
- Only cells `1..N` are evolved. A ghost row is rebuilt by `Apply_BC` after
  every stage, so its own width is not a stability requirement, and the
  lowest ghost does not carry a width of its own at all (READ,
  `define_grid.f90` line 169: `dr_j(1-Ng)` is copied from the cell above).
- The ghosts nevertheless bound `r_edg(0)` and `r_edg(N)`, which are faces
  of the evolved cells 1 and N. Taking the minimum over `1..N` of a
  cell-centered expression would drop the only representation of those two
  boundary Riemann problems; the face form keeps them (R6).

**Validity.** `S_f` is formed from the CELL AVERAGES bounding the face, not
from the reconstructed face states, which can carry a larger `|v| + c` inside
a steep cell. That margin is the one a Godunov number below 1 leaves and it
is the margin this expression has always carried.

**What the derivation does not change.** `eval_dt` supplies the stationary
route's initial pseudo-time and the JFNK floor as well as the marching step
(READ, `EXHALE_main.f90` lines 1644 and 5851, `steady_newton.f90` line
16618), so the change is measured on that path too (section 5).

### 6.3 What each of the four relations says

**The equation of state.** Closed at every ghost at the rounding level, with
a census taken here from the written species columns and not from the code's
own arrays. The two agree bitwise, which also settles that `n_tot` counts
what the census counts. This leg is algebra and it holds.

**The hydrostatic continuation.** Ghost 0 sits at the level's own radius on
both grids, and its cell average stands above the level's point value by
1.5875e-04 in pressure (LHS) and 1.6322e-06 (hot Uranus). That is the volume
average of a convex profile over the cell against the value at its centre,
and it scales as the square of the cell's thickness in scale heights:
`dr/H` measured from the ghost pair is 7.835e-02 and 7.674e-03 on the two
grids, whose squares stand in the ratio 104.2, and the two offsets stand in
the ratio 97.3. The continuation is
doing what `base_ghost_averages` says it does, and the residual departure
from `(1/24)(dr/H)^2` is the `r^2` weight and the temperature variation
inside the cell.

**The molecular caloric relation.** The two ghost cells lie on one
isentrope, and the test that needs no outside number is that its index comes
out the same from the pressure and from the temperature:
`ln(p_{-1}/p_0)/ln(rho_{-1}/rho_0)` is 1.399002 (hot Uranus) and 1.648092
(LHS 1140 b), while `ln(T_{-1}/T_0)/ln(rho_{-1}/rho_0)` is 0.399002 and
0.648093, so `gamma` and `gamma - 1` agree to 2.9e-07 and 1.2e-06. The
`p`, `rho`, `T` triple the boundary writes into the two ghosts is one
isentrope of one caloric gas. The values themselves are those of the two
mixtures: 1.399 for a gas whose hydrogen is 98.5 per cent H2 at 1140 K, where
the rotational modes are open and the vibrational ones are beginning to
contribute, and 1.648 for a gas carrying 9.7 helium atoms per hydrogen
nucleus, just below the monatomic 5/3 by the H2 it still holds.

**The chemistry-temperature consistency.** The ghost's composition and its
heavy-particle plus electron count are fixed points of the sweep's own map,
closed to 1.18E-07 and 3.97E-12 (hot Uranus, 2 applications) and 4.08E-09
and 3.66E-16 (LHS 1140 b, 4 applications), against the contract's 1e-6
(P1 step 2). This leg is the one P1 step 2 installed and it holds on both
states.

### 6.4 Where the 4.7 per cent came from

READ, [lhs1140b_stationary_D5a_20260918.md](lhs1140b_stationary_D5a_20260918.md)
section 1: on an LHS 1140 b molecular state `ntot_bc` moved from
8.402582811444896e-01 to 8.794250686178137e-01, +4.66 per cent, in one sweep,
because the sweep then overwrote that variable, which holds the PRESCRIBED
count at the base level, with the count it had just solved IN THE LOWER
GHOST. Those are two radii one cell apart. MEASURED here, the ratio of the
ghost -1 count to the level count is +4.87e-02 on the LHS 1140 b state and
+5.50e-03 on the hot-Uranus state, and a base cell of the two grids is
7.84e-02 and 7.67e-03 pressure scale heights: the per cent follows the grid.

Two further statements travelled with it and neither survives measurement.
D2b restated it as a distance between the reservoir's particles per unit mass
and the ghost's own; that quantity is 2.7e-08 and 1.3e-07 (section 6.1, 6.2),
and D5a says so itself. D2b and `viscous_conduction.f90` then restated it as
the distance between the ghost's `p/n_part` and the level's temperature on
`carrier_model_a_newton`; that distance is 9.1e-07 at ghost 0, where both are
defined at one radius, and 2.2e-03 at ghost -1, which is one cell lower and
on an adiabat. The measured movement D2b itself reported when the conduction
operator changed from one to the other is 2.596e-05 in one velocity (READ,
D2b section on the conduction case), which is consistent with 9.1e-07 in a
boundary temperature and not with 4.7 per cent.

**The overwrite is already gone.** READ, `ionization_equilibrium.f90` lines
3712 to 3730: the solved counts are handed to `base_boundary` as a
measurement and `ntot_bc` and `dp_bc` keep the values `input_read` resolved.
Nothing here asks for that to change.

### 6.5 What is NOT settled by this

The ghost's pressure and density are built with the reservoir's particles per
unit mass while the caloric map that turns them into an energy and a sound
speed uses the composition's own count (READ, `base_ghost_averages` and
`report_base_boundary_model`). That is a real seam of the model, and what
this item measures is its size: 2.7e-08 and 1.3e-07 on these two states,
because the chemistry at the base returns the base mixture it was given. It
is a property of the state, not a guarantee, and the boundary report prints
both counts at every run so that a state where it opens is visible.
