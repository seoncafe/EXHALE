## 1. L23: the loader does not restore `cert_reason`

**Evidence.** `backup/regression/roundtrip_check.sh` second half:
`ref coupling: ... certified=F cert_reason=no_stationary_claim mode=init`
against `dump coupling: ... certified=F mode=init`; identical on the campaign
binary `db87b88d1ce5`, so pre-existing. `parse_coupling_header` in
`src/modules/files_IO/load_IC.f90` restores `sec_ion`, `sec_ion_step`,
`valve`, `recon`, `iontrans`, `certified`, `mode`, `t_phys`, `fluxconst`
and nothing sets `state_certification_reason` on a reload.

**Plan.** One `case ('cert_reason')` in that parser, its value carried
through `set_state_certified(.false., reason)` (the routine that already
takes the reason) so a re-dumped state says what the original said.
Tests: the `restart_intent_and_metadata` suite gets one assertion (a
dumped reload carries the reference's `cert_reason`); `roundtrip_check.sh`
must pass end to end. Goldens: none move (the header of a reloaded dump
only); `roundtrip`'s golden is re-snapshotted because its header line is
part of the compared file. Effort: under an hour plus the regression run.
Decision needed: none beyond "do it with the next rebuild".

## 2. L15: the JFNK solve is not reproducible at eight threads

**Evidence.** `docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L4h_20260915.md` section 5.0 (two
8-thread runs of the same fixture part after about 21 iterations, mass rows
3.08e-2 and 1.20e-2 at the end of pass 1; one thread bitwise) and
`docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L15_20260916.md` (stage 1: five repeated
`stationary evaluate` runs of one frozen certified state at 1 and at 8
threads give one md5 for each output file, the log identical but the
IEEE flag summary; so the residual evaluation and the ionization sweep are
not where the thread dependence enters).

**Plan, stage 2.** Instrument the JFNK path between two consecutive
residual evaluations: (a) the Krylov cycle (the matrix-free action
`J v`, the Arnoldi orthogonalization, the norms), (b) the banded
preconditioner solve, (c) the line search's merit. For one fixture
(`atomic_elem_newton` reload) and one Newton iteration, dump the linear
right-hand side, every Arnoldi basis vector and Hessenberg entry, the
preconditioned step and the merit at 1 and at 8 threads, and find the
first quantity that differs. The candidates are ordered by likelihood and
each is a different fix: (i) an OpenMP reduction (`sum`, `dot`, `maxval`)
inside the action or the norms — fix by a fixed-order reduction (serial
serial sum of the partial sums of every thread, in thread order) or by serializing that loop;
(ii) a shared scratch array written by more than one thread (a race) —
fix by making it threadprivate or allocated inside the iteration; (iii) a
mutable cache (the attenuated field, the ionization roots) that one thread
updates while another reads — fix by a barrier or a copy; (iv) OpenBLAS
threading inside the banded solve — measured off (`pool = 1`) since S1 to
S9, verify. Acceptance: two 8-thread runs of the fixture identical for 200
iterations, and the 8-thread run identical to the 1-thread run at the
printed digits (bitwise if the reduction order is fixed the same way).
Cost of the fix measured (the 8-thread speed-up is 2.07x on the ionization
sweep; a serialized reduction must not undo it). Then: a bounded
reproducibility study of the catalog — ten certified cases re-solved twice
at 8 threads from their own states, comparing arrays, certification rows,
Mdot and the line (expected: identical after the fix). Tests: a new suite
`jfnk_thread_reproducibility` (two runs at 8 threads, byte comparison).
Goldens: none (they are single-threaded). Effort: one to two days.
Decision needed: whether the campaign's 8-thread REPRODUCE.md records
should be annotated "path not reproducible before L15" (recommended: yes,
one line each, by `write_reproduce.py`).

## 3. L22: one scalar movement bound over the whole carrier column

**Evidence.** `docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L7e_20260915.md` section 23 (row
decomposition of the two refusing well-mixed states): every carrier
relaxation of every pass ended on the composition movement bound, none on
its own residual; the bound was cut 5.0e-3 -> 2.5e-3 -> 1.25e-3 -> floor
1.0e-3 with the pass movement falling in proportion; the cell that attains
the bound is the H2 front (cell 231 at 1.26 Rp) or the far wind (cell 466
at 16.5 Rp, `kzz1e9/HeH0.083`), while the cell that refuses is decades away
in abundance (cell 500 at 29 Rp with x2 = 7.8e-3; cell 306 at 1.95 Rp with
x2 = 2.7e-6). Four catalog cases are held: `molecular_scalar_gj1132_wellmixed`
0.083, 0.55 (and 2.13 for want of a seed) and `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH0.083`.
The bound exists because the wind is held fixed while the carriers relax
(the alternation's linear-response assumption), and it is measured on the
particle count, the quantity the wind feels (review item 3 of 2026-09-15).

**The three questions the plan row poses, and the plan's answers to test.**
(a) Over what region is the fixed wind's linear response one statement?
Physically the wind responds to the particle count through the pressure
and the mean molecular weight, cell by cell, so the response is local: a
bound on the particle-count change of EACH cell (not the maximum over the
column) is the statement, and it is the one already coded as
`carrier_particle_count_change` except that its maximum is taken over the
grid and one scalar `grow` is cut for all cells. (b) The object: a
relaxation whose trial interval is set cell by cell would break the
conservation of the face fluxes (the review's own caveat), so the
candidates are, in order of cost: (b1) a bound per region of the column
with a shared interval inside each region and conservative face fluxes
between regions — two regions suffice for the measured states, the front
(x2 above 1e-2) and the wind above it; (b2) an implicit coupled carrier
step over the whole column (the carriers' block Newton with the wind
response linearized in the Jacobian), which needs no bound because the
wind is no longer held fixed; (c) how to keep either from becoming a knob:
the acceptance is unchanged (every gated row inside its own tolerance, the
particle-count change of every cell inside `trust` at acceptance), only
the path to it changes, and the certified state is compared with the
one-bound route on a case both reach (`kzz1e9/HeH2.13`, certified at pass
11 on the one-bound route) to the certification tolerance.

**Measurements first.** On the frozen `wellmixed/HeH0.083` state: (1) the
relaxation with the bound evaluated per cell and the interval chosen by the
tightest cell OUTSIDE the front region — does cell 500 descend when the
front's cells are excluded from the interval choice (a diagnostic run, no
conservation claim yet)? If yes, (b1) is the design; if the far wind still
does not descend, the obstruction is elsewhere (the outer boundary's
carrier treatment, L8's kind, or the Jacobian's deferred reconstruction
terms) and (b1) is not the answer. (2) The same on `kzz1e9/HeH0.083` where
the bound is set by the far wind itself.

**Implementation, if (b1).** `relax_photochemical_composition` takes a
region table (front / wind, from the x2 profile at entry, re-read each
pass); each region gets its own `grow` and its own bound counter; the
transport step keeps one conservative face flux at the region boundary by
taking the shorter interval on that face. Default on only if the certified
state of `kzz1e9/HeH2.13` is reproduced to the tolerance and the three
well-mixed cases certify; else default off and reported. Tests:
`carrier_retry` rows for the two-region relaxation (conservation of every
element across the region boundary to 1e-14; the one-region case bitwise
the old code), `carrier_returned_state_acceptance`. Goldens: the `mol_*`
snapshot cases do not enter the relaxation (they are marching snapshots),
measured; `mol_carrier` is the one to watch. Effort: two to three days
including the measurements. Decision needed: open it (recommended, since
four catalog cases and the whole well-mixed molecular family depend on it).

## 5. L25 (new): the low-Mach base, the seven low-XUV atomic cases

**Evidence.** L17 (`docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L17_20260915.md`): at 0.02
of the fiducial XUV the base is subsonic to Mach 1e-8 with alternating-sign
velocities in cells 1-6 and the HLLC contact selection (flux chosen by the
sign of S* ~ v) puts every base cell on a kink of the residual; the Newton
model fails (trust-region ratio -26.8); the C1 blend of the two star
fluxes tried there covers 409 of 500 cells at any band width because the
whole domain is below Mach 1e-2 and changes Mdot by 2 percent, so it was
reverted. After L21 the same shape appears at higher XUV: `x0.10/HeH9.7`
and photochem x0.15, 0.20, 0.25 hold the cell-1 mass row at ||R|| about
1.5 with the composition still travelling, at dtau0 = 1 and at 1e8 alike
(`docs/lhs1140b_rerun_20260915.md`; each case's `not_solved.md`).

**What the physics asks for.** A base that is hydrostatic to Mach 1e-8 is
not a wind at the base; the residual there is the pressure balance, and
the flux function must be smooth in the velocity through zero at the
level of that balance. Three candidate treatments, to be measured on the
frozen `x0.10/HeH9.7` state (the one closest to solving) before any is
adopted:
(a) **An all-speed (low-Mach) flux for the base layer only**: an HLLC
whose contact speed is replaced below a local Mach threshold by the
Rusanov/central average of the two star states (continuous derivative in
v), applied only where the cell Mach is below 1e-4 and blended to HLLC
above it; the 2 percent Mdot change L17 measured came from applying a
band over the whole domain, so the first measurement is whether a band
confined to the base (cells 1-20, Mach below 1e-6) changes Mdot at all
(expected: below 1e-4, since the mass flux is set at the sonic point far
outside).
(b) **The base velocity mode itself**: the alternating sign is the P44
odd-even mode; L21 removed it from the boundary's decision, not from the
state. A one-cell viscosity or a face-flux upwinding keyed on the face
flux (P44's rule) in cells 1-6 may remove the mode; measured on the same
state, with the certified 0.03 state as the control (it must not move
beyond tolerance).
(c) **A different base row**: for cells where |v| c is below the
truncation of the pressure gradient, certify the momentum row on the
hydrostatic balance (departure from the well-balanced equilibrium) rather
than on the flux difference; this is a certification change and needs the
anchoring measurement of `docs/certification_tolerance_anchoring_20260910.md`
redone for that row.
Acceptance for any of the three: the certified 0.03 state re-certifies to
tolerance (control), `x0.10/HeH9.7` certifies, and the regression bases
(hot Uranus, WASP; all above the window) are byte-identical. Effort: two
days for the measurements, more for an adopted flux. Decision needed:
open it, and which candidate first (recommended: (b) first, because it is
the cheapest test of whether the kink is the mode or the flux; then (a)).
Without it the XUV grid of the closed branch stays unresolved below 0.30
of the fiducial, and the 2025 non-detection comparison of that branch
cannot be closed.

## 6. L7g remainder: the thermalized fraction of the H2 association energy

**Evidence.** `docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L7g_20260915.md`: the reaction
heat ledger closes on the ionization potential once (no double counting);
R23's photon (8.10 of 9.16 eV) now leaves the gas (review item of
2026-09-15); what remains is that `h2_vibrational_heat_fraction`, the
Hollenbach & McKee v = 1 quenching expression, is applied to the 4.478 eV
of the three-body association (R15), which is not a v = 1 excitation,
giving about 1 at the base (n(H2) 4e9 to 5e13 against n_cr(v = 1) of
2e6 to 6e6). The molecular base sits at 808 K on the corrected boundary
where the atomic one is 238 K; the H2 IR bands radiate 0.2 percent of the
deposit; both IR channels together move the base by 0.2 K.

**Plan.** A formation-state model of the association energy: the nascent
H2 from H + H + M is formed high in the vibrational ladder (the
literature to obtain and read: the three-body association product
distributions — Jacobs et al. 1967, Cohen & Westberg 1983 for the rate,
and for the nascent distribution the measurements or calculations that
Hollenbach & McKee 1979 and Le Bourlot et al. 2012 use for grain
formation; the primary sources are to be found through ADS and read in
the published version); then the cascade: each level relaxes by collisions
(the quenching rates of the existing routine, level by level) or by
radiation (the H2 quadrupole A values already in the IR band module), and
the thermalized fraction is the collisional share summed down the
cascade. Validity stated at the code site (collider density against each
level's n_cr; the assumption of a thermal collider). Measured effect on the
held molecular base state (`kzz1e9/HeH2.13`): the heat deposited per
association, the base temperature, x2 at cell 1, the cell-1 mass row; then
the certified molecular cases re-solved (three of them) and the movement
reported. Tests: `physics_probe` rows for the cascade fractions at three
densities; `molecular_seed`. Goldens: `mol_*` cases carry `heat_mol_chem`
and will move; measured and refreshed once. Effort: three to four days
including the literature. Decision needed: open it; and whether the
same treatment is extended to the dissociative recombination channels
(R5/R6/R7/R16), whose product kinetic energy is thermalized at once now
(defensible: fragments, not excited molecules).

## 7. L12 stages B to F: the ionization closure of the atomic wind above 4 Rp

**Evidence.** `docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L12a_design_20260913.md` and

2 Rp to 1.4e-2 at 44 Rp for H; the line EW read on the solution's
equilibrium composition is 15.5 %A against 1.17 on the `_adv` profile),
so every EW of the atomic catalog is quoted on the post-processed `_adv`
composition, not on the certified state. Stage A (one routine imposing
the transported fractions in all seven ionization systems, byte-identical,
31 tests) landed 2026-09-13; stages B to F are: (B) carry x(H II) per H
nucleus and x(He II), x(He III) per He nucleus as members of the carrier
relaxation operator, advected on the element nucleus flux; (C) lift the
molecular gate so the imposition runs in the atomic route; (D) the
imposed helium fractions in the cell state; (E) certification rows for
the carried fractions (gated above the binding radius as the elements
are); (F) the He 10830 line synthesized from the certified composition
and the `_adv` post-processing retired for the fractions it now carries.
The user's decision of 2026-09-13 was (a) this route first, (b) an
alternative (the post-process as the closure) open for later.

**Plan.** B and C together on one certified atomic case (`kzz1e9/HeH2.13`):
the carried x(H II) must reproduce the `_adv` profile of that case to the
post-process's own conditional tolerance (1e-2 in the mass row) — that is
the acceptance of stage B; then D, E; then F with the EW compared three
ways (equilibrium, `_adv`, certified-carried) on the fiducial and on the
crossing rungs. Every stage byte-identical with the key off. Effort: a
week; the largest item here. Goldens: the `hp_*` cases (ionization
transport) move at stage B; the atomic goldens move only if the key is
turned on for them (it is not, by default). Decision needed: when to
