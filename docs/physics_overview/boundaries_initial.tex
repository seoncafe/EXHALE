\section{Boundary and initial conditions}
\label{sec:bcic}

\subsection{The lower boundary}
\label{sec:bcic:base}

The lower boundary is a characteristic condition imposed \emph{at the base
face} $r_{-1/2}$, with the number of conditions set by the sign and magnitude
of the Mach number there (\texttt{base\_boundary.f90}).  At that face the
eigenvalues are $v-c$, $v$ and $v+c$, and a wave enters the domain when its
eigenvalue is positive, the radial coordinate increasing outward:
%
\begin{equation}
  \begin{array}{llcl}
    0 < v < c   &\text{subsonic inflow}      &\quad& 2\ \text{reservoir conditions},\ 1\ \text{interior}\\
    -c < v < 0  &\text{flow reversal}        &\quad& 1\ \text{reservoir condition},\ 2\ \text{interior}\\
    v > c       &\text{supersonic inflow}    &\quad& 3\ \text{reservoir conditions},\ 0\ \text{interior}\\
    v < -c      &\text{supersonic outflow}   &\quad& 0\ \text{reservoir conditions},\ 3\ \text{interior.}
  \end{array}
  \label{eq:base_branches}
\end{equation}
%
The interior relation is the linearized $v-c$ compatibility condition in its
LODI form \citep{Thompson1987, Poinsot1992},
%
\begin{equation}
  p_b - \rho_i c_i v_b = p_i - \rho_i c_i v_i,
  \label{eq:lodi}
\end{equation}
%
with the subscript $i$ denoting the interior state continued to the face.  The
closed-form Riemann invariant $v - 2c/(\gamma-1)$ is deliberately not used: it
assumes a single constant $\gamma$, and the caloric equation of state gives
every cell its own $\gamma_{\rm eff}(x_{\mathrm{H_2}}, T)$, so that invariant
is wrong wherever the $\mathrm{H}_2$ fraction or the temperature varies across
the boundary stencil, which is the whole molecular base.  The sound speed
$c_i$ is the interior one, because the outgoing wave is an interior wave.

\paragraph{The reservoir is $(p,s)$.}
The two reservoir conditions are the pressure and the specific entropy of the
lower atmosphere at the base \emph{level}, not the pressure and the density.
The distinction matters as soon as the base composition changes: with $(p,s)$
the temperature follows the composition, with $(p,T)$ the entropy does.  The
entropy is carried implicitly, as the isentrope through
$(p_{\rm base}, T_{\rm base})$ at the base composition, which is what
\texttt{base.inp} and the lower-atmosphere profile reader state.

\paragraph{The face is the level.}
The level is $r = 1$, and every grid type places its first face exactly
there, $r_{-1/2} = r_{\rm edg}(0) = 1$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:numerics:grid};
\texttt{base\_boundary\_states} stops the run if it is not).  The
reservoir's $(p, s)$ is therefore the state of the face side it owns, with no
transport along any isentrope, and the contact between the reservoir and the
domain neither moves with the cell width nor encloses a slab of reservoir
gas; the two lower ghost cells lie below the level.  Until 2026-09-27 the
level was the center of a ghost cell, the face lay half a cell above it on
the \texttt{Mixed} grid, and the reservoir was carried up to the face along
its own hydrostatic isentrope.  That put a slab of reservoir gas of thickness
$\Delta r/2$ under the domain and made the base pressure of the domain
$p_0[1-\Delta r/(2H_{\rm res})]$, a first-order error in $\Delta r$: MEASURED,
the molecular lower layer of the LHS~1140\,b He/H\,$=1.8$ state sat 2.2 and
1.15 per cent low in density on 500 and 1000 cells, and the pressure gap
between the interior at the level and the reservoir, $2.93/1.44/0.70$ per
cent on 500/1000/2000 cells, is $0.114/0.067/0.038$ per cent with the face at
the level (boundary model v4, \texttt{face\_at\_level\_v4}).  The wind
quantities stay first order in $\Delta r$ with errors 1.6--1.9 times smaller;
the remaining first-order error is likely the thermal layer at the contact,
which the grid does not resolve (cell-1 temperature 800/695/604\,K against
the 226\,K reservoir), and two first-order terms were found and left: the
element-diffusion Dirichlet He/H at the cell-1 center
(Sec.~\ref{sec:mol:diffusion}) and the conduction bath at the ghost center
$r(0)$ (\texttt{viscous\_conduction.f90}).

\paragraph{The interior state at the face.}
The interior state $(\rho_i, v_i, p_i)$ that \eqref{eq:lodi} reads is built
from the cell average of cell 1 alone (\texttt{interior\_state\_at\_level\_face}),
never from the reconstruction stencil, because that stencil reaches into the
ghost and a boundary condition that reads its own output is circular.  How
cell 1 is carried to the face depends on the reconstruction the boundary
has to agree with.

\emph{With the well-balanced reconstruction} (\texttt{Well balanced: True},
Sec.~\ref{sec:hydro:wb}; boundary model
\texttt{face\_at\_level\_discrete\_equilibrium\_v5}) it is cell 1's own
constant-density equilibrium at the face,
%
\begin{equation}
  \rho_i = \rho_1, \qquad
  p_i = P_{\rm dn}(1) = p_1 + \rho_1\bigl[\Phi_c(1) - \Phi_i(0)\bigr],
  \label{eq:base_interior_wb}
\end{equation}
%
the pressure the reconstruction itself extrapolates cell 1 to at the same
face, formed by the same expression from the same cell averages and
potential arrays, with $T_i = p_i/(\hat n_1\rho_1)$ at cell 1's particle
count.  The boundary and the reconstruction then measure the pressure of
cell 1 at the face from one discrete equilibrium.  That equilibrium is
mechanical, the discrete form of $dp/dr = -\rho\,d\Phi/dr$, and says nothing
about the temperature or the entropy \citep[Sect.~2.1]{Kaeppeli2016}; it
holds for a column of any entropy stratification, and the reservoir is not
changed by it: it still states the pressure and the entropy of the gas it
supplies.  The isentropic continuation below, which this replaces under the
well-balanced option, differs from $P_{\rm dn}(1)$ by about
$(1/2\gamma)(\Delta r/2H)^2$ of $p$, a truncation difference between two
second-order constructions with no physics in it.  MEASURED
(\texttt{src/tests/grid\_and\_gates}, \texttt{base\_reservoir\_equilibrium}
and \texttt{low\_mach\_face\_flux}): on a static column built in the
reconstruction's discrete equilibrium and meeting the reservoir's pressure at
the level, that difference left a face-0 flux of $1.46\times10^{-4}$ of
$(\rho c)_{\rm res}$, $v_b = -2.6\times10^{-4}\,c_{\rm res}$ and a cell-1
momentum row of $3.1\times10^{-3}$ of the weight, $1.47\times10^4$ times the
wind flux against a manufactured wind at Mach $10^{-8}$; with
\eqref{eq:base_interior_wb} and the ghosts below, all three are at the
rounding level (below $10^{-14}$) on the isothermal column, on a column
warming upward and on a column with a pressure-balanced contact at the level,
and the face flux of the manufactured wind is within $2.1\times10^{-3}$
(isothermal) and $2.5\times10^{-3}$ (warming) of the wind at every Mach number
from $10^{-3}$ to $10^{-9}$, falling at first order with the base cell width.

\emph{Without it} the reconstruction carries no discrete equilibrium to
share, and cell 1 is continued along its own hydrostatic isentrope by
\texttt{continue\_hydrostatic\_isentrope} (boundary model
\texttt{face\_at\_level\_v4}), which uses the fact that at rest and at
constant entropy the Bernoulli function is uniform,
%
\begin{equation}
  u(T) + T - \frac{\Phi(r)}{\hat n} = \text{const}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  u(T_b) + T_b = u(T_a) + T_a - \frac{\Phi(r_b) - \Phi(r_a)}{\hat n},
  \label{eq:isentrope_T}
\end{equation}
%
with $u$ the internal energy per particle of the caloric equation of state and
$\hat n$ the particles per unit mass, solved by Newton with
$dh/dT = c_v + 1$; the density then follows from $d\ln\rho = c_v\,d\ln T$ as
$\rho_b = \rho_a (T_b/T_a)^{c_v(T_{\rm mid})}$.  The midpoint heat capacity
makes this exact to third order in $\ln(T_b/T_a)$, and the routine is only
ever used over half a cell and over the two ghost cells, where that logarithm
is below $0.1$ and the density error is below $10^{-7}$ relative.  It is not
a general isentrope integrator over a scale height and is not used as one.
The same routine gives, under both models, the temperature of the reservoir
continued below the level, which the ghost cells carry.  The conduction
operator reads its bath at the ghost center $r(0)$ from
\texttt{base\_reservoir\_temperature\_at}: where a lower-atmosphere profile
is handed over, the bath is the profile's own temperature at that radius
(\texttt{lap\_value\_at\_radius} in \texttt{lower\_atmosphere\_profile.f90},
linear in $r$ between the bracketing levels), because the gas below the level
is the profile's column and not an isentrope (on the LHS~1140\,b Photochem
profiles an isotherm at the skin temperature, which the isentrope missed by
3.5\,K one ghost cell down, 188.89 against 185.42\,K, lowering the conductive
flux into the level by about 4.6 per cent at a fixed cell-1 temperature;
MEASURED, update log stage~3 section~70); without a profile it is the
isentropic continuation, and a reservoir that returns no positive
temperature leaves the ghost's own (\texttt{conduction\_base\_level\_T} in
\texttt{viscous\_conduction.f90}).  The ghost cells of the advection keep
their own isentrope in both cases.  The bath is the reservoir's at rest, at
inflow and during a reverse flow alike, so the conduction boundary does not
follow the direction the contact is upwinded on.  The isentropic continuation
balances an atmosphere that is isentropic between the level and the center of
cell 1 and no other rest state exactly: hydrostatic balance does not imply
constant entropy, and the continuation starts from a point state while cell 1
holds an average.

In both models the interior velocity is carried to the face at constant
$\rho v r^2$, $v_i = \rho_1v_1r_1^2/(\rho_i r_b^2)$, rather than copied.

\paragraph{What is held, and the order of the three steps.}
The acoustic matching comes first: the pressure is a reservoir condition in
every subsonic branch, $p_b = p_{\rm res}$, and the face velocity then
follows from \eqref{eq:lodi} as $v_b = v_i + (p_b-p_i)/(\rho_ic_i)$, so
$v_b$ is a \emph{result} of the matching and does not depend on $\rho_b$.
The direction of the contact is read off the matched state next, and only
then is the contact upwinded: the entropy and composition of the face are
the reservoir's where gas enters the domain and the interior's where it
leaves,
%
\begin{equation}
  \rho_b = (1-w_{\rm rev})\,\rho_{\rm res}
         + w_{\rm rev}\,\rho\bigl(s_i, p_b\bigr),
  \qquad w_{\rm rev} \in \{0,1\}.
  \label{eq:contact_upwind}
\end{equation}
%
\textbf{The upwind choice is discrete and has no width.}  A contact carrying
different entropies on its two sides has direction-dependent traces; a face
density between them is an interpolation and not a law of thermal contact,
and the average of the two isentropes that a symmetric handover returns at
zero has no physics behind it.  This is what distinguishes boundary model v2
from v1, whose entropy source was handed over by a cubic smoothstep in the
face Mach number.

\textbf{The interior's mass flux is carried at the face density (well-balanced
model).}  Through a stationary face the mass flux is continuous: a contact
cannot stand at a face gas flows through, so in a steady state the gas just
inside the level has the density $\rho_b$ of the face state the contact
selects.  Under the well-balanced model $\rho_i = \rho_1$ is the density of a
mechanical model of cell 1 and not of the gas at the face, so once $\rho_b$
is chosen the advective part of the face velocity is the interior's mass
flux at that density,
%
\begin{equation}
  v_b = \frac{\rho_1 v_1 r_1^2}{\rho_b r_b^2} + \frac{p_b - p_i}{\rho_ic_i},
  \label{eq:vb_mass_flux}
\end{equation}
%
and the face state carries the interior's own mass flux wherever the
pressures balance.  MEASURED on the manufactured winds above, dividing by
$\rho_1$ instead made the base face carry 3.9 (isothermal) and 7.8 (warming)
per cent more than the wind at every Mach number, and the ghosts, which
continue $\rho_bv_br^2$, the same excess.  The direction of the contact is
read from the velocity at $\rho_i$; $\rho_1v_1$ has the same sign at any
density, so the two differ only where the two terms of $v_b$ cancel to
$O(\Delta r/H)$, a face that carries no resolved flow either way.

\textbf{At rest and at inflow the reservoir owns the level.}  At a
pressure-balanced contact at rest the two sides may carry two entropies and
characteristic theory alone does not choose between them; this model chooses
the reservoir, and the physical assumption is stated rather than carried
implicitly: the lower atmosphere below the base level is dense and
radiatively controlled, so on the time scales of a stationary solution it is
a heat bath and a column standing at rest above it takes the level's own
temperature.  Its validity is a subsonic base at a level lying inside the
radiatively controlled lower atmosphere, and it says nothing about a
supersonic base or about a level above that region.  Only a \emph{reverse}
flow hands the trace to the interior, and then the reservoir keeps exactly
the one condition its single entering characteristic allows, the pressure.

\paragraph{Which quantity the direction of the contact is read from.}
The contact moves with the gas, so the side that owns the face is the side
the level's mass flux comes from.  The first interior cell's own product
$\rho_1v_1r_1^2$ is not that flux at a base carrying a collocated odd-even
velocity mode: MEASURED on the certified LHS 1140\,b atomic state, the
Riemann face flux is $+1.00000\,F_{\rm wind}$ at every face of the grid while
the cell-centered product reads $-2.00\,F_{\rm wind}$ at cell 1, and the
hot-Uranus base reads $-196\,F_{\rm wind}$ against a face flux of $+0.98$.
The matched face velocity $v_b$ is the face's own velocity and is what
characteristic theory names, but it carries $(p_{\rm res}-p_i)/(\rho_ic_i)$,
and a converged state leaves that pressure difference at about $10^{-4}$ of
$p$, which at a base Mach number of $3\times10^{-7}$ is a velocity two orders
above the flow's own.  The direction is therefore taken from the mass flux
the state carries, read where the cell-centered product \emph{is} a flux:
the wind window $r \ge r_{\rm flux}$ the convergence gate is already defined
on.  The face flux itself is not available here, being produced by the
Riemann solve this boundary feeds.

The window has standing only when it carries a flux at all and carries
\emph{one} flux, both properties of its own profile:
%
\begin{equation}
  M_{\rm wind} = \frac{F_{\rm wind}}{\rho_i r_b^2 c_i},
  \qquad
  d_{\rm window} = \frac{\sqrt{\langle F^2\rangle - \langle F\rangle^2}}
                        {|\langle F\rangle|},
  \qquad F = \rho v r^2 ,
  \label{eq:wind_window_say}
\end{equation}
%
the moments taken over the physical cells of the window.  Where
$|M_{\rm wind}| \ge 10^{-12}$ and $d_{\rm window}\le 2\times10^{-3}$ the
window states the direction; otherwise the matched face velocity does, and
then a velocity below the rounding of the cancellation it is formed from,
$4\epsilon[(|p_i|+|p_b|)/(\rho_ic_i) + |v_i|]$, counts as zero and the
reservoir owns the level.  A relative standard deviation is used rather than
a range because the range is not a differentiable function of the state: its
extrema are carried by particular cells, and MEASURED with one window cell
swept through the argmax the two one-sided derivatives of the face density
are $-242.2$ and $+0.0128$, a slope jump of $1.9\times10^4$ at a point where
the value itself is continuous.  The spread threshold is the geometric middle
of the measured gap between the two populations, to one digit: 302 certified
stationary states run from $4.06\times10^{-5}$ to $1.82\times10^{-4}$ and a
stalled transient reads $1.90\times10^{-4}$, while the tightest state that is
not a wind reads $1.39\times10^{-2}$.  The amplitude floor acts on no state
the code meets: the smallest window Mach number measured anywhere is
$3.7\times10^{-9}$, at a cold start.

\paragraph{The direction is held through each steady solve.}
The separation above is one of \emph{written} states.  The iterates of a
steady solve are not written states, and they do cross the threshold.
MEASURED on three stalled LHS\,1140\,b molecular continuations (the
well-mixed He/H $=0.3169$ and $0.3347$ rungs and the $K_{zz}=10^9$ He/H
$=1.09$ rung, stage exports of every pass): every solve that converged
returned $d_{\rm window}=8.4\times10^{-5}$ to $1.4\times10^{-4}$ with the
reservoir owning the level, and every solve that failed returned
$d_{\rm window}=2.000\times10^{-3}$ to $4.1\times10^{-3}$ with the interior
owning it, several at $2.000\times10^{-3}$ to four digits.  A solve entered at
a written state is pushed across the threshold by its first steps, and to
reach the root it has to cross it again through a residual that steps
there; the outer passes then alternated between a converged and a failed
solve for 60 to 80 passes.  The upwind side of the contact is a property of
a solution, so while a steady solve runs the side is the one the rule
selects at the solve's first evaluation, its entry state, and it does not
change inside the solve.  The rule is applied again to the state the solve
returns; where a converged solve returns a state whose rule selects the other
side, one more solve from that state holds that side, so a root is accepted
only on the side its own rule selects, the condition every written state was
judged by before.  Only the path to the root changes.  The marching route and
every evaluation outside a steady solve apply the rule at every evaluation,
and \texttt{EXHALE\_BASE\_CONTACT\_PER\_EVALUATION=1} does so inside the
solves as well, for a control experiment.  On the well-mixed He/H $=0.3169$
rung, which had alternated for 80 passes, holding the side certified the
state in six passes.

Holding the incoming acoustic invariant $p_b + \rho c v_b = p_{\rm res}$
instead of the static pressure is present in the source as a weight that is
zero.  Both forms are exactly well balanced at rest; on a moving solution the
invariant makes the static face pressure fall below the reservoir's by a
fraction $M$ of itself where a real reservoir's Bernoulli drop is a fraction
$M^2$, which is why the weight is zero.

\paragraph{The supersonic branches.}
A supersonic \emph{outflow} takes every characteristic out of the domain, so
nothing may be prescribed and the whole face state passes to the interior.
That transition is a change in the number of outgoing characteristics and its
width is a declared numerical regularization, a $C^1$ cubic smoothstep in
$M_i$ of half width \texttt{base\_face\_mach\_blend} $=10^{-8}$ about
$M_i=-1$; it is the module's one designed-in width and it does not touch the
entropy source.  A supersonic \emph{inflow} would need a third reservoir
condition which a $(p,s)$ reservoir does not have, so the state is not a
solution of a well-posed problem there; the run reports the crossing and does
not invent a velocity.  A compatibility relation is linear and far from a
solution can ask for any velocity, so the face state is capped at Mach 5 and
the events are counted.

\paragraph{The ghost cells.}
\texttt{base\_ghost\_averages} fills the two lower ghosts from the face state
downward, with the velocity carrying a constant $\rho v r^2$ so the
reconstruction sees no spurious velocity gradient.  Without the well-balanced
reconstruction they are the \emph{volume averages} of the hydrostatic
isentrope through the face state, continued below it, evaluated by 8-point
Gauss-Legendre quadrature weighted by $r^2$.  With it they continue the
reconstruction's discrete equilibrium below the level,
%
\begin{equation}
  P_{\rm up}(0) = p_b, \qquad P_{\rm up}(-1) = P_{\rm dn}(0),
  \qquad
  p_j = \frac{P_{\rm up}(j)}{1 - [\Phi_i(j)-\Phi_c(j)]/\chi_j},
  \quad \rho_j = \frac{p_j}{\chi_j},
  \label{eq:ghost_wb}
\end{equation}
%
each ghost keeping the ratio $\chi_j = p_j/\rho_j$ of the isentropic average,
that is the reservoir-side temperature at the particle count the reservoir
states, so that only its pressure and density move, together, by a factor
$1 + O[(\Delta r/H)^2]$.  The equilibrium mismatch of face 0 is then
$P_{\rm dn}(1) - p_b$, the same pressure difference \eqref{eq:lodi} closes,
and it vanishes with the WENO3 departures of faces 0 and 1 (whose stencils
read the two ghosts) and the jump of the face below the ghosts, whose state
is $P_{\rm dn}(0)$ on both sides, on any column at rest in discrete
equilibrium with the reservoir.  A ghost wider than two scale heights of the
gas it holds, $[\Phi_i(j)-\Phi_c(j)]/\chi_j \ge 1$, has no such equilibrium
and keeps the isentropic average; the events are counted.  Writing the face
value into a ghost cell average instead of either construction is a half-cell
error: the two differ by $\Delta r/2H$, which is first order in $\Delta r$ in
the state and \emph{zeroth} order in the momentum residual it produces, an
imbalance that no resolution removes.  \texttt{Rec\_BC} then puts the face
state itself into the left slot of the base face; the right slot is the
interior's reconstruction of cell 1 down to that face and is deliberately
left alone, the Riemann solver being what mediates the two.

A column sampled from a continuum hydrostatic profile (an analytic isothermal
or isentropic column as cell averages) is not a rest state of the discrete
scheme: its cell averages lie $O[(\Delta r/H)^2]$ of $p$ from the discrete
equilibrium through the same reservoir pressure, and under the well-balanced
model that distance appears at the base face as $P_{\rm dn}(1) - p_{\rm res}$
(MEASURED on the analytic isothermal column, face-0 flux $3.1\times10^{-4}$ of
$(\rho c)_{\rm res}$ on the coarsest base grid, falling at order 2.0).  A seed
has to be projected onto the discrete equilibrium for its lower layer to be
at rest.  The measurement key \texttt{EXHALE\_SEED\_HYDROSTATIC\_CELLS=}$K$
(off unless set; it requires \texttt{Well balanced: True}) does that for a
seed at load (\texttt{seed\_layer\_in\_discrete\_equilibrium},
\texttt{init.f90}): holding $p/\rho$ and the mass fractions of every cell, it
sets cell 1 so that its constant-density equilibrium reaches the reservoir
pressure at the level face, cells $2\ldots K$ so that the equilibrium
mismatch of faces $1\ldots K-1$ vanishes, and scales every cell above $K$ by
the one factor that balances face $K$; a seed so projected is a new state,
and its exact code-unit file is not read.

The base Mach number is recorded every step and a first crossing into
supersonic inflow is warned about, as a validity check on the boundary that is
independent of the chemistry (\texttt{Apply\_BC.f90}).

\paragraph{The ghost composition is a fixed point of the sweep that produced
it (boundary model v3).}
The boundary is built on the two lower ghost cells, whose composition is not
prescribed but solved: the composition sweep is a map from the composition it
is entered at to the composition it returns, the entry state setting the
columns, the cell depths, the electron and particle counts, the
secondary-ionization partition, the shielding and the ionization the base
handoff's partition is stated against.  A ghost that is merely one
application of that map is therefore a boundary that depends on the
composition the run happened to be entered at, and MEASURED it does: on one
frozen physical column, reservoir, spectrum, options and executable, two
entry compositions returned ghosts $4.7\times10^{-3}$ to $5.5\times10^{-2}$
apart in their trace ions, both accepted by every test the sweep applies at
reaction residuals of about $3\times10^{-26}$, and the base continuity row of
the first physical cell read $8.335\times10^{-8}$ in one and
$2.923\times10^{-9}$ in the other, against the same tolerance.  Neither state
is a fixed point, and the difference does not close with the inner solve: over
six decades of the cell solver's stopping accuracy the two states stay put to
$10^{-15}$ while remaining that far apart.

Two statements close it, and together they are what distinguishes boundary
model v3 from v2.  \emph{The seed is part of the model}: the ghost solve
starts from the composition of the gas the reservoir holds at the base level,
whose partition within each element is the first physical cell's, at the
first sweep of a run, and from the ghost the previous sweep returned at every
sweep after it.  The first is the only seed that is a function of the
physical column and the declared inputs alone, which is why the loader
installs it in place of the ghost rows a restart pair carries.  \emph{The
returned ghost is a fixed point of the map that produced it}: \texttt{ioniz\_eq}
applies its own map again at the ghost it returned, restoring the cells above
the ghosts to the composition they were handed at each application, until the
largest absolute change of any ghost species density, in the cell's own
units, is at most $10^{-11}$ and the ghost's heavy-particle plus electron
count moves by at most $10^{-6}$ of itself.  A boundary that cannot reach
that in twenty applications stops the run by name, with both moves and the
accuracy, exactly as the ghost's imposed H$_2$ partition closure does.  What
the routine returns for the physical cells is still one application of one
map, so the stationary residual is still one application of one operator.

The accuracy is set by the base continuity row, which is a near-cancellation
of two face fluxes and therefore reads the ghost composition with a large
gain: MEASURED, a ghost difference of $4.7\times10^{-3}$ takes that row from
$3.90$ to $111.3$ of its own rounding floors, so one floor of the row is
bought by $4.4\times10^{-5}$ of ghost composition and $10^{-11}$ is a quarter
of one floor.  \textbf{The norm is absolute and not per species.}  The sweep
pins the composition absolutely, in the cell's own units, and not stage by
stage, and no quantity the boundary builds reads a trace stage against its
own value: MEASURED on a molecular carrier configuration, a ghost settled
into an exact two-cycle whose H\,{\sc i} moved by $6.5\times10^{-12}$ and
whose H$_3^+$ moved by $2.2\times10^{-12}$ of the cell's composition, worth
$0.06$ of one rounding floor of the base row, while against their own values
those same moves read $4.1\times10^{-9}$ and $5.1\times10^{-3}$.  A relative
statement cannot be made for a stage sitting at $4\times10^{-10}$ of the
cell.  Species below $10^{-18}$ of the cell's own density are left out of the
comparison.  The bracket is stated rather than tuned: $10^{-11}$ sits a
factor 1.5 above the lowest plateau measured, so a configuration whose
composition solve plateaus higher stops the run, and the answer there is to
measure that plateau.

The temperature leg closes with the composition.  The ghost's heavy-particle
plus electron count is what turns its temperature into the pressure
\texttt{base\_ghost\_averages} builds and into the internal energy and sound
speed the caloric map returns, so a ghost whose composition has stopped
moving but whose count has not is a ghost whose rates belong to one gas and
whose thermodynamic state to another; the count is measured across every
application and the rates are rebuilt from the composition each application
is entered at.  Because the applications are \emph{one} sweep, nothing that
separates one sweep from the next may turn over between them; the frozen
background's readiness is held at the value the sweep was entered with and
takes the sweep's own value only when the sweep ends.

The contract covers the lower ghosts of a run whose network is molecular and
carries helium, which is where the boundary's composition holds the trace
ions the base row reads.  A run outside it is marked reached before the first
traversal and leaves after it, so not one number of such a run moves.  The
atomic ghost is not under the contract and its seed dependence is not
measured.  On the state that motivated the contract, one refused molecular
generation, the evaluation now gives one answer whatever the ghost solve is
started from: three seeds $9.7\times10^{-1}$ apart certify with the same
cell-1 row $2.923\times10^{-9}$ and stand at most $8.0\times10^{-11}$ apart
over every column of both state files (MEASURED, item P1 step 2).

The model identity, the seed and what the fixed point reached are printed in
the boundary report and the identity travels in every state file
(Sec.~\ref{sec:bcic:restart}).


\subsection{The upper boundary}
\label{sec:bcic:top}

At an outflowing outer face the characteristics $v+c$ and $v$ leave the
domain; $v-c$ leaves too only where the outflow is supersonic.  Where it is
subsonic, $v-c$ enters and one condition has to be supplied from outside, and
the ghost, a continuation of the interior, supplies it implicitly: at a
subsonic outer edge the continuation is a physical outer condition that can
set the interior mass flux, and its validity is a property of where the outer
face is placed.  The rule is stated once, for every reconstruction: which
characteristics cross a face is a property of the flow and not of the scheme
that reads the ghost (\texttt{Apply\_BC.f90}).
The continuation (\texttt{free\_outflow\_ghost}) is the isothermal hydrostatic
one,
%
\begin{equation}
  \rho_g = \rho_N\,\exp\!\left[-\frac{\Phi_c(N{+}k) - \Phi_c(N)}{p_N/\rho_N}\right],
  \qquad
  p_g = p_N\,\exp[\,\cdots\,],
  \qquad
  v_g = v_N,
  \label{eq:outflow_ghost}
\end{equation}
%
the state of rest the last physical cell carries outward under the code's own
potential with the isothermal sound speed squared $p/\rho$ held at cell $N$'s
value.  Since $\rho$ and $p$ are scaled by the same factor, the ghost is at the
\emph{same} temperature as cell $N$, so the ionization sweep, the column
integral and the sound speed of the time step all see the temperature they saw
before, at the density the stratification gives.

A zero-gradient copy would assert $dp/dr = 0$ at the outer face, a hydrostatic
imbalance of the order of the local weight that no resolution removes, and
under PLM it is worse: the forward difference entering the limiter is then
exactly zero, so the limited slope of cell $N$ vanishes and that cell carries
no pressure gradient at all.  On an analytic hydrostatic column the momentum residual of cell $N$ in units
of its own weight converges at order $1.80$ for \eqref{eq:outflow_ghost},
$1.42$ for a linear extrapolation and not at all ($0.006$) for the
zero-gradient copy.  The velocity
stays a copy: the outgoing characteristics carry it out of the domain and the
boundary has nothing of its own to say about it; a gradient-carrying ghost
velocity either drives the run into a base-breathing limit cycle or divides by
a ghost density the stratification can make arbitrarily small.  A ghost that leaves the
admissible set falls back on the zero-gradient copy and is counted.  The
continuation neglects the inertial term in the ghost cells, which holds where
$M^2H/L_v \ll 1$ with $L_v$ the length over which the velocity varies; a
subsonic Mach number alone is not sufficient, and the incoming acoustic
condition of a subsonic edge is not justified by this approximation, which
is why the outer face is placed beyond the sonic point where a run can place
it.

For the reconstructed face states, \texttt{Rec\_BC} extrapolates outward with
a zero-gradient copy under PLM and a linear extrapolation under WENO3.  The
diffusive operators carry zero flux through the outer face: heat conduction
(\texttt{thermal\_conduction\_coeffs} forms no right-face term in cell $N$,
Sec.~\ref{sec:hydro:transport}), the binary element operator and the eddy
part of the carrier and stage fluxes; the gas crosses that face only with the
advective flux.

\subsection{Initial conditions}
\label{sec:bcic:ic}

\texttt{IC mode:} selects among \texttt{cold}, \texttt{transonic},
\texttt{hot\_parker}, \texttt{auto} and \texttt{windae}
(\texttt{input\_read.f90}; an unknown word is warned about and taken as
\texttt{cold}); the older \texttt{Transonic IC} and \texttt{Hot Parker IC}
keys select the same families and take precedence over \texttt{auto}.
\texttt{set\_IC.f90} builds the first three, \texttt{auto} chooses between
the first two, and \texttt{windae} is built in \texttt{init.f90}.

\paragraph{Cold hydrostatic (\texttt{cold}, the default).}
An isothermal hydrostatic atmosphere at the base temperature,
%
\begin{equation}
  \rho(r) = \rho_{\rm bc}\,\exp\bigl[-b_{\rm eff}\bigl(\Phi_c(r) - \Phi(1)\bigr)\bigr],
  \qquad
  p = \frac{\hat n_{\rm bc} + \delta_{\rm bc}}{\rho_{\rm bc}}\,\rho,
  \qquad
  T = 1,
  \label{eq:cold_ic}
\end{equation}
%
with $\rho_{\rm bc}$ the density at the base level $r=1$ (the ghosts take the
same stratification continued downward), $\delta_{\rm bc} = 10^{-10}$ the hydrogen
ionization fraction of the seed, whose electrons it adds, a small linear velocity seed
$v = \tfrac12(r - 1)$ that vanishes at the level, and a nearly neutral
composition.  The factor $b_{\rm eff}$ starts at 1 and is raised in steps of
$0.2$ until the density at the cell nearest the middle of the domain falls
below $10^{-8}$, so that a low-gravity planet does not start with a domain at
uniform density; the value used is reported, and densities below $10^{-8}$
are then floored there.  This is the ATES initial condition.

\paragraph{Transonic (\texttt{transonic}).}
The steady isothermal-wind profile obtained from the algebraic Bernoulli
integral of the isothermal wind equation in the code's own potential,
%
\begin{equation}
  \tfrac12 v^2 - c^2\ln v - 2c^2\ln r + \Phi(r) = B,
  \label{eq:parker}
\end{equation}
%
with $B$ fixed at the sonic point, where $v = c$ and
$d\Phi/dr = 2c^2/r$.  The subsonic root is taken inside the sonic radius and
the supersonic root outside it, and the density follows from
$\rho v r^2 = \dot M$ with $\dot M$ pinned to the base density.  It exists only
where the potential admits an interior sonic point; where it does not, the
cold hydrostatic profile is kept.  It is needed for deep Roche-lobe-filling
cases whose sonic point sits near L1 and which launch no wind from a
hydrostatic start.

\paragraph{Hot Parker warm seed (\texttt{hot\_parker}).}
Seeding the full isothermal Parker wind, its density as well as its velocity,
destabilizes the run: the hot, low-density Parker column is globally
inconsistent with the code's non-isothermal equilibrium and drains onto the
base.  The warm seed therefore keeps the stable cold hydrostatic
\emph{density} and overlays only a warm temperature and ionization ramp and
the Parker \emph{velocity} as a head start, both anchored to the cold static
base through a cubic smoothstep window that is zero at $r = 1$ and unity above
a stated transition radius.  This removes the inverted base pressure gradient
that otherwise drives an inflow and a thermal runaway.

\paragraph{Automatic selection (\texttt{auto}).}
One exact probe decides between the cold and the transonic family with no
tunable threshold: does the \emph{cold} base sound speed admit an interior
sonic point in the actual potential?  If it does the transonic profile is
taken, which catches both the deep Roche-lobe cases, where the Roche
$d\Phi/dr \to 0$ toward L1 always crosses the critical condition, and the
low-gravity cases whose sonic radius falls inside the domain; if it does not,
the cold hydrostatic profile is taken.  The hot Parker seed is deliberately not
auto-selected.

\paragraph{Wind-AE (\texttt{windae}).}
The ported Wind-AE solver in \texttt{src/modules/wind\_ae/} is run in process
to build a warm-start profile on the code's own grid, which is then loaded
through the restart path.

Every family fills the whole species vector, and a molecular run seeds the
$\mathrm{H}_2$ column from the same two functions the inflowing ghost uses:
the handoff value where a lower atmosphere states one
(Sec.~\ref{sec:mol:handoff}), and the chemical-equilibrium mixing ratio
\eqref{eq:mol:qh2} at the local pressure of each cell of the seed and $T_0$
otherwise, clipped at the
element-ratio ceiling $q_{\max} = 0.5/(0.5 + y)$.  The reason is that
the optically thick base equilibrium is strongly molecular, and a zero-$\mathrm{H}_2$
start makes the base composition solve land on the spurious atomic root.

Two of the families set \texttt{force\_start}, which suppresses the
convergence test for the first iterations: a transonic profile already carries
$\rho v r^2 = \text{const}$, so the marching measure of \eqref{eq:du} would be
near zero at step 0 and the run would exit before the cold wind had heated to
its steady state.

\subsection{Restart}
\label{sec:bcic:restart}

\texttt{Load IC? True} restores the state from \texttt{Hydro\_ioniz\_IC.txt}
and \texttt{Ion\_species\_IC.txt} (\texttt{load\_IC.f90}).  Files carrying the
\texttt{\# columns} schema header are read by label, so every species the file
holds, metal ionization stages included, is restored; a headerless legacy file
takes the original fixed seven-column read and the metals are rebuilt from the
abundances, which is reported.  An element the file does not carry, or carries
as identically zero, is initialized from its abundance exactly as a cold start
does.  An element whose reservoir a lower-atmosphere handoff states has its
loaded column renormalized onto that reservoir by one factor common to all its
ionization stages (a file with the metadata block arrives within the He/H
tolerance below, its \texttt{\# reservoir} line being compared first, so the
factor is 1 for it; a state bound for another reservoir is carried there by
\texttt{map\_state\_to\_grid.py --reservoir} first).  The file's own He/H
ratio is compared against the input's to $10^{-6}$ relative
(\texttt{heh\_dev\_tol}), and a state written at a different element ratio is
rescaled or refused rather than silently adopted, the input file being the
authority on composition.

\paragraph{The exact state.} Beside the dimensional pair every written state
has \texttt{output/conserved\_state.txt}
(\texttt{conserved\_state\_restart.f90}): the conserved variables $u$ and
every species fraction of every cell in code units, written to 17
significant digits, which read back as the same binary64 values.  Read as
\texttt{output/conserved\_state\_IC.txt} beside the two \texttt{\_IC} files,
it restores the physical cells and the upper ghosts at the bits the run ended
on, where the dimensional route rebuilds $(\rho,\rho v,\mathcal{E})$ from
16-digit $\rho$, $v$, $p$ with the energy re-formed from $p$ through the
caloric equation of state.  It is used only when every recorded condition
matches the present run (format, $N$, $N_g$ and the species count, the
checksums of the two dimensional files it was written with, the reservoir
and option fields, the boundary model, the species order, the normalization
constants, the input files, the gravity and irradiation fields, the cell and
face radii, and a checksum of its own data); any mismatch, a load that
changed an option or migrated a legacy token, or a seed whose lower layer is
projected leaves it unused with a printed notice, and the restart proceeds
from the dimensional pair.  The lower ghosts are derived again by the
boundary in either case.  A state whose bits matter, the input of a
stationary evaluation in particular, is to be loaded with it: MEASURED, a
certified state reloaded from the text pair alone moved its rounding-level
mass row past its tolerance at one cell ($3.316\times10^{-12}$ against $3.0\times10^{-12}$),
and reloaded exactly it certified (update log stage~3 section~83).

\paragraph{The intent of a restart.}
\texttt{Restart intent:} states what the loaded state is continued as, and is
refused when no state is being loaded.  \texttt{trajectory} continues the
history, including its elapsed time, and is refused with \texttt{Run mode:
init}; \texttt{relaxation} treats the state as a numerical iterate and
marches toward stationarity; \texttt{stationary} measures the loaded state
and enters the stationary solve from it at once (pseudo-time $1.0$, no CFL
step), and requires \texttt{Solver: Newton}.  Absent the key, a physical run
continues the trajectory and any other run relaxes.  \texttt{stationary}
takes a second word: \texttt{evaluate} certifies the stored pair, writes the
products from it and stops without a step
(Sec.~\ref{sec:postprocessing:files}), and \texttt{equilibrate} puts the
loaded composition on its own fixed point before the solve.  The
stationary solve of a loaded state writes no \texttt{\_adv} products
(Sec.~\ref{sec:postprocessing:transit}).

\texttt{Restart option change:} names the entries of the state file's option
line that are allowed to differ from this run's (\texttt{load\_IC.f90},
\texttt{opt\_name}).  A token that decides which species the state files
carry (\texttt{metals}, \texttt{mol}, \texttt{oxychem}, \texttt{carrier}) is
refused: naming it cannot make the file's rows this run's rows.
\texttt{iontrans} and \texttt{he23strans} are not of that kind, since the
stage and the $2^3$S columns exist either way and the key decides whether
they are a local root or a carried population, so they may be named.
\texttt{carrier\_newton} is a token of the route and not of the equations and
may differ unnamed (the difference is reported and recorded as a
\texttt{route\_change} line).  A token absent from a file written before it
existed (\texttt{interdiff\_enth}, \texttt{carrier\_enth},
\texttt{lowE\_partition}, \texttt{stage\_enth}, \texttt{he23strans}) reads as
\texttt{F}, the equations of the code that wrote it.  The two tokens of a
continuation factor, \texttt{cond} and \texttt{interdiff\_enth}, carry a
factor $s\ne1$ as \texttt{ES23.16E3} (\texttt{T} at $s = 1$), so two factors
are two states.  A five-decimal (F7.5) token, written from 2026-09-29 until
2026-10-01, stands for the interval $[v-5\times10^{-6}, v+5\times10^{-6}]$
and not for one factor: a requested factor inside it loads the state as a
seed with its certification claim dropped (\texttt{legacy\_factor\_token}),
and a factor outside it is a change of the equations under the rule of every
other token.

Every state file carries the lower boundary as well as the state: one line
names the boundary model that built it and one states the prescribed
reservoir with its version, $(p, T, \hat n, r_{\rm level})$ at round-trip
precision.  The reservoir is the only boundary input the physical column
cannot reconstruct, which is why it travels; the ghost rows the file holds
are the boundary's own output and are rebuilt at the read from the column and
this run's reservoir rather than adopted.  A state written under an older
boundary model is \emph{reported} and loaded, not refused: the loader names
the model that wrote it, names the model this run solves, and says that the
boundary is rebuilt from the physical column and this run's reservoir and
that its ghost composition is solved to this model's own seed and fixed
point, so the state's residual is not the residual it was written with.  A
file written before the identity was recorded says so as well.  A state
written before 2026-09-27 (boundary model v3 or older) is nevertheless
refused, by the grid check and not by the boundary model: every cell center
has moved since the face was put on the level (by half a cell on the
\texttt{Mixed} grid), and the loader
compares the file's centers with the run's over the physical cells to
$10^{-10}$ relative (\texttt{verify\_restart\_radii}, \texttt{load\_IC.f90});
a non-finite center is a mismatch and is refused, not a match.  A state on
another grid enters a run only as an initialization seed mapped onto the new
grid by \texttt{src/utils/map\_state\_to\_grid.py} (\texttt{--ic} writes the
\texttt{\_IC} names), which writes
\texttt{mode=init t\_phys=0 certified=F cert\_reason=mapped\_seed} into its
\texttt{\# coupling} line, and the run has to solve it again.  The mapper
interpolates in $\ln r$ ($\rho$, $p$ and the source rates in $\log_{10}$, $v$
and $T$ linearly) and carries each species as its ratio $n_s/n_{\rm H}$ to
the hydrogen-nucleus density, so a ratio uniform in the source stays uniform
to rounding; it is not a conservative remap.  The first physical cells are
mapped from physical cells: the source's lower ghost rows are the reservoir,
boundary data that the loader does not read, so a target center between the
base face and the source's first center (the first cell of a refined base
block) takes the source's cell~1 values instead of an interpolation across
the face against the reservoir row; the upper ghosts are the free-outflow
continuation and stay interpolation partners.  \texttt{--reservoir El/H v}
multiplies every stage column of the element by one factor, the ratio of the
new value to the source's \texttt{\# reservoir} line, at fixed pressure (the
density and the temperature follow the added nuclei).  The hydrogen-nucleus
density of every row is held: for helium the factor $k$ multiplies HeH$^+$
too, and the $(k-1)\,n({\rm HeH^+})$ hydrogen nuclei that brings are taken
out of H\,I, H\,II, H$_2$, H$_2^+$ and H$_3^+$ by one factor
$g = 1-(k-1)\,n({\rm HeH^+})/n_{\rm H}^{\rm H\text{-}only}$, so that every
row carries $k$ times its source He/H and its source ratio of every other
element exactly.  Left in, that hydrogen moves each element ratio of the row
by $-(k-1)\,n({\rm HeH^+})/n_{\rm H}$, $1.5\times10^{-6}$ for
$n({\rm HeH^+})/n_{\rm H}=2.0\times10^{-6}$ and $k=1.75$ (the LHS\,1140\,b
He/H ascent).  The map is refused when the base rows do not come out at the
new ratio within \texttt{BASE\_ROW\_RATIO\_TOL} $=10^{-6}$ relative, the
tolerance of the loader that reads the file, which catches a species holding
nuclei of two elements that the factor does not move (the oxygen carriers
OH, H$_2$O and CO the tool does not multiply).
\texttt{--extrapolate-beyond} continues a state above a stated radius at
constant temperature, isothermal hydrostatic density, constant mass flux and
frozen composition, a seed and not a solution of the steady equations.

A stationary run that alternates a wind solve with a composition relaxation
also leaves a restartable pair of its last completed outer pass, the
generation \texttt{output/pass\_state/<id>/} that
\texttt{output/pass\_state/current} names
(Sec.~\ref{sec:steady:partitioned}); copied to the \texttt{\_IC} names it is
loaded like any state file, and it is never a certified state.  The periodic
write of the marching loop (every 1000 steps) is written \texttt{certified=F}
with \texttt{cert\_reason=marching\_snapshot\_s<step>} for the same reason.
