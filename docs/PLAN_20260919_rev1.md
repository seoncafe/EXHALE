## 1b. Independent re-verification of P1 (advisor, 2026-09-19)

The review's central case was re-run here rather than quoted. The refused
generation `g0004_20260919T103629Z_9b8394a3` of
`molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH9.7` was staged in a scratch directory
from its immutable files and evaluated TWICE with the binary of record,
single threaded (`Restart intent: stationary evaluate`).

**What repeats.** Both evaluations refuse with the same numbers, and their
two output files are byte-identical to each other: the evaluation is a
deterministic function of what it is given. The refusal is
`hydrodynamic mass row max = 8.335E-08 at cell 1`, and the sweep it runs
reports 504 converged roots with a largest accepted reaction residual of
3.65E-17 and a ghost H2 partition closed to 0.0 in at most three passes.

**The tolerance did not move.** The stored certificate of the solve reads
`max = 3.873E-09 ... tol = 1.4E-11 within` in its summary line, which is the
anchor of the cell whose verdict was taken (cell 210), and it states two
lines below that "the largest measure sits at cell 1, whose own tolerance is
7.5E-09 there". The fresh evaluation is judged at cell 1 against the same
7.5E-09. So the whole change is in the measure: 3.873E-09 to 8.335E-08, a
factor 21.5, against an unchanged criterion. (A reader can misread the
summary line's `tol=` as the tolerance of the cell that carries the maximum;
it is not. That is a legibility point, not a defect.)

**Where the two states differ.** Comparing the stored state with the state
the fresh evaluation wrote, over every column:

| rows | largest relative difference |
|---|---|
| the 500 physical cells | 1.3e-11 (`Hydro_ioniz.txt`), 5.6e-11 (`Ion_species.txt`) |
| the two lower ghost rows, thermodynamic | `v` 3.2e-6, `p` 7.2e-7, `T` 7.1e-7, `rho` 1.3e-8 |
| the two lower ghost rows, composition | H2+ 5.5e-2, H3+ 9.8e-3, HeH+ 9.3e-3, He 2^3S 9.3e-3, He II 4.7e-3, H II 4.7e-3, He III 1.5e-4 |

The interior is reproduced; the ghost is not. Both ghosts are accepted roots
of their own cell systems at 1e-17, and the carrier and level rows of the two
certifications agree to every printed digit (carrier balance H2 4.762E-05 at
cell 191 in both). What changes is the base face state and the rows that read
it: the mass row at cell 1 and the energy row, whose maximum moves to cell 1.

**What this establishes, and what it does not.** It establishes that the
solve's ghost is not the ghost the boundary operation reproduces from the
same physical column and the same prescribed reservoir, and that the base
mass row, a near-cancellation of two face fluxes, turns a difference of
order 1e-6 in the ghost's thermodynamic state into a factor 21.5 in the row.
It does not say why the two ghost solutions differ while both are accepted
roots. Two readings remain open: the ghost's own context (its temperature,
its particle count, the local radiation) differs slightly between solve and
reload and the trace chemistry is stiff in that context; or the ghost system
is ill-conditioned in those directions, so acceptance at 1e-17 does not fix
the state to better than 1e-2 in the trace ions. The experiments of P1 step 1
are what separates them, and the decisive one, seeding the reload's ghost
with the solve's ghost, needs the diagnostic key that item introduces.

### P1. A certified molecular state is refused when it is read back

**Evidence.** `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH9.7` certified at outer pass
9 (`info = 0`, carrier H2 row 8.82e-6 against 1e-5, generation `g0004`). A
fresh evaluation of that generation refuses it on the hydrodynamic mass row
at cell 1: 8.335e-8 against the same 7.5e-9 the solve was judged by there,
where the solve's own block read 3.873e-9. Section 1b re-ran this twice and
measured where the two states differ: the physical cells to 1e-11, the
ghosts' thermodynamic state at 1e-6 to 1e-8, and the ghosts' trace ions at
5e-3 to 5e-2.

**What the first text got wrong.** It named the ghost's ionization
acceptance band (`ieq_res_tol = 1e-6`) as the cause and a tighter tolerance
as the fix. That state's accepted roots sit at 3.65e-17 in the same measure,
so the band is not what the evaluation is leaving. Three controls were
conflated: the acceptance of a returned residual, the cell solver's stopping
accuracy, and the imposed partition's own tolerance. Lowering the first does
not make the second take a more accurate step.

**What the evaluation actually does** (READ, `EXHALE_main.f90` lines 5558 to
5700): it loads the conserved state and composition, derives a boundary,
runs the sweep, rebuilds pressure and temperature at fixed conserved energy
from the refreshed composition, rebuilds the boundary and assembles the
residual. So a no-step evaluation can move the eliminated species over the
whole column, not only in the ghosts. Measured interior differences between
the stored and re-evaluated state are 0 in density, 2.2e-16 in velocity,
4.5e-16 in pressure and 2.5e-12 in temperature (READ, the review's state
comparison), which is why the attribution experiment must control more than
the two ghost rows.

**The mechanism, as far as it is measured.** The boundary operation does not
reproduce the solve's ghost from the solve's own physical column, and the
base mass row is a near-cancellation that amplifies the resulting 1e-6 change
in the face state into a factor 21.5 in the row. Whether the residual
difference is context (a slightly different ghost temperature, particle
count and local radiation) or conditioning (acceptance at 1e-17 not fixing
the trace ions better than 1e-2) is the open question; the answer decides
whether an accuracy contract can close P1 at all, or whether the base row
needs a formulation that does not rest on a cancellation.

**P1 step 1, the controlled attribution** (all on one frozen physical
column, reservoir, spectrum, options, executable and thread count):

| experiment | what changes | what it decides |
|---|---|---|
| exact replay | nothing | whether the signed base rows and face fluxes repeat |
| ghost-seed family | the ghost's entry composition only | whether the solved ghost depends on its seed, and by how much in the mass row |
| inner-accuracy ladder | the cell solver's stopping accuracy, with acceptance reported separately | whether the boundary state and the mass row converge as the solve is resolved |
| temperature closure | iteration of the thermodynamic state and the temperature-dependent rates with the composition | whether the restart difference survives a fully closed boundary |
| interior refresh held or free | a declared diagnostic policy on the eliminated species | separates an interior refresh from a ghost effect |
| reload ladder | the written state re-read and re-evaluated repeatedly | a first refresh, a fixed point, a cycle or a drift |

Record at each stage: `p`, `rho`, `T`, the particle and electron counts, the
molecular fractions, the charge and elemental budgets, the local reaction
residuals, both face mass fluxes and their signed difference, the row scale
and its rounding estimate, with the closure context that produced them.

**P1 step 2, the fix, stated as an accuracy contract** (replacing "solve to
rounding"): the boundary returns a state that satisfies its thermodynamic,
partition, ionization and inventory conditions simultaneously, at the
returned state, with a declared physical branch; the ghost must be an
admissible chemical root, not merely a state with a small imposed-partition
residual; a boundary that cannot reach the contract fails explicitly. The
temperature leg (formerly P6) belongs here, not in a later item. The effect
of the remaining closure error on the hydrodynamic boundary residual is
measured, not assumed.

**What P1a does not claim.** A ghost-only change leaves the interior array
untouched in that call but changes the flux the interior equations see, so a
later solve can converge elsewhere. That is measured, not excluded by where
the edit sits.

**P1b is not the next default.** Widening the cell-1 gate to fit the observed
restart spread converts an unresolved measurement into a certificate. Keep
the three statements apart: the discrete balance is resolved and passes; the
state is plausible but the residual is not resolvable to the requested
accuracy; the residual is resolved and fails. The second is reported as
unresolved.

**Prerequisite from R2**: correct the solved ghost electron count before the
attribution experiment reads it (the count enters the diagnostics the
experiment records). Use `calc_ne`'s value, in its own units, rather than a
second incomplete sum.

### P2. The hydrodynamic Newton makes no progress on five states

**Evidence** (D9 step 3b): `atomic_scalar_gj1132x0.01_kzz1e9/HeH2.13`, mass
row frozen at 1.52e-1 from pass 12, `info = 2` at every pass, stopped by the
3 h ceiling in pass 33; `.../HeH9.7`, energy row 0.998 at cell 1 from pass 1;
`atomic_photochem_gj1132x0.01_kzzprofile/HeH9.7`, mass row 0.578 at cell 1,
stopped by the stall rule twice; `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH2.13`,
40 passes then the 6 h ceiling. `.L22/i3_alt` is NOT in this class: it was
published as a generation and never re-solved. The first text listed it as a
fifth failure, which the evidence does not support.

Six times the wall clock and 2.25 times the passes left the stuck rows where
they were, so budget is not the answer. The three x0.01 cases are open
because their old certificates were earned on the pre-D2b blended base face,
and under the accepted contact law their base face flux is 38 to 178 times
the window mean (D9fix section 6): the refusal is physical and a solve is
what is missing.

**If the user opens the investigation**, its design is:
1. Reproduce the stalled iteration completely: not only the written state
   pair, but the radiation context, the elimination state, the branch
   decisions, the scalings, the pseudo-time shift and the reconstruction
   state. Show the residual repeats at fixed inputs before anything else.
2. Establish a resolved interval of perturbation lengths for directional
   differences, and check that the direction crosses no active bound or
   contact branch. A forward difference carries truncation error
   proportional to the step and evaluation noise divided by it, so a
   smaller step is not automatically better and a condition number of an
   unresolved difference describes nothing.
3. Measure separately: the unshifted scaled Jacobian, the pseudo-time
   shifted system actually solved, the preconditioned operator the Krylov
   iteration sees, and the true linear residual against the nonlinear trial
   residual and the refusing rows.
4. Record accepted step lengths, state and merit changes, branch and limiter
   events, and every reason a counter resets.
5. Compare x0.01 with the certified x0.10 as a guide, stating that their
   chemistry and radiation differ as well as the Mach number, so it is not a
   one-variable experiment.
Stop at the measurement and report. No solver control changes, and no
reopening of the held movement bound, without a further decision.

The two `coupled_block_jacobian` failures (R5) join this item: the carrier
action's 0.895 relative error against a central difference whose largest
response in support is 4.79e-12 needs the same noise-floor ladder before it
can be called an implementation error, and the species-row scale inequality
at the last digits needs its own arithmetic contract rather than a bitwise
demand. "It also fails on the control" says only that the recent increments
did not cause it.

### P4. Termination classification, provenance and records (promoted)

**P4a, a correctness defect, before any further campaign.**
`classify_ending` returns `solved` on `INFO = 0` before testing that a
complete, finite state exists, and it accepts an inner `(JFNK|PTC) done
info=` line when the outer route printed no verdict. `publish_state.py`'s
`solver_from_log` repeats the same fallback independently, so a manifest can
carry the same mislabeling. The repair:
1. Determine the route and require that route's terminal event.
2. Keep four facts apart: the process ended, the solver returned, a complete
   state exists, the state certified.
3. Require a complete finite state before calling a stored solve successful.
4. Bind the certificate and the terminal event to one run and one state
   identity.
5. Report conflicting evidence rather than resolving it silently, including
   `info = 0` with a refusing certificate.
6. Test: truncated log, timeout after a successful inner pass, missing
   output, nonfinite output, stale output, ordinary success.

**P4b, the seed of a new solve.** The molecular HeH9.7 solve published a
manifest with a null parent although its seed was known. Carry the resolved
seed generation from selection through publication, alongside the warm
restart policy, and record an atomic-to-molecular conversion as its own
transformation. This is not confined to the old imported records.

**P4c, historical provenance.** Recover what the older records hold into a
separate provenance attachment or a new versioned record. Do not rewrite an
immutable manifest to pretend the fact was there. An identical state pair is
evidence of state identity but does not identify a unique run; where the run,
configuration or time cannot separate two candidates, keep the ambiguity.

**P4d, records that remain wrong.** `classify_ending`'s fallback, six
`REPRODUCE.md` files with the superseded seed sentence (four `.L35/` study
directories and two `pre_D9s3/` archival copies), and the section 8 rows
whose solve facts are "not recorded".

### P6. Physics and numerics (corrected)

- **P6a, the ghost's temperature leg**: moved into P1.
- **P6b, the CFL restriction.** `eval_dt` minimizes over `1-Ng:N+Ng`
  (READ, lines 30 to 46). The correction is not "drop the ghosts": the
  restriction must cover each evolved cell through its own volume, face
  areas and bounding signal speeds, and the base face's Riemann problem can
  carry the fastest wave from the reservoir. Derive it for the flux,
  reconstruction, geometry and integrator in use, and keep the boundary
  trace in the face-speed estimate. Note also that `eval_dt` supplies the
  stationary route's initial pseudo-time and the JFNK floor, so a change
  moves solver behavior, not only a marching time step, and that the no-step
  evaluate route does not call it, so it cannot explain P1.
- **P6c, the ghost temperature against the level temperature.** Replace the
  first text's "4.7 per cent inconsistency" with a table comparing the
  reservoir level, the face trace and the ghost cell averages AT THEIR OWN
  locations, and evaluate the equation of state, the hydrostatic
  continuation, the molecular caloric relation and the chemistry-temperature
  consistency separately. On the fresh HeH9.7 evaluation the written ghost
  values satisfy `p = n k_B T` to 4.4e-16 (READ, the review), which settles
  the algebraic consistency of those values and nothing else. The 4.7 per
  cent figure is D2b's, measured on `carrier_model_a_newton`; it is not
  restated here as a general property and is not to be removed by forcing a
  hydrostatically continued ghost to carry the input level's temperature.
- **P6d, `cx_add_to_turnover`** reads the thread-local rate coefficients and
  the metal base without the cell guard the other entry points carry
  (`charge_exchange.f90` lines 627 to 641). No production call with stale
  rates was reproduced, so the confirmed defect is the missing guard, not a
  demonstrated wrong number. Because the turnover sets a normalization that
  chemical acceptance reads, it is more than cosmetic. Supply the context or
  make the rates explicit, and test alternating cell temperatures on one
  thread and on several.
- **P6e, the elemental base flux.** `element_base_diffusive_flux` is
  assigned from `Jf(0)` of the last operator call
  (`binary_element_diffusion.f90` line 856), so printing it after a
  different evaluation can describe a different state. Report the advective
  and diffusive contributions with their sign convention, units and the
  evaluation they belong to; for a stationary evaluation use that
  evaluation's flux. A report-only change must not move the elemental
  budget, and if a golden moves because a record was added, that is
  identified separately from a numerical change.
- **P6f, withdrawn and replaced.** The transit tool is not restricted to the
  `_adv` products: `EXHALE_TRANSIT_STATE=solution` selects the solved state
  pair (READ). What remains is to test that path on a stationary solution:
  the solution-state spectrum, the meaning of the metastable populations it
  uses, the consistency of the state pair and its provenance, reading from a
  resolved generation rather than a moving `output/`. Do not manufacture
  `_adv` files to satisfy a filename, and do not present a second
  discretization as the certified state.
- **P6g, the line search.** The first text said a collapse costs the full
  3000-iteration cap. The solver already stops after `n_no_descent_max = 12`
  consecutive unsuccessful searches (READ, lines 16378 and 18071 to 18090).
  The item is therefore to diagnose which event keeps a stalled run alive
  (tiny accepted steps resetting the counter, another control resetting it,
  or a different path), not to add a second counter. It belongs with P2.

## 4. Order of work

| order | work | completion criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | P0: freeze the case list and the identities | a count is reproducible from a published rule |
| 2 | P4a and P4b: termination classification in the runner and the publisher, and the seed of a new solve | an interrupted or incomplete solve cannot read as completed |
| 3 | P5a, P5b and R2's electron count | the measurement and certification paths can be trusted |
| 4 | P1 step 1: the controlled attribution, including the temperature closure | which input or closure defect moves the base row |
| 5 | P1 step 2: the boundary accuracy contract | one physical state and branch give a consistent, resolved boundary residual |
| 6 | Molecular reload and boundary tests, with atomic controls over the shared code | physical and numerical acceptance, with nothing claimed that was not run |
| 7 | P2, if opened: the bounded operator diagnosis, with P6g and R5 inside it | cause-specific evidence, no solver policy change |
| 8 | Re-solve the affected catalog cases and refresh the affected references once | results tied to the corrected operator and a validated state identity |
| 9 | P4c, P4d and P3 | the history is recovered or labeled ambiguous |

P6f (the solution-state transit path) is independent and can run at any
time. P6b (the CFL restriction) is justified and validated on its own paths
and is not a prerequisite for P1.

### The user's instruction of 2026-09-20: a state that is a solution is certified

The three molecular states that PASS certification under the binary of record
carried no certified generation, because `run_case.sh --evaluate` resolves
`latest_certified` and refuses where the index names none, and an evaluation
made outside the runner publishes nothing. The user's instruction is that this
is backwards: a certificate is a measurement of the state as written, so a
state that passes it is certified whatever the run that produced it ended as,
and the procedure must not decide otherwise.

Being implemented: `--evaluate` measures a named generation, or falls back to
`latest_complete` where no certificate exists, and says which it took; the
publisher gains the symmetric half of its demotion rule, so an evaluate child
whose certificate says CERTIFIED makes that child `latest_certified`, with the
index recording that the certificate came from an evaluation and under which
binary. The guard rails are that the child must be an evaluation of that
state's own bytes, its certificate must be of the bytes it wrote, an
incomplete or nonfinite state is never promoted, and a refused child still
demotes. The three states are then certified through the runner, and the other
six molecular states are measured the same way so that the catalog's record of
them is a measurement under the current binary rather than an older refusal.

The old refusals were the boundary, not the states: they were made under
boundary model v2, whose ghost composition was seed dependent (P1 steps 1 and
2).
