## 1. What stage 2 did, in brief

**Starting point (2026-09-05).** `docs/code_status_20260905.md` inventoried
the physics and a five-group source audit found three P0 defects (the cell
width `dr_j` shifted by one cell; the photon-grid quadrature at the
thresholds, +9.5 percent in the H I photoionization rate; the sub-Lyman field
500x too faint for He 2^3S) and twelve P1. Phase 0 wrote the tests red on
them; Phase 1 fixed them in three batches (2a, 2b, 2c), with the decisions of
2026-09-05 (one spectrum type for every band, He mass 3.9715, Jupiter radius
7.1492e9 cm everywhere, signed du, every planet example on its literature
SED).

**The PLAN rev2 series (2026-09-06 to 07).** Acceptance contracts and run
modes (Step A: the A2 certification contract, the coupled source step B3c,
the acceptance rules ACCEPT-1/2, the thermal identity gate T15); one
discretization of the material advective term shared by the RK stages, the
stationary rows and both relaxations (B4-1, B4-1c); the stationary Newton
gaining a row and an unknown for every transported balance (B5, B5b, B5c);
the physics corrections of that series (LW band 912-1201 A, the Lya escape
probability of Harrington/Neufeld, the metal photoionization as the phfit2
shell sum with inner shells, the Ca I RR row, the CO destruction channels,
the WENO3 stencil coefficients, the positivity limiter, the fine-structure
escape argument, the IR grid to 30100 K); determinism (THREAD-DET: one
binary for every thread count). Series gate 2026-09-07: `make test` green,
16 cases moved as expected, log Mdot within +-0.05 dex, goldens refreshed
(previous set `golden_pre_series_20260907/`), `make check` PASS, fcheck CLEAN.

**The batches of 2026-09-08 (this session).** The coupled loop's cost was
explained rather than reduced (COST4: the pass count is the slowest of a
ladder of nested modes; COST5: `passes = 2 + log10(excursion / tolerance) /
(decades per pass)`, tested against a tolerance scan; COST6: a global-mode
extrapolation, default off; COST7, decision 8: the loop stops on the
estimated error with a measured safety factor 3). The stationary species rows
were taken apart (B5d: empty base row and collapsing row scale; B5e: the
first species-row solve meeting its target, a trust region defect named and
repaired; B5g: probe evaluations leaving state behind, which turned B5e's
convergence into a stagnation; B5h: the local-equilibrium elimination does
not determine the shielded layer's H2 content, resolved by decision 10, the
carrier transport is required; B5j/B5k: the carrier unknown is `ln n`,
decision 11). The advection post-process was corrected (ADV-STATIC: the 0.78
K of a static column is the dropped enthalpy-flux term; ADV-ENERGY: the
exact steady energy equation; ADV-REFUSE, decision 9: refused where the state
is not stationary, `adv_status` column). Hygiene and documentation sweeps
(HYG-4, HYG-T9, HYG-SETUP, HYG-CHECKED, HYG-DOCS, DOCS-LINES, HYG-PY). Gates
2 to 5; goldens refreshed 2026-09-08 19:05 (previous set
`golden_pre_cost7_20260908/`), gate 5 PASS, 64 files byte-identical.

**Decisions taken by the user, items 1 to 12** (all in the decision
document): 1 b (an energy failure rejects the step), 2 a (amnesty cap), 3
(the D0 document accepted), 4 C (the WASP-121 b composite SED), 5 (planets
on their loaded spectra), 6 B (LW band 912-1201 A), 7 b (inner shells as

### 3.5 The coupled source loop's cost is understood and fixed for the present fixed-point map
