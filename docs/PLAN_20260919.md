## 2. The issues

### P1. A certified molecular state is refused when it is read back (highest priority)

**Evidence** (D9 step 3c, `docs/lhs1140b_catalog_refresh_20260919.md`, the
2026-09-19 D9 step 3c subsection). `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH9.7`
certified at outer pass 9 (`info = 0`, carrier H2 row 8.82e-6 against 1e-5,
generation `g0004_20260919T103629Z_9b8394a3`). The runner's evaluate pass on
that same generation refused it on the hydrodynamic mass row at cell 1:
8.335e-8 against a tolerance of 7.5e-9, where the solve's own final block
read 3.873e-9. The re-measurement is 21.5 times the original. The atomic
catalog's round-trip range is 0.78 to 1.71 (READ, L18 memo).

**What it most likely is.** The lower ghost is re-derived on a restart
(D5b-2). Its molecular partition closes to rounding since D9fix, but its
ionization is accepted at the sweep's normalized tolerance
`ieq_res_tol = 1e-6`, which admits a few parts in a thousand in the trace
ions of a nearly neutral cell (MEASURED, D5b-2 section 5). The cell-1 mass
row has a gain of about 1e9 rounding floors for each relative unit of ghost
composition (D5a table 3). So a restart that starts the ghost's ionization
from a different seed can land inside the same acceptance band and still
move the row by tens of floors. D5a section 9.6 already argued, for this
reason, that the row should not be an acceptance criterion as it stands.
The cause has not been isolated; the first step below does that.

**Plan.**
1. Isolate. Evaluate `g0004` twice, once with the ghost ionization seeded
   from the solve's own last ghost (a diagnostic key, default off) and once
   as the runner does. If the first reproduces 3.873e-9, the ghost's
   ionization is the whole cause.
2. P1a, the recommended fix: solve the two lower ghost cells' ionization to
   rounding (a tighter tolerance and more iterations in those two cells
   only), so the boundary is a deterministic function of the column and the
   reservoir. This adds no physics and moves no interior cell. Acceptance:
   the HeH9.7 solve and its evaluate agree on the cell-1 row to its
   rounding; D5b-2's four ghost variants remain bitwise equal;
   `boundary_state`, `molecular_seed` and `grid_and_gates` pass.
3. P1b, the alternative: derive the cell-1 mass row's tolerance on molecular
   states from the ghost's acceptance band times the measured gain. This
   changes an acceptance tolerance and needs the user's approval.
4. After the fix, re-evaluate the molecular cases, refresh the molecular
   goldens once, and report the movement.

### P2. The hydrodynamic Newton makes no progress on five states

**Evidence** (D9 step 3b, same memo).
- `atomic_scalar_gj1132x0.01_kzz1e9/HeH2.13`: `info = 2` at every pass; the
  mass row is frozen at 1.52e-1 from pass 12 (worst at cells 231 to 232);
  stopped by the 3 h wall in pass 33.
- `atomic_scalar_gj1132x0.01_kzz1e9/HeH9.7`: `info = 2` at every pass; the
  energy row is 0.998 at cell 1 from pass 1; stopped in pass 35.
- `atomic_photochem_gj1132x0.01_kzzprofile/HeH9.7`: mass row 0.578 at cell
  1; stopped by the stall rule twice.
- `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH2.13`: 40 passes, `hydrodynamic_refusal`
  (mass 3.6e-5 at cell 287, carrier H2 6.8e-2 at cell 227), then the 6 h wall.
- `.L22/i3_alt`: published as a generation, not re-solved.
Six times the wall clock and 2.25 times the passes left the stuck rows
where they were, so a larger budget is not the answer.

**Why the three x0.01 cases are open at all.** Their previous certificates
were earned on the pre-D2b blended base face (`w_rev` 0.32 to 0.37). Under
the accepted contact law their base face flux is 38 to 178 times the window
mean (D9fix memo section 6), so the old states are not stationary solutions
of the current model. The refusal is physical; what is missing is a solve.

**Plan (only if the user opens it).**
1. Freeze one Newton system of `x0.01_kzz1e9/HeH2.13` at the pass where the
   mass row freezes, and measure it as L32 did: the Jacobian's conditioning,
   the Krylov residual history, the line search's step lengths, and which
   rows the step moves.
2. Compare with the certified `x0.10_kzz1e9/HeH2.13`, the same model at a ten
   times stronger XUV, to see what the weak wind (log10 Mdot about 5.8, base
   Mach number of order 1e-8) changes in the system.
3. Stop after the measurement and report. A fix is a separate decision.

### P6. Physics and numerics noticed during the series, not yet acted on

- **`eval_dt` takes the CFL minimum over the ghost cells** as well as the
  interior (READ, `eval_dt.f90` lines 20 to 42), so a boundary refresh moves
  the time step at the last bits (D5b-3). Decide whether the ghosts belong
  in the CFL condition. The inner ghosts carry the reservoir state, so they
  should likely be excluded.
- **The ghost closure has no temperature leg**: the ghost's molecular
  partition is closed against its ionization at fixed temperature; the loop
  composition, particle count, temperature is not iterated (D5b-2 section
  12).
- **A 4.7 per cent gap** between the ghost's `p/n_part` and the level
  temperature on a molecular base (D2b). Conduction now reads the level's
  temperature, but the gap itself is an inconsistency of the ghost state and
  is not reconciled.
- **`element_base_diffusive_flux` is recorded but not printed**. At face 1 of
  `lower_profile` the diffusive flux is -5.57e-13 g/cm^2/s, not zero (D2b).
  Printing it changes the elemental flux statistics that `lower_profile`
  checks, so it goes with a golden refresh.
- **`cx_add_to_turnover` reads the rate coefficients without the cell guard**
  that D7a gave the other charge-exchange entry points (D7a, D7b).
- **A JFNK line-search collapse ends a pass only at the 3000-iteration cap**,
  so a pass that cannot converge costs the whole cap before the outer loop
  can judge it (D8 step 8).
- **The stationary route writes no `*_adv.txt` products**, so He 10830 cannot
  be computed for a state solved on that route (`iontrans_metals`). The
  transit tool reads only the `_adv` profiles.
Plan: take them in order of physical consequence. `eval_dt` and the ghost
temperature leg first, since both are boundary-state statements of the
kind P1 depends on. Then the stationary-route products. The rest follow as
small items.
