## 1. L27: the diagnostics evaluate the production stationary operator, and the certification report prints a face-flux budget

**Why.** A diagnostic that reconstructs with whatever `rec_method` the input
left is a diagnostic of the input, not of the solution. The L26 probe's
"base face flux +32.38 F_wind" was that (review section 3.1, verified). The
stationary and residual routes set `rec_method = 'WENO3'` by literal
assignment in nine places of `EXHALE_main.f90` (lines 1231, 1357, 1497,
1531, 1604, 1938, 3657, 3728, 5464, READ), the two-stage marching sets
`'PLM'` at 1873, and the residual probe at 4617 to 4627 sets both and
restores a saved value: the selection is a rule stated ten times and nowhere
once, which is how an eleventh caller came to miss it.

**What to build.**

1. One routine that installs the stationary operator: sets `rec_method`,
   `use_weno3`, `use_plm` and whatever the reconstruction reads, and returns
   the previous selection so a caller can restore it. Name it for what it
   selects (`select_stationary_reconstruction`), not for its role. Replace
   the nine literal WENO3 assignments in `EXHALE_main.f90` by calls to it
   (the two-stage marching's `'PLM'` at 1873 and the probe's save/restore at
   4617 to 4627 keep their own meaning and are left). This is a refactor
   with no intended physics change and must be byte-identical on
   `wasp_full`, `mol_base_handoff` and `lower_profile`.
2. One evaluation entry point for a stored state: install the composition
   and caloric state, install the boundary through `Apply_BC` (so the ghosts
   the reconstruction sees are the boundary's, not the file's; `init.f90`
   lines 199 to 200 update `u` and not `W`, and a caller that converts `W`
   back overwrites the boundary), select the stationary reconstruction,
   reconstruct, and return the face mass flux `r_f^2 rho_f v_f` on every
   face together with the operator identity (reconstruction name, flux name,
   well-balanced flag). Name it for the quantity
   (`stationary_face_mass_flux`). The evaluate route of L9 and the L26 probe
   both call it.
3. In the certification report, one new block printed beside the
   hydrodynamic rows, on the same state and operator: the face mass flux
   minimum and maximum over all faces and at the base face, in units of the
   wind-window mean, and the offset of the base face from that mean. It is a
   REPORT LINE, not a gate: no tolerance is added or changed by this item.
   If a later item wants to gate on it, that is a decision with its own
   anchoring measurement.
4. Rewrite `base_boundary_continuity_probe.f90`'s `local_flux_candidates`
   on the entry point of step 2. Its rows that compared local closures are
   re-measured under the stationary operator; the rows that asserted the
   negative base readings are replaced by rows that assert what the review
   measured (base face within `2e-5` of the window mean on
   `.L26/fid_resolve` and the certified 0.10 state). Keep ONE row that
   evaluates the input's PLM explicitly, labeled as the non-stationary
   operator, so the suite demonstrates that the two differ and that the
   difference is the operator and not the state.
5. A new test row: a restart input that says `Reconstruction scheme: PLM`
   with `Restart intent: stationary evaluate` prints
   `operator: WENO3` and gives the WENO3 face flux.

## 4. L30: one spherical geometry for all transport, and the element face flux exposed

**Why.** The physically conservative spherical divergence is
`D(F)_j = [r_+^2 F_+ - r_-^2 F_-] / V_j` with `V_j = (r_+^3 - r_-^3)/3`, and
every contribution to one conserved quantity must divide by the same `V_j`.
The implementation divides advection by that `V_j` and diffusion by
`r_j^2 (r_+ - r_-)` with a 1 cm floor on the width (review section 4.1;
verified at the lines named in section 0). Under the exact weights the
diffusive face terms acquire the factor `V_j/(r_j^2 dr_j)`, which differs
between neighbors on the nonuniform grid, so an internal face does not
cancel in the column sum: the mixed operator conserves nothing exactly. That
is a defect of the implemented equations, and by the standing rule it is
corrected regardless of its size. L12b named it and deferred it because the
correction moves every element row; the review says, and this plan adopts,
that this is not a reason.

**What to build.**

1. Geometry defined once. In the grid module: `face_area(0:N) = r_edg^2`
   and `cell_volume(1:N) = dr (r_+^2 + r_+ r_- + r_-^2)/3` in the
   cancellation-resistant form, in the same units the transport modules use
   (`rp = r R0`, `rep = r_edg R0` today). Both element and carrier modules
   read these and form no geometry of their own. The 1 cm floor is removed:
   a zero-width cell is a grid error and the grid constructor refuses it.
2. The element operator: the diffusion matrix and residual (lines 2395 and
   2589), the trace-element residual and matrix (3204 and 3337) divide by
   `cell_volume(j)`; the advective coefficients (2273 to 2279) read the same
   arrays. The Jacobian rows `aa, bb, cc` follow the residual coefficient by
   coefficient, and the preconditioner that uses them is rebuilt from them,
   never edited separately.
3. The element face flux exposed. One public routine returns, for a given
   state and face mass flux, the four arrays the L12b memo section 7 asks
   for: the advective face element mass flux `F_rho(f) Y_el(f)`, the
   diffusive face flux `J(f)` (the existing private `element_face_flux`,
   lines 2174 to 2209, assembled over faces), the face element nucleus
   density `n_el(f)` and the face mass per nucleus `m_1(f)`. The residual
   and the matrix are then written FROM these arrays, so there is one
   spelling of the flux and the diagnostics read the same object. Name it
   for the quantity (`element_nucleus_face_flux`). Units stated in the
   header.
4. The carrier operator: the same two replacements (4600 and 4864) and the
   same shared geometry in `carrier_advective_divergence` and
   `carrier_advective_face_coefficients` (3628 and 3663).
5. The boundary budget: the nucleus flux through the base and the outer
   face is what the reservoir and the outer ghost rule (`X_ghost = X_N`, L22
   step 2c) imply, stated once; at the outflow no stage or element flux is
   imposed that the elemental flux does not carry.

**Tests** (new rows in `src/tests/element_operator/` and the carrier
suites; each RED on the entry text and GREEN after):

- Conservation identity: on the catalog's nonuniform 500-cell grid, the sum
  over cells of `V_j R_j` equals the boundary face-flux difference plus the
  integrated source to roundoff (`1e-13` relative), for the element and the
  carrier operators separately.
- A zero-source constant-flux state has zero residual to roundoff.
- A static equilibrium (`v = 0`, uniform composition) has zero residual.
- A manufactured smooth profile: the residual of the exact solution falls
  as second order under refinement 250 / 500 / 1000.
- Derivative checks: the analytic Jacobian rows against a central
  difference of the residual, after the geometry change, on the certified
  fiducial and on `mol_diffusion`.
- The exposed face flux: `element_nucleus_face_flux` summed against the
  residual reproduces the residual bitwise (one spelling).

**Impact measurement (not a criterion).** Evaluate, through the L9 route
and with the L27 budget, the certified atomic fiducial, the certified
`x0.10/HeH2.13`, and the certified molecular `kzz1e9/HeH2.13` on the control
build and the measured build; report the movement of every gated row and
whether any certification flips. Expected order: `dr^2/12` relative on the
diffusive term, i.e. small against the 1e-5 elemental tolerance on most of
the column, but this is to be measured, not assumed. The regression cases
that run element diffusion or carrier transport (`mol_diffusion`,
`mol_carrier`, `lower_profile`, and any molecular case with `He_diffusion`)
will not be byte-identical; report the movement and refresh the goldens
ONCE at the close of this item, keeping the 2026-09-17 set as
`golden_L30_<date>/` with a `NOTE.txt`.

**Files.** `src/modules/init/define_grid.f90` (or the grid module that

## 6. L32: one frozen rejected coupled Newton system, measured before any preconditioner is designed

**Why.** Thirteen of thirteen linear solves of the coupled block exhausted
40 vectors at relative residual 0.2597 against 0.1 (L22 I3, READ); the same
failure mode was met at L24 and N27. The evidence places the failure at the
linear solve; it does not identify a defective species preconditioner as the
cause, because a mean Jacobian error of 3.7e-4 does not bound the stiff rows
and a finite-difference check can share a defect with the action (review
section 6.1). Widening the subspace cannot cure an inaccurate action;
strengthening the preconditioner cannot either. The user's standing rule of
2026-09-10 closed the stage-2 solver program; this item is a bounded
measurement whose verdict is a decision for the user, not a solver item.

**The checkpoint.** The coupled block's ENTRY state on the certified

quoted `2.597e-01` was measured; it is the largest of the seventeen legs
and the state is on disk. (Correction of 2026-09-18, READ from the run log
by L32: trust-region iteration 14 was an ACCEPTED step at `1.337e-01`; the
rejected ones are 13 and 16, and `2.597e-01` belongs to the first leg

linked against the production objects, the way the review's probe was;

   interleaving an unrelated state; the spread against the noise floor.
2. A perturbation ladder for `Jv` (four step sizes), with the maximum and
   the RMS error reported SEPARATELY for the mass, momentum, energy,
