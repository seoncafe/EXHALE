## 1. Verdicts

**The solved ghost DOES depend on its seed, and the dependence is between
two accepted states, not a smooth sensitivity.** Entering the sweep with the
lower ghost rows the state file itself carries, instead of the reservoir row
whose partition is the first physical cell's, moves the ghost's trace ions by
0.5 to 5 per cent, and the cell-1 continuity row from 8.335e-8 to 2.923e-9,
a factor 28.5, against an unchanged cell-1 tolerance of 7.5e-9. The
evaluation then CERTIFIES. Both ghosts are accepted at reaction residuals of
3e-26, so acceptance at that residual does not fix the trace ions of this
cell.

**Seeding the reload with the solve's own ghost reproduces the solve's
certificate in the verdict and not in every number.** The verdict cell and
its measure are reproduced to every printed digit (cell 210,
`1.148E-11 against 1.4E-11, distance 8.057E-01`, READ from the stored
`certification.txt` and MEASURED in the reload). The cell-1 maximum reads
2.923e-9 where the solve's own block read 3.873e-9: both below the 7.5e-9 of
that cell, a factor 0.755 apart.

**Within that state the seed dependence is weak.** Moving one part in a
thousand of the ghost's H2 into H I, at fixed hydrogen nuclei and fixed mass,
moves the solved ghost by at most 5.6e-5 relative and the cell-1 row by
0.07 per cent (2.923e-9 to 2.925e-9), and the evaluation still certifies. So
the two evaluations are not two points of one smooth branch: they are two
states the same cell system accepts.

**What this does NOT establish.** It does not separate the two readings
section 1b of the plan left open. Two accepted states 5 per cent apart in the
trace ions, both at 3e-26, are equally consistent with a genuinely
multi-rooted ghost system and with one root whose trace directions are not
resolved by the cell solver's stopping accuracy. The inner-accuracy ladder
(experiment 3) is what separates them, and it is not run here. No default and
no tolerance was changed by this item.

**R2, the solved ghost electron count, is corrected.** The diagnostic now
reports `calc_ne`'s own count of the composition the sweep returned. On this
state it moves from 7.0823617917690252E-08 to 7.1555170238566316E-08 in units
of n0, a relative change of 1.022e-2: the 1.02 per cent the review measured
for the molecular ions of ghost cell -1, met again. No data row of any state
moves.

## 2. R2: what changed and what it is a count of

`ghost_ne_solved` summed H II, He II, He III and the metal stages and
declared the molecular ions negligible. `calc_ne` counts H2+, H3+ and HeH+
with their charges, and the sweep calls it on the post-sweep composition
before the diagnostic is built, so the consistent array `ne` is already in
hand. The second sum is deleted and the count is now `ne(1-Ng)/n0`.

The comment at the assignment now states what the count is of (the free
electron density of the composition the sweep returned, counted by `calc_ne`
and by nothing else), the charge carriers it contains, its units and its
validity range (any network the sweep solves; in an atomic gas the molecular
terms of `calc_ne` are absent and the value is the H/He count it always was).

The consumers are `write_setup_report.f90` and the boundary report
(`report_base_boundary_model`), both diagnostics, so nothing numerical moves.

## 3. The instrumentation, all default off

### 3.1 `EXHALE_GHOST_COMPOSITION_SEED`

The composition the two lower ghost cells enter the composition sweep with.
Unset, the sweep is entered at the rows the caller installed, which for a
restart are the reservoir row whose partition within each element is the
first physical cell's
([lhs1140b_stationary_D5b2_20260918.md](lhs1140b_stationary_D5b2_20260918.md)
section 7). Set, it names the source:

| value | the seed |
|---|---|
| `the_state_file_ghost_rows` | the lower ghost rows the restart pair carries, which `load_IC` otherwise drops |
| `the_previous_sweep_ghost` | the ghost composition the previous sweep of this run returned |
| `<path>` | a file of rows in the format `EXHALE_GHOST_SEED_WRITE` writes |

A named source with nothing stored falls back to the entry state and says so
on one line. The replacement stands before the element census, because it
changes the ghost's elemental ratios, which the census asserts are invariant
ACROSS a sweep and not across a seeding.

`EXHALE_GHOST_SEED_WRITE=<path>`, also default off, writes the lower ghost
rows as they were installed, in the format the seed key reads back, so that a
perturbed seed is made from a measured one rather than assembled by hand.

A run with either key set is entered at a composition the boundary model does
not state. Its result is a measurement and never a certificate.

### 3.2 `EXHALE_GHOST_RECORD`

One record per evaluation, appended to `output/ghost_record.txt`. Per lower
ghost cell: `p`, `rho`, `T` from the conserved ghost rows the boundary LEFT
through the same caloric map the work state was built with, the heavy-particle
and electron counts of the composition the sweep returned, the molecular
fractions `x_H2`, `x_H2p`, `x_H3p`, `x_HeHp` and the ionization fractions
`x_HII`, `x_HeII`, `x_HeIII`, the charge and elemental budgets, the reaction
residual the cell was ACCEPTED at, the residual of its imposed molecular
partition at the returned state, and the pass the closure ended on.

The charge budget is the charge the composition the sweep WROTE OUT carries,
read back through the mass fractions and the species table's charges, against
the electron count `calc_ne` made of the same state: it closes the round trip
every consumer of the state takes. The elemental budget is the largest
relative departure, over helium and each trace element, of the ghost's
nucleus ratio n_El/n_H from the first physical cell's.

And in the same record, one base line: the signed continuity row of cell 1 as
the residual holds it, the mass flux through the base face and the flux
through the face above it, their signed difference, the row's scale and the
rounding floor in the units the row is judged in
(`steady_residual.f90`, `mass_row_rounding_floor`). The row is not the flux
difference: the face areas and the cell volume stand between them, and the
record carries both so that a move in either can be seen.

(111.3 and 3.90 floors), so neither reading is arithmetic noise.

## 8. Noticed outside this item, reported and not changed

1. The summary line of the hydrodynamic mass row prints `tol=` of the VERDICT
   cell beside `max=` and `cell=` of the cell that carries the maximum, so
   `max= 2.923E-09 ... cell=1 tol= 1.4E-11 within` reads as a contradiction
   until the verdict line two rows below is read. Section 1b of the plan
   already calls this a legibility point; it is met again here on three
   evaluations.

   file holds the last sweep's entry rows. That is what it is for (the
   evaluate route runs one sweep), but a marching user reading it should know
   which sweep it describes.
