# PLAN_20260913: the stationary solve of the LHS 1140 b wind

User decision of 2026-09-13 (option 1): the stage-2 solver program, closed
on 2026-09-10 and reopened once on 2026-09-11 for the partitioned route, is
reopened for ONE item: making the current code reach a stationary,
certifiable state of the LHS 1140 b atomic wind (subsonic to the 30 R_p
boundary, T = 226 K helium-rich base at 1 microbar, binary H/He element

## 1. What is known at the opening (2026-09-13, 10:20)

- **The archived solutions were never stationary in the base layer.** The
  2026-08-30 gates were blind below 1.2 R_p (`Update_EXHALE_stage1` §133
  and §161: the old flux window began at 1.2 R_p and the residual was
  judged on the wind); the archived `info = 0` states carry the odd-even
  velocity sawtooth of P44 in cells 1 to 30 (v = -538, +32, +29, -15, +1.5,
  -12 cm/s in the first eight cells of `examples/scalar_base_kzz1e9`),
  which is O(1) in the mass and momentum rows of the current cellwise
  residual. Mapping such a state onto the current grid hands the solver a
  seed whose residual is 1.9 in the base layer and small above 1.03 R_p.
- **The physics of the base layer moved by factors 2 to 3.4.** The current
  code's evaluation of the mapped state (`Do only PP` pass) gives a total
  heating 3.36 times the archived value at r = 1.000 (H I, He I, He II
  channels each 3.4x), 1.95x at 1.010, 1.00x at 1.035, and 0.85 to 0.98x
  above 1.05 (the photon-grid quadrature, 2c-QUAD, removing the 9.5 percent
  overcount); the He(2^3S) channel is 0.55 to 0.86x everywhere and the He
  recombination-photon channel 0.1x at 1.035 falling to 1e-5x at 20 R_p
  (§125). The wind above 1.05 R_p therefore needs a re-adjustment of a few
  percent, the base layer one of order unity.
- **Marching at the default CFL does not settle**: the mass-flux spread
  `du` stays 8 to 15 for 28 000 steps from a cold start and 4 to 11 for
  25 000 steps from the mapped seed; the cellwise residual stays 1.6 to 2.0
  at every 500-step checkpoint. This is the CFL-0.6 limit cycle of §161
  (absent at CFL 0.15 on the hot Uranus), on a planet whose base layer is
  colder and whose wind is subsonic throughout.
- **Marching at CFL 0.1 and 0.05 from the mapped seed does not settle
  either** (corrected 11:10 by the L1 measurement): `du` 0.077 at step 2,
  0.13 to 0.25 at step 499 and then rising monotonically, 1.54 at 7202 and
  2.27 at 11121 (CFL 0.05), 4.82 at 11349 (CFL 0.1); the march drives an
  inward flow of 7.7e3 cm/s at 1.05 R_p and the residual rows move there
  (mass 1.70 at cell 165, energy 1.96 at 327). Stopped.
- **The partitioned route itself is sound on the current tree** (control,
  11:35): the HD 209458 b element-diffusion reload
  (`backup/regression/atomic_elem_newton/IC`, `Restart intent: stationary`,
  `EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0=1.0`, 16 threads) is CERTIFIED at outer pass 12
  (`info = 0`; hydro rows mass 1.2e-9, momentum 1.7e-14, energy 1.3e-8;
  worst species row 7.7e-6 of 1e-5), 12 passes in about 10 minutes. The
  route therefore works on one planet from a consistent seed; that does not
  by itself say that the seed is the only LHS obstruction (conditioning,
  coupling, the boundaries and the closures all change with the regime;
  review of 2026-09-13, section 1), and L5c later showed the discretization
  to be the larger part. L4d then measured a second obstruction that is in
  the route and not in the seed: the stagnation detector stopped every pass
  after the first, on this fixture and on LHS alike
  (`docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L4d_20260913.md`).
- **The stationary solves do not take a far seed**: the coupled JFNK
  (dtau0 1, 0.1, 0.01), the marching hand-off and the partitioned route all
  end at a cellwise residual 1.4 to 2.0 with "no descent along the Newton or
  the damped Gauss-Newton direction" or a refused composition pass.

  `mol`, `molbase`, `carrier`, `carrier_newton`, `oxychem` and `iontrans`
  are layout tokens of the `# options` line (`load_IC.f90`): they decide how
  many unknowns a state has, so a file written with `mol=F` (34 species
  columns) is "a state of another equation set" for a run with `Molecular
  chemistry: True` (38 columns: the atomic 34 plus `H2 H2p H3p HeHp`), and
  `Restart option change` may not name them (K44). That refusal is right:
  the file does not carry the molecular unknowns, so nothing can be loaded
  for them.

The opening is therefore to WRITE those unknowns: a state file that carries
the certified atomic wind (rho, v, p, T, the 34 atomic species, the
diffused He/H) and a physically stated molecular partition, so that
`Load IC? True` starts the molecular run from a wind that is stationary to
the atomic tolerances and only the chemistry has to move.

### 5.2 Two ways to write it, and the recommendation

**(A) An offline tool, `src/utils/atomic_to_molecular_state.py`** (the same
kind of tool as `map_state_to_grid.py`; no Fortran change):

1. Read `Hydro_ioniz.txt` and `Ion_species.txt` of a certified atomic case
   whose input matches the molecular case in everything but the chemistry
   keys (same spectrum, base, He/H, K_zz, grid): for the LHS tree that is
   the `atomic_scalar_*` case of the same group name with `atomic` replaced
   by `molecular`, and for `molecular_photochem_*` the atomic rung of the
   same profile. Refuse anything else (the reservoir line, the grid line and
   the option tokens other than the six molecular ones must agree).
2. Form the H2 partition as an explicit choice, stated in the file. The
   code's own molecular seed (`src/modules/init/set_IC.f90`, "WHERE x2 COMES
   FROM") takes the fraction `x2` of the hydrogen NUCLEI bound into H2,
   `x2 = 2 q (1 + He/H)/(1 + q)` with `q` the base handoff's `q_H2_base`
   (or the profile's value at the matching level), and moves that fraction
   OF THE TOTAL HYDROGEN BUDGET into H2, capped by the neutral hydrogen the
   cell has: in density space
   `delta = min(x2 n_H,nuclei, n_HI)`, `n_H2 = delta/2`, `n_HI -> n_HI - delta`
   (the `dfHI = min(x2/mass_per_H, f_HI)` of the code). That is not the
   same as a fraction of the NEUTRAL budget (`x2 n_HI/2`) once hydrogen is
   partly ionized; the two agree only in fully neutral gas. The seed follows
   the code's form, with each cell's own element census (the diffused He/H
   of that cell, not the base value) where `n_H,nuclei` is counted. An
   imposed base partition is a boundary statement; carrying it through the
   whole wind is an initialization choice and is written as such. `H2+`,
   `H3+`, `HeH+` are written at zero; the first sweep finds their roots.
3. Write BOTH files consistently: `Ion_species_IC.txt` with the 38 columns
   and `Hydro_ioniz_IC.txt` with the same metadata block (the loader
   compares every metadata field of the two files and refuses a
   disagreement, `load_IC.f90` near line 1162), the `# options` tokens
   derived from the TARGET run's configuration (`mol=T`, and `molbase`,
   `carrier`, `oxychem`, `iontrans` as that input states them, not a fixed
   set), the species declaration and the reservoir fields of the target,
   the `# coupling` line `mode=init t_phys=0 certified=F` (a seed, not a
   continuation), the source's provenance kept as `# molecular-seed-from:`
   and one `# molecular_partition:` line stating `q`, `x2`, the cap and the
   thermodynamic invariant chosen (3a below).
3a. Choose the thermodynamic invariant and say so. Two H atoms into one H2
   molecule keep the nuclei and the mass but remove half a particle per
   converted pair, so with the electrons and the other species unchanged
   `n_particles,new = n_particles,old - delta/2` and `p = n k_B T`: rho, v,
   p and T cannot all stay. The loader reads p and T from the hydro file and
   rebuilds rho from the species (`load_IC.f90` near line 834), and the
   caloric EOS reads the molecular composition, so the hydro file is NOT
   independent of the partition. The pilot keeps rho, v and p (the
   pressure-force profile of the certified wind), recomputes T from the new
   particle census and the energy with the production EOS; the alternative
   (keep rho, v, T; recompute p and the energy; then a pressure-balance
   adjustment) is run once on the same seed and compared. Neither keeps the
   chemical or the energy equilibrium of the source: the seed does not
   promise that only the chemistry moves.
3b. Prefer an explicit conversion executable that calls the production
   census, EOS, boundary and metadata routines (a small program linking the
   EXHALE modules, or a mode of the binary that reads the atomic state and
   writes the molecular one and stops), with a Python driver only for the
   bookkeeping; a Python re-implementation of the census and the EOS is a
   second implementation to maintain. This is an initialization product; it
   does not weaken the ordinary restart validation.
4. Checks before writing (the B5 rule of the mapper): the mixing ratio `q`
   against its ceiling `0.5/(0.5 + He/H)` (NOT `x2`, which runs 0 to 1
   over that range: `y = 2.13, q = 0.1` gives `x2 = 0.569`, admissible),
   `x2 <= 1`, the transfer against the hydrogen actually available, H-nucleus
   and mass conservation to 1e-12 in every cell, every species non-negative,
   the He/H of every cell unchanged, and the EOS closure of the written
   state (composition, p, T, energy describe one state).

**(B) In the code: admit `mol` and `carrier` in `Restart option change` and
rebuild the partition at load.** The same partition, formed inside
`load_IC` when the file lacks the molecular columns and the run has them.
It saves a step in the runner but puts a physical construction inside the
restart contract that was designed to refuse exactly this, and it makes the
partition invisible in the state files (no line records it).

**Recommendation: (A).** It keeps the contract as it is, the seed is a file
one can read and diff, and it fails loudly on a mismatch. (B) is not
numerically better when (A)'s seed is far: the same partition and the same
thermodynamics give the same distance whether the construction happens
offline or in `load_IC`.

### 5.3 The route once the seed exists

`Load IC? True`, `Well balanced: True`, `Secondary_ionization: Immediate`,
`Solver: Newton`, `Restart intent: stationary equilibrate` (the loaded
composition is put on its own chemical root before the state is measured,
which is what a seed whose molecular ions are zero needs), the carrier
partitioned route (the H2 carrier is an alternated row, as in
`backup/regression/carrier_model_a_newton`), `EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0=1.0`, and
`CFL: 1.0e-12` for the post-processing pass. The runner already has the
molecular branch prepared as `models/run_case.sh.molecular.patch` (L4c); it
becomes: pick the atomic case of the same name, run the initializer into
`output/`, then the recipe above.

### 5.4 What has to be measured, in order

Zero initial H2 does not switch the molecular network off: its source terms
form molecules from atomic material (`System_HeH_mol.f90`), so "the atomic
solution is reproduced with H2 below 1e-20" is not an invariant and is not a
test. The tests are separated:

| test | what it shows | pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| T-L7-1, conversion identity | zero requested transfer (`q = 0`) preserves the source species, nuclei and the chosen primitive invariants before any chemistry runs | every written species column and rho, v, p equal to the source to round-off; metadata of both files consistent |
| T-L7-2, EOS closure | the written composition, p, T and energy describe one state under the production EOS | the energy recomputed from (composition, p, T) equals the written one to 1e-12; the same after the loader's rebuild |
| T-L7-3, loader acceptance | both headers and all 38 columns are valid and the file is treated as initialization | `load_IC` accepts, reports `mode=init certified=F`, and the setup report names the molecular tokens of the target input |
| T-L7-4, chemical validity | after `stationary equilibrate` the reaction residuals, the charge closure and the element budgets meet their criteria; H2 is not required to stay zero | the closure ledger of the first sweep: no cell without a root, budget violations below 1e-6 |
| T-L7-5, end to end | one LHS seed reaches joint hydro and species certification | `molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH2.13` from `atomic_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH2.13`: certified, or the refusing row named |
| T-L7-6, the hot-Uranus round trip | `carrier_model_a_newton`'s certified molecular state projected to an atomic seed and back | the projection has to be DEFINED first (removing columns loses the nuclei in the molecular ions and, for HeH+, helium; the electron and charge bookkeeping and the invariant must be stated); then certified, Mdot within 1e-3, the H2 front within one cell |
| T-L7-7, the He-poor case | `molecular_scalar_gj1132_wellmixed/HeH0.083` | whether the 5e4 K table ceiling is reached on the stationary route |
| T-L7-8, the profile cases | `molecular_photochem_*` from the atomic rungs' last iterates | as T-L7-5 |

A first chemical sweep that fails does not prove that no root exists:
preserve the failing cell and distinguish an inconsistent reservoir
constraint, a poor initial guess, an inaccurate Jacobian or scaling, and
genuinely incompatible equations, over several admissible starting states,
before attributing the failure to the rate set's temperature range.

### 5.5 The two molecular blockers are items of their own

- **The caloric domain.** `caloric_eos.f90` (near line 215) makes a cell
  molecular whenever its H2 density is positive, and
  `energy_semi_implicit.f90` (near line 790) then applies the molecular
  ceiling `T_ceiling_mol_K` = 5e4 K: a trace of transported or
  nonequilibrium H2 selects the ceiling, and it need not vanish instantly
  in a hot cell, so "a hot cell has no H2 worth considering" does not
  remove it, and an abundance cutoff would add a discontinuity and break
  the energy consistency. Item: inspect the energy bracket with the
  composition held exactly as the operator holds it, then decide between a
  consistent high-temperature extension of the molecular caloric model
  (with a consistent internal energy and heat capacity and no double count
  of the dissociation energy), a change of the coupled chemistry/energy
  solve, or an explicit refusal of states outside a documented validity
  domain.
- **The ghost reservoir chemistry.** Reproduce the ghost -1 failure with
  the boundary-imposed composition and thermodynamics, not with an
  unconstrained interior equilibrium solve: a reservoir that prescribes a
  molecular partition is not automatically in local chemical equilibrium
  with the irradiated ghost state. Determine which quantities the boundary
  may prescribe and which chemical rows it must solve; neither exempting
  the ghosts from the check nor tightening the iteration tolerance is an
  answer without that analysis.
- The Koskinen (2022) rate set was measured on a 1140 K base; whether a
  root exists at 226 K is what T-L7-4 and the reproduction above answer,
  and only then is it a physics item.
