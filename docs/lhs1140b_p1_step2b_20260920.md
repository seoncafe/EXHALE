## 1. The verdict

**The contract asked for an accuracy in a norm the sweep cannot deliver in
and no quantity the boundary builds reads: each ghost species against its OWN
value.** It is none of the four candidates the brief lists, and the measured
sequence refutes three of them by name (section 3).

The composition the sweep returns is pinned ABSOLUTELY, in the composition's
own units, and not stage by stage: MEASURED on this case, the sweep settles
into an exact two-cycle in which H I moves by 6.5e-12 of the cell's own
composition and H3+ by 2.2e-12. Measured against each species' own value,
those same moves are 4.1e-09 for H I, which is at its fixed point, and
5.1e-03 for H3+, which is at 4.2e-10 of the cell and has no resolved value of
its own to be measured against. The contract read the second number, found no
fixed point, and stopped a run whose boundary was in fact converged in every
quantity it builds: the same two-cycle moves the ghost's particle plus
electron count by 4.9e-12 and, by step 1's measured gain, the base continuity
row that reads the ghost by 0.06 of that row's own rounding floor.

**The fix is the norm, not the number.** The move is now taken in the
composition's own units, where each species enters the cell's particle count
with the weight it has and, times its own mass, the cell's mass and the base
face flux; and the accuracy, 1e-11, is bracketed by a MEASURED floor and a
MEASURED ceiling that leave exactly one decade (section 4). The refusal stays
loud and no accuracy was loosened to what a case happens to reach: 1e-11 is
below what the base row can resolve, while the case that failed reaches
6.8e-12.

## 3. Why this configuration and not the ones that closed at 4e-13

Each candidate was stated before it was run.

**1. The coupled carrier solve changes what the sweep is entered with between
applications.** REFUTED. Dropping `Coupled carrier solve: True` and keeping
`Molecular carrier transport: True` alone reproduces the same two-cycle and
the same stop, with the same 5.145e-03 from application 12. The coupled
carrier key is incidental: the two failing rows are simply the only rows of
that fixture that run a molecular case with the carriers transported.

**2. A quantity that must be held across the applications is turning over, as
`bg_ready` did.** REFUTED. A turnover is a discrete flip and would not move
with the arithmetic. The plateau does: over the inner composition solve's
stopping accuracy (`EXHALE_IEQ_TOL`),

| inner stopping accuracy | what the sweep settles at | the run |
|---|---|---|
| `sqrt(dpmpar(1))` = 1.49e-8, the default | 5.145e-03 | stops at twenty applications |
| 1e-10 | 1.302e-06 | stops at twenty applications |
| 1e-12 | below 1e-06 in two to five applications | completes |

**3. The carrier's unknowns and the ghost's composition are one coupled pair,
so iterating one at fixed other cannot contract.** NOT WHAT HAPPENS. The map
does contract here in every direction that carries weight: the first sweep of
the run closes in three applications, the composition closes to 5.0e-14 in
the composition's own units, and after the opening transient the moves fall
below 1e-16. What does not contract is the direction the plateau of candidate
2 lives in: the map's gain in the H3+ direction is close to one, so the
solve's own resolution is not damped away, and the cycle sits at the
resolution rather than below it. That is a statement about arithmetic at
2e-12 of the composition, not about a coupled pair of unknowns.

**4. The state is a cold start that nothing has converged yet.** REFUTED as
the cause. The cold start is the ONE sweep that closes without trouble
(three applications, ending at 5.0e-14); the sweeps that stop the run are
later ones, at a state the marching has already moved.

**And why the states of step 2 closed.** Their trace ions sit three decades
higher: on the LHS 1140 b generation the ghost's H II is 8.8e-07 and its H3+
2.1e-08 of the cell (READ,
[lhs1140b_p1_step1_20260919.md](lhs1140b_p1_step1_20260919.md) section 5.1),
so the same absolute resolution is 1e-05 to 1e-04 of their own values and the
relative norm had room. Here the same ions sit at 4e-10 and 4.7e-18. The
contract did not meet a worse state; it met the same arithmetic on a gas
whose ions are smaller.

## 4. The accuracy, and why this number

The move is now the largest change of any species of the two lower ghost
cells in the `f_sp` layout, the species' number density per unit mass of the
cell. That is the weight with which the species enters the cell's particle
count and, times its own mass, the cell's mass and the base face flux built
on it. No quantity the lower boundary builds -- the mass flux, the charge,
the particle count, the opacity, the caloric energy -- reads a stage at 1e-10
of the cell with the weight it reads one carrying a third of it.

**The ceiling: what the base continuity row can resolve.** READ,
[lhs1140b_p1_step1_20260919.md](lhs1140b_p1_step1_20260919.md) sections 5.1
and 5.2: a ghost that moves by 4.1e-09 in its hydrogen partition (x_HII
8.848e-7 against 8.888e-7, x_H2 by 3.9e-9) takes that row from 3.90 to 111.3
of its own rounding floors. That is 107.4 floors per 4.1e-09 of composition,
so ONE rounding floor of the row is bought by 3.8e-11. (That partition and
the `f_sp` layout differ by the hydrogen nuclei per unit mass, 0.75 on that
state, an O(1) factor.)

**The floor: what the composition solve delivers.** The plateaus MEASURED
here, in the same units: 6.8e-12 on the case that failed, 3.5e-12 on the
hot-Uranus molecular carrier state of `backup/regression/carrier_model_a_newton`,
and 1.3e-14 to 3.3e-13 on the LHS 1140 b generation from three different
seeds.

**1e-11 is the one decade inside both.** It is a quarter of the rounding floor
of the row that reads the ghost, so a ghost held to it cannot move that row by
anything the row's own arithmetic resolves; and it is above every plateau
measured, by a factor 1.5 on the case that failed and 2.8 on the fixture. The
margin is thin and is stated as such: a configuration whose plateau exceeds
1e-11 stops the run by name, with both numbers, which is the intended
behavior. The alternative, an accuracy above one rounding floor of the base
row, would be stating a tolerance the row can read.

**The count leg keeps its own accuracy, 1e-6 against its own value**, where
step 2 set it and for the reason step 2 gives: the ghost's heavy-particle plus
electron count is an O(1) quantity whose relative move is what turns the
ghost's temperature into its pressure and its caloric energy. MEASURED, the
composition leg is what binds everywhere: the count closes at 4.9e-12 on the
case that failed and at 4.0e-13 to 1.9e-12 on the LHS 1140 b generation.

**The compare floor is gone.** `ghost_composition_compare_floor` (1e-18)
existed to keep species whose own last digits are not a composition out of a
relative comparison. In the composition's own units nothing needs keeping out:
a metal column of a run without metals sits at a value of order 1e-301 and
moves by at most that.

## 9. What this does not settle

- **The margin between the two ends of the bracket is a factor 5.6**, and the
  accuracy sits 1.5 above the worst plateau measured. A configuration whose
  composition solve delivers less than that stops the run. Widening it would
  mean stating an accuracy above the rounding floor of the row that reads the
  ghost; tightening the inner composition solve, which the ladder of section 3
  shows would work, changes every number the code produces and is not this
  item's to do.
- **The atomic ghost is still not under the contract**, as step 2 left it.
- **The base row still rests on a cancellation**, as step 2 left it: this item
  changes the accuracy the ghost is held to, not the conditioning of the row
  that reads it.
- **A full stationary solve under the contract has not been run**, as step 2
  left it.
