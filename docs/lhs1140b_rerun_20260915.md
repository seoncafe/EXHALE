# The LHS 1140 b catalog re-solved on the corrected base boundary

every case of the catalog, case by case, against the results of 2026-09-14.

Every number below is READ from the case directories by

## 1. What was re-solved, and why

The campaign of 2026-09-14 was solved with the base boundary condition
taking its entropy branch from the CELL-1 VELOCITY -- on this planet the
odd-even artifact of item P44, not the flow -- and with a handover window of
1e-6 in face Mach number, above this planet's own base Mach number of
4.6e-7. Both are fixed (item L21,
`docs/lhs1140b_stationary_L21_20260915.md`): the branch is keyed on the
wind-window mass flux and the window is 1e-8. The base then receives the
226 K reservoir `base.inp` states, instead of the interior isentrope, and
that is a different first cell on every case of this planet.

The 2026-09-14 results are preserved unchanged in
`LHS1140b/models_20260914_preL21/` (83 case directories with their own
`REPRODUCE.md`; its `README.md` states what they are). They are the SEED of
the re-run: `pick_seed.py` gained a first tier, a case's own most recent
certified state, so each case continues from the state that already solves
every equation but the base row.

The catalog was solved TWICE, and both generations are here. The first,
`db87b88d1ce5` (built 2026-09-15 20:38; L4h, L7e, L7f, L18, L19, L21),
measured the boundary correction itself; the second, **the final pass** on
`c2e9c9990b9f` (built 2026-09-16 10:43, manifest
`models/BINARY_MANIFEST_c2e9c9990b9f.txt`), put the whole catalog on the
binary that carries the review fixes as well, so that every case of record
stands on one md5. Each case's `REPRODUCE.md` names the binary, its md5 and
the host; both machines of the NFS mount, `lart4` and `lart3`, were used.

## 3. What moved

**The base is the stated reservoir now, and it is the biggest movement in
the tree.** The first physical cell falls by up to 72 per cent in
temperature, median 46, and its density rises by a factor of up to 3.40,
median 1.83. The ghost the boundary states carries `base.inp`'s `T_base` to
the digit it is given in. Nothing else in the column moves by that much: the
profiles cross near cell 21 and agree to a fraction of a per cent in the
outer wind (item L21 section 5 measures that crossing on the fiducial case).

**The mass-loss rate falls by about 2 per cent everywhere**, 0 to -4.0 per
cent with a median of -2.0, which is the fiducial case's -1.77 per cent
repeated across the tree. No case rises: a colder, denser base is a smaller
wind.

**The He I 10830 line follows the base, and where the line is weak it
follows it hard.** The red-pair equivalent width moves by a median -2.0 per
cent, but the range runs from -82 per cent to +4. The large negatives are
the low-helium rungs, where the metastable column is small and sits low:
`atomic_scalar_gj1132_kzz0/HeH0.55` goes from 0.1682 to 0.0305 %A as its
first cell goes from 304.7 K to 234.0 K, and `kzz1e6/HeH0.55` from 0.1682 to
0.0591. These rungs are far below the measured 1.108 %A either way and do
not carry a crossing; what they show is that the 2^3S population of a cool,
helium-poor base is not a robust number.

**The crossings move by a few per cent and one moves by 14.** The scalar
ladders at K_zz = 1e7 to 1e11 and the well-mixed one move by 1 to 3 per cent
in He/H; the two ladders with the least diffusion move most,
`kzz0` 2.7169 -> 3.1010 and `kzz1e5` 2.7074 -> 3.0706, because their rungs
sit nearest the measured line and a 2 per cent shift in equivalent width
walks a long way along a shallow ladder. The flux-closed rungs still have no
crossing: every one of them sits above the measurement.

**The rungs converge to the same composition as before**, in four or five
iterations: He/H at the matching level 2.0924, 3.0034, 5.0057, 7.0080,
8.0091, 9.0103, 9.7111, 10.011, 12.014.

## 4. The cost, and the cases that do not come easily

A case that was near its root cost 1 to 34 outer passes, median 5, and that
is what the seed tier of section 1 buys: the seed is the answer to every
equation but the base row.

The XUV-scaled cases are the exception. At 0.10 to 0.33 of the fiducial
spectrum the composition enters far from its fixed point (item L14 measures
that), and on the corrected boundary they enter further still: the first
solve spends its forty outer passes with the hydrodynamic rows refusing --
the outer-cell energy row of item L4f, cell 500, above 1e-6 while the gated
species row is already at 1e-12 -- and the state then has to be continued at
the raised pseudo-time start (`DTAU0_CONTINUATION`, item L4e), which is what
certifies it. Measured: `atomic_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH4.0` took 14 min on
2026-09-14 and 4 h 30 here, the first solve refusing at pass 40 and the
continuation returning info = 0 immediately after.

take the same mapped seed to `EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0=1.0e8` instead of letting the

what was wrong and nothing else.

## 5. The final pass, and what it proves

The 79 cases that certified on `db87b88d1ce5` were solved again on
`c2e9c9990b9f`, each from its OWN db87 state (`pick_seed.py` tier 0, the db87
tree preserved by `models/archive_results.sh 20260915_db87`). **All 79
certified**, and they did it in ONE outer pass each: 67 prescribed cases in
five minutes of wall clock, about 24 s apiece, the 9 rungs in forty minutes,
the 3 XUV-scaled photochemical cases in a minute. That is what a seed that is
already the root costs.

The agreement between the two generations is the check that the final binary
did not move the wind, and it is printed in the block above, `final against
db87`: over the 79 cases both certify, the red-pair DEPTH is identical to the
last bit in all 79; the mass-loss rate in 52 of 79, worst departure 1.8e-9;
the first cell's temperature and density agree to 8.3 decimal digits, the
equivalent width to 7.6. Every one of those is four to eight decades inside
the tolerances the certification itself asserts (1e-6 on a hydrodynamic row,
1e-5 on a gated species row), and the handful of largest departures are all
flux-closure rungs, where the Photochem column is re-solved and the
alternation stops at a slightly different point of the same fixed point. The
two binaries solve the same equations and reach the same states.

## 6. A defect this re-run found

`run_case.sh` carried only He/H onto the seed. `load_IC` compares the
`# reservoir` line ELEMENT BY ELEMENT and refuses a state whose ratio
differs from the run's by more than 1e-6 in any of them, and a case with a
lower-atmosphere profile takes every ratio from that profile's `X_<El>`
column at the matching level. The six XUV-scaled photochemical cases read
their column from the flux-closed He/H = 9.7 rung, which this re-run
re-solved, so C/H moved by 3.7e-6 and all six were refused at load with
their helium correctly carried. FIXED 2026-09-16: the rule now lives in
`src/utils/profile_match_level.py` (`profile_match_ratios`,
`reservoir_ratios`, `reservoir_moves`, and a `--reservoir-options` command),
`run_case.sh` calls it and carries every element that moved, and
`element_flux_closure.py`, which had its own copy of the same rule, now
calls the same routine. Verified by the six cases loading and solving.
