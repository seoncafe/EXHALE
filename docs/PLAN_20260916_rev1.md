## 1. L23: restore `cert_reason` under a stated metadata contract

**Evidence.** `parse_coupling_header` (`src/modules/files_IO/load_IC.f90`
lines 1770-1829) reads `certified` and has no `cert_reason` case, so
`roundtrip_check.sh` shows `ref ... certified=F cert_reason=no_stationary_claim`
against `dump ... certified=F`; pre-existing on `db87b88d1ce5`.
`set_state_certified(flag, reason)` sets both fields at once
(`utilities.f90` lines 31-38), and a TRUE certification carries the token
`wind_certification_token(rep)` when species rows were judged in the wind
alone (`certification.f90` lines 1357-1364).

**Contract.** The file's pair (Boolean, reason or token) is read into
imported-state metadata (`ic_certified`, a new `ic_cert_reason`), and is
distinct from the pair the current executable writes after it evaluates
the state. A raw reload that writes without evaluating carries the
imported pair through unchanged (that is the roundtrip). An evaluation
writes the pair it determined, and never a stale success token after the
state or the model changed. The restoration is by the reload path
writing both imported fields into the state metadata once, not by calling
`set_state_certified` inside the parser (which would make the header's
token order decide the result).

**Tests.** `src/tests/grid_and_gates/restart_intent_and_metadata.sh` gets
rows for: true claim with token, true claim without token, false claim
with each reason, absent reason, reordered header fields, a reason at the
32-character limit, and a re-evaluation that changes the verdict.
`roundtrip_check.sh` end to end. **Goldens:** no output number moves; the
`roundtrip` golden is re-snapshotted because its header line is part of
the compared file, and that is reported as a header change, not as "no
expected output changes". Effort: half a day. Decision needed: none.

## 2. L15: localize the thread dependence along the failing trajectory

**Evidence.** L4h memo section 5.0: two 8-thread runs of the same fixture
part after about 21 iterations (mass rows 3.08e-2 and 1.20e-2 at the end
of pass 1); one thread bitwise. L15 memo: five repeated `stationary
evaluate` runs of ONE certified atomic state give one md5 at 1 and at 8
threads. That excludes the residual evaluation AT THAT STATE for the
quantities written; it does not exclude the perturbed evaluations
`F(U + eps v)` the Jacobian action makes along a trajectory that is not
at a certified state, nor arrays not written, nor values below the
printed precision. The OpenMP regions of the code are all inside the
residual evaluation (the ionization sweep, `util_ion_eq`, the constrained
chemical equilibrium, `diffusive_photochemistry`, `RK_rhs`, `PLM_rec`);
the Newton and Krylov code holds none, and its inner products are serial
intrinsic sums.

**Experiment.**
1. Two 8-thread runs from the failing seed (`backup/regression/atomic_elem_newton/IC`,
   `EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0` as the fixture uses), same binary and BLAS setting
   (`pool = 1`), with a checksum of U, the composition, the
   scales and the pseudo-time shift written to a side file at each iteration; find the first
   divergent iteration.
2. At the preceding checkpoint dump U, composition, active bounds, the
   cached radiation and chemistry state, the preconditioner input and the
   shift, in binary.
3. From that checkpoint, repeat `F(U)` and the actual perturbed evaluations
   of the Jacobian action at 1 and 8 threads, restoring the state between
   evaluations; also change the evaluation order, to expose a dependence on
   history (a cache updated by one evaluation and read by the next).
4. Compare, in order: the preconditioner input and output, each Jacobian
   action, the Arnoldi coefficients, the merit. Dump full basis vectors
   only around the first divergence.
5. One molecular configuration after the atomic cause is understood.

**Fix candidates, by what the localization shows.** A shared scratch
array written by more than one thread: give it an owner (threadprivate or
allocated inside the region); a barrier does not repair it. A mutable
cache read across threads: copy per evaluation. An OpenMP reduction whose
result depends on the thread count: a reduction with a fixed partition
and tree independent of the thread count, or a serialized sum; adding
partial sums in thread order is not enough because the partition changes
with the count. Acceptance: two 8-thread runs of the fixture identical for
200 iterations; the 8-thread run agrees with the 1-thread run bitwise
where the reduction is made count-independent, and the speed-up of the
sweep (2.07x at 16 threads, memory record) is re-measured after the fix.
A reproducibility study of the catalog then uses difficult saved
iterates or controlled admissible perturbations, not the certified end
states (which exercise almost no Krylov work). Tests: a new
`jfnk_thread_reproducibility` suite. Goldens: none (single-threaded).
Effort: one to two days. Decision needed: annotate the 8-thread
REPRODUCE.md records with the precise sentence "the solve path was not
demonstrated repeatable at 8 threads before L15" (recommended: yes, by
`write_reproduce.py`), without naming a mechanism before it is located.

## 3. L22: the composition movement bound and the outer progress control

**Evidence.** L7e memo section 23: in the two refusing well-mixed states
every carrier relaxation of every pass ended on the movement bound
(5.0e-3 -> 2.5e-3 -> 1.25e-3 -> floor 1.0e-3), the bound attained at the
H2 front (cell 231, 1.26 Rp) or in the far wind (cell 466, 16.5 Rp), while
the refusing cell is decades away in abundance (cell 500, x2 = 7.8e-3;
cell 306, x2 = 2.7e-6). The R4 Jacobian corrections are in and the cases
still refuse. Three catalog cases are on the bound
(`molecular_scalar_gj1132_wellmixed` 0.083 and 0.55, `kzz1e9/HeH0.083`);
`wellmixed/HeH2.13` has no seed, which is a different statement.

**What the defect is.** The maximum over cells and a bound in every cell
describe the same feasible set. The defect is one update multiplier and
one cumulative excursion budget applied to a front and a wind whose
responses differ by decades; regional maxima alone would not enlarge the
set, only the path inside it. The wind's response is not local: the EOS
is, but the pressure coupling and the attenuation are not, and at fixed
conserved thermal energy the pressure `p = (gamma-1) e_th` does not
depend on the particle count while the temperature does
(`pressure_and_temperature_at_fixed_conserved_state`). So the quantity to
monitor is not the particle count alone but p, T, the sources and the
coupled residual.

**Step 1, the progress diagnostic.** The outer loop's progress test
compares `comp_drift_last` with `comp_drift_before`
(`EXHALE_main.f90` lines 6631, 6809-6810); a smaller movement limit makes
the drift smaller and reads as contraction. Fix: progress is judged on
the unbounded fixed-point defect of the composition (the carrier half of
L14's rule already is) and never on a bounded displacement; the log says
which quantity decided. Test row in `carrier_retry`.

**Step 2, the causal comparisons** on the frozen `wellmixed/HeH0.083`
state, single-threaded, with the partition FROZEN for the comparison
(x2 > 1e-2 at entry; the memo's own case whose x2 never falls below 1e-2
is the reason this is a diagnostic partition and not a physics one):
(a) the relaxation with the multiplier chosen by the tightest cell
outside the front region, conservation not claimed; does cell 500
descend? (b) the same on `kzz1e9/HeH0.083`, where the far wind sets the
bound. If neither far cell descends, the obstruction is elsewhere (outer
boundary carrier treatment, the deferred reconstruction terms of the
Jacobian) and no interval design answers it.

**Step 3, the contract, chosen from step 2.** Two algorithms are kept
apart. (i) Cell-local pseudo-time preconditioning of the UNCHANGED
residual: `M_j dU_j/dtau_j + R_j(U) = 0` with one common physical face
flux; different positive `dtau_j` do not move the root, intermediate
iterates need not conserve an inventory, admissibility and the vanishing
of the unmodified conservative residual are the requirements. This is
tested first, because it needs no region table. (ii) Conservative
physical subcycling: neighbors exchange the same time-integrated face
flux over a common synchronization interval, with accumulated face
transfers and source integration; this is written only if (i) fails.
Taking the shorter interval at a face (rev0) is neither and is withdrawn.
Candidate "coupled carrier block Newton" is kept as (iii) with the
statement that a linearized pressure derivative at fixed hydro is not
the wind response, and that a coupled block still needs positivity,
simplex feasibility and a nonlinear acceptance.

**Acceptance.** Residual decrease under fixed scales, element and charge
balances, admissibility, and independence of the certified state from
the iteration controls; the conservation check is stated as a normalized
metric (face flux mismatch relative to the larger of the two face fluxes,
1e-14) rather than a bare number. Agreement with the one-bound route on
`kzz1e9/HeH2.13` (pass 11) is a comparison under the same operator, not
an oracle. Tests: `carrier_retry`, `carrier_returned_state_acceptance`.
Goldens: `mol_carrier` is the one to watch; the marching `mol_*` snapshots
do not enter the relaxation. Effort: two to three days. Decision needed:
open it (recommended).

## 5. L25: the low-Mach base of the seven low-XUV atomic cases

**Evidence.** L17 memo: at 0.02 of the fiducial XUV the base is subsonic
to Mach 1e-8 with alternating-sign velocities in cells 1-6, the HLLC star
flux selected by the sign of S* (`Num_Fluxes.f90` lines 178-187) puts the
base cells on a kink; a C1 blend of the two star fluxes covered 409 of 500
cells and moved Mdot by 2 percent, reverted. After L21, `x0.10/HeH9.7`
and photochem x0.15/0.20/0.25 hold the cell-1 mass row near ||R|| 1.5 at
dtau0 = 1 and 1e8 alike (`lhs1140b_rerun_20260915.md`).

**Premises corrected.** A base at Mach 1e-8 with a large density carries a
finite mass flux and its momentum balance is hydrostatic at leading order
only; the finite-flow equations and their certification stay. The
transonic solution is selected by the reservoir entropy, the heating and
the subsonic structure together, so a base-local change may move Mdot,
and the acceptance is the physics judgment first and the movement
reported after it. Candidate (c) of rev0 (certify the momentum row on its
hydrostatic part) is withdrawn: it changes the governing equation, and
the failing row is the mass row. A well-balanced, algebraically
equivalent evaluation of the FULL momentum residual remains allowed.

**Step 0.** L26 (section 6) first: the boundary's own kinks are probed
before any kink is attributed to the flux.

**Step 1, the existing damping option.** `low_mach_dissipation.f90`
already adds a Mach-gated Jameson fourth-difference stress with its
energy pair to the momentum flux (`RK_rhs.f90` line 166; the production
stationary residual goes through `RK_rhs`; the quadruple-precision
diagnostic route refuses it). Measure it on the frozen `x0.10/HeH9.7`
state and on the certified 0.03 control. Before relying on it, check its
discrete energy quadratic form with the actual volume weights, the
nonuniform grid and the gate: the header's nonnegative-dissipation
argument drops boundary terms of a variable-coefficient integration and
is not established as written; if it fails, a weighted composition of
negative-semidefinite second differences is the form to write.

**Step 2, a defined low-Mach flux.** Rieper (2011), eq. (3.15)-(3.16),
verified in the published PDF: the normal velocity jump entering the Roe
dissipation is scaled by `min(Ma_face, 1)` with `Ma_face` from the Roe
averages; the eigenstructure is untouched. The code location is the Roe
branch of `Num_flux` (lines 324-331): `dvel = vR - vL` enters `a1` and
`a3`; the central flux, the well-balanced pressure departure `dp_wb` and
the admissibility logic stay. The one-dimensional factor is `|U_Roe|/a_Roe`;
its behavior where neighboring velocities cancel in the Roe average
(the alternating base mode) is probed explicitly. Rieper section 5 says
the correction is an accuracy fix, not a stiffness fix (explicit steps
stay acoustically limited, implicit solves stay slow), and that HLL and
Rusanov are not suited to it; an HLLC adaptation needs its own
derivation of the wave contributions. So L25 measures spatial accuracy,
Jacobian-action regularity and nonlinear convergence as three separate
quantities, on: a stationary contact, a resolved low-speed flow with a
fixed background, a compressible control (hot Uranus, WASP), then the
gravity-balanced and chemically coupled base; the low-Mach analysis is
applied to departures from the gravitational equilibrium, not to a
flattened background. The spatial extent of any Mach-gated change is set
by a resolution-aware criterion (cells whose face Mach lies below the
truncation of the pressure departure), not by "cells 1-20".

**Acceptance.** The certified 0.03 control re-certifies to tolerance; the
regression bases are byte-identical where the gate is closed; the
low-Mach tests reproduce their known solutions; `x0.10/HeH9.7` certifies
or its obstruction is named on the row that holds it. Effort: two days of
measurement, more for an adopted flux. Decision needed: open it; the
order is L26, step 1, step 2.

## 6. L26 (new): continuity of the wind-window base boundary

**Evidence.** `base_boundary.f90`: `wind_window_mass_flux` (lines 484-526)
averages rho v r^2 over `j_flux..N` and sets `have_F = .false.` when the
mean is exactly zero; the blend (lines 660-677) then returns the cell-1
weight `w_i`. With every window flux equal to eps != 0 the spread is zero,
the window takes full weight, and as eps -> 0 the weight tends to 1/2
(`characteristic_branch_weight(0)`), while at eps = 0 it is `w_i`. The
boundary density is therefore discontinuous on the class of states a
hydrostatic initial condition belongs to, and a Newton perturbation
crosses it. Also, the spread's `max` and `min` have kinks in U when the
extremal cell changes inside the blending interval, so the weight is C1
in its scalar argument and not in the state.

**Plan.** Frozen-state probes on the certified 0.03 state and on the
hydrostatic initial condition of `x0.02`: the boundary density and its
directional derivative along (i) a constant window flux approaching zero
from both signs, (ii) local and window signs disagreeing, (iii) an
extremal cell switching, (iv) a transient with the base flux differing
from the window's. Then the repair, whichever the probes require: the
zero-flux exception replaced by the same limit the blend takes (weight
1/2 at zero window Mach with `s_wind` from a spread that is defined at
zero), and the spread measured by a smooth norm (a root-mean-square
departure from the mean) instead of `max - min`. The durable objective
stays what section 7 of `restart_contract_design_20260909.md` states: a
boundary set by the physical reservoir and the local characteristics;
agreement between solver routes is necessary and not sufficient. Tests:
`base_branch` rows for (i)-(iii). Goldens: the regression bases lie above
the window threshold and must stay byte-identical; measured. Effort: one
day. Decision needed: open it (recommended, before L25).

## 7. L7g remainder: the deposit of the H2 association energy

**Evidence.** `molecular_reaction_heat.f90` lines 469-496: the whole
4.478 eV of R15 is multiplied by `h2_vibrational_heat_fraction`, the
Hollenbach & McKee `(1 + n_cr/n)^-1` for v = 1, written with one A and
the H and H2 v = 1 quenching fits, helium deliberately absent
(`h2_vibrational_relaxation.f90` lines 189-230); the comment at the site
states the effective-fraction assumption and its validity (n far above
n_cr of every level). The ledger closes on the ionization potential once;
R23's 8.10 eV photon leaves the gas. The R6 channel is `H3+ + e -> H2 + H`
(reaction table lines 261-267), so its products include a molecule.

**What is wrong in the present term.** The three-body energy partitions as
`D0 = E_trans + E_int(H2) + E_int(M)`; only the internal share is subject
to a radiative-or-collisional branching, the translational share is
thermalized at once. Multiplying the whole D0 by the vibrational fraction
suppresses that prompt share in the low-density limit, which is the wrong
limit even though it evaluates to 1 in the present layer. Hollenbach &
McKee (1979) treats grain formation and the H- route and gives no

**Correction to the design.** Section 1.3 of the design transports the
stage fraction on the element flux, which already carries the eddy term
(`binary_element_diffusion.f90` line 2009, `rho_f (D_f + K_f)/dr`), and
adds `Phi_x = -n_tot K d(x n_el/n_tot)/dr` again. With `y = n_el/n_tot`,
`-n_tot K d(xy)/dr = x J_el,eddy - n_el K dx/dr`; since `x J_el` already
holds `x J_el,eddy`, the additional stage term is `-n_el K dx/dr` only.
The implementation makes the same derivation in its own mass or number
convention. Mandatory discrete test: the sum of the stage fluxes equals
the element flux on the actual faces, with a nonuniform abundance,
constant and varying fractions and nonzero Kzz; charge consistency and
the helium simplex beside it. The neglect of drift between charge stages
stays an approximation with its collisional regime stated at the site.
