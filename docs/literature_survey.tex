\noindent This survey gathers, for HD\,209458\,b, HD\,189733\,b, WASP-52\,b, and
WASP-121\,b, the observational detections and the theoretical/model studies of the
upper-atmosphere transmission tracers targeted by \texttt{EXHALE}: hydrogen
H$\alpha$ and Ly$\alpha$, the metastable He\,\textsc{i} 10830 triplet, and the
metal excess-absorption lines. For each model study we record the code or method,
the initial conditions/setup, and the reported results. References are given as
clickable ADS bibcodes; entries are drawn from an ADS query and its abstracts.
