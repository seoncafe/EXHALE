## 1. The verdict

**The boundary's composition now carries an accuracy contract, and it closes
P1 on the state that opened it.** The refused generation
`molecular_scalar_gj1132_kzz1e9/HeH9.7` `g0004_20260919T103629Z_9b8394a3`
evaluated on a scratch copy gives ONE answer, independent of the composition
the ghost solve is started from: with the seed unset, with
`the_state_file_ghost_rows` and with the perturbed seed of step 1 the
evaluation CERTIFIES, reproduces the stored verdict at cell 210 digit for
digit, and reads the same cell-1 continuity row 2.923E-09 in all three. The
three states stand at most 8.0e-11 apart over every column of both state
files. Under the entry text the same three seeds gave 8.335E-08 (REFUSED),
2.923E-09 and 2.925E-09.

**What the contract is.** A sweep is a map from the composition it is entered
at to the composition it returns, and the lower boundary is built on the two
lower ghost cells of that returned composition. The sweep now applies its own
map again, at the ghost it returned and at the composition the cells above the
ghosts were HANDED, until the ghost's species fractions and its
heavy-particle plus electron count both move by at most 1e-6 over one
application. A ghost that does not reach that in twenty applications stops the
run by name, with the numbers, as the ghost's H2 partition closure already
does. The seed the solve starts from is stated by the boundary model, travels
in the model identity a state carries, and is printed in the boundary report.

**What it costs.** One application where the ghost is already at its fixed
point, which is the ordinary case, since the ghost a sweep is entered at is
the one the previous sweep returned. Four applications on the frozen state
from the reservoir row, two from the state's own ghost rows.

## 2. The accuracy, and why that number

The base continuity row of the first physical cell is a near-cancellation of
two face fluxes, so it reads the ghost composition with a large gain. READ,
step 1: a ghost difference of 4.7e-3 in the trace ions takes that row from
3.90 to 111.3 of its own rounding floors. That is 2.29e4 floors per relative
unit of ghost composition, so ONE rounding floor of the row is bought by
4.4e-5 of ghost composition.

**The contract is set at 1e-6, 44 times inside that.** A ghost pinned this
well cannot move the base row by a fortieth of the row's own arithmetic noise,
which is also what bounds the non-smoothness the contract itself introduces:
whether a state takes one application or two is a discrete property of the
state, and crossing that boundary moves the returned ghost by at most the
accuracy, hence by at most 1/44 of one rounding floor of the row.

**The other bound is what the solve underneath can deliver.** READ, step 1b:
the inner composition solve stops at `sqrt(dpmpar(1))` = 1.49e-8, and at
1e-14 MINPACK answers `info = 3` on 225 of 508 cells, so the returned
composition is pinned to about 1e-13 and no tighter. The map at its own fixed
point was MEASURED at 1.5e-5 after one application and 4e-13 after two. 1e-6
sits two decades above the inner solve's stopping accuracy and is crossed by
the second application.

**The pass bound is twenty.** The furthest seed the boundary can present is
the reservoir row, 9.7e-1 from the ghost in H II (READ, step 1b section 6.1),
and the map's measured gain is 4.9e-3 per application, so four applications
carry that seed to 5e-10. Twenty is five times what the measured contraction
needs from the worst seed. MEASURED here: four applications from the reservoir
row on the frozen state, three or four on every molecular catalog state.

**What is compared, and the floor.** The species densities of both lower ghost
cells, in the mass-fraction layout the state carries, each against its own
value; and the ghost's heavy-particle plus electron count, which is the
thermodynamic leg. Species below 1e-18 of the cell's own density are left out:
a stage that small moves the ghost's charge, particle count and opacity by at
most 1e-12 relative, twelve decades below the 4.4e-5 that one rounding floor
of the base row costs, and a run without metals writes its metal columns at a
floor of order 1e-301 whose last digits are not a composition (READ, step 1b
section 10).

## 3. The temperature leg, inside the contract

The leg D5b-2 left open is the pair the sweep does not iterate: the
composition sets the particle count, the particle count and the boundary's
pressure set the temperature the balance is solved at, and the balance sets
the composition (READ,
[lhs1140b_stationary_D5b2_20260918.md](lhs1140b_stationary_D5b2_20260918.md)
section 12).

The contract closes it in the quantity the boundary reads. The ghost's
heavy-particle plus electron count is what turns its temperature into the
pressure `base_ghost_averages` builds and into the internal energy and sound
speed the caloric map returns, so a ghost whose composition has stopped moving
but whose count has not is a ghost whose rates belong to one gas and whose
thermodynamic state to another. The count is therefore measured across every
application and held to the same 1e-6, and the applications rebuild the
temperature-dependent rates from the composition each one is entered at, so
the rates the returned ghost carries are the rates of the returned ghost.

MEASURED on the frozen state: the count closes to 3.66E-16 (seed unset),
2.44E-16 (the state file's ghost rows) and 4.75E-15 (the perturbed seed), ten
decades inside the accuracy, so on this state the composition is what binds
and the count follows it. The leg is nonetheless part of the contract and
fails loudly on its own.

What the contract does NOT do is force the reservoir's prescribed particles
per unit mass and the ghost's own count together. They remain two quantities,
reported side by side; item P6c has since MEASURED the distance between them
at 1.3e-07 here and 2.7e-08 on the hot-Uranus state, the 4.7 per cent of the
first text being a level count against a ghost count one cell lower
([lhs1140b_p6b_p6c_20260920.md](lhs1140b_p6b_p6c_20260920.md)). Restating the
reservoir at the solved count was MEASURED to take
the cell-1 row of a certified state from 1.2e-08 to 1.0 (READ, D5a section
6.2).

## 4. The seed is part of the boundary model

READ, step 1b section 6.1: the reservoir row and the ghost the solve returns
are the same gas to 2.2e-5 in hydrogen's own partition and two different gases
in the trace ions, the reservoir row carrying 31 times the ghost's H II and 26
times less H3+. The choice is not neutral, and under the entry text it was
made in `load_IC`'s handling of rows it drops.

It is now stated by the boundary, in `base_ghost_composition_seed_id`:

> the reservoir row at the first sweep, the ghost the previous sweep returned
> after it

which is what the code does. The reservoir row is the seed of record at the
first sweep because it is the only seed that is a function of the physical
column and the declared inputs alone, which is why `load_IC` installs it in
place of the ghost rows a restart pair carries; from the second sweep on the
ghost the previous sweep returned is the state's own ghost and is what the
composition array already holds. The contract is what makes the choice cost
nothing but applications.

The statement travels three ways: in the boundary model identity, which
`write_output` writes into every state file; in the boundary report, in words;
and in `load_IC`'s own message at the read, which now points at the boundary's
statement instead of making one of its own.

**The model identity moves to v3.**

| | |
|---|---|
| v2 | `characteristic_face_ps_reservoir_C_minus_contact_upwind_v2` |
| v3 | `characteristic_face_ps_reservoir_C_minus_contact_upwind_ghost_fixed_point_seed_reservoir_row_v3` |

A state written under v2 is REPORTED and loaded, not refused: `load_IC` says
which model wrote it, that this run solves v3, and that the ghost composition
is solved to this model's own seed and fixed point, so the state's residual is

## 6. One sweep is one sweep: the frozen background

The first delivery of this contract broke `mol_carrier`, and the reason is
worth keeping.

`bg_ready` is false until a sweep has filled the frozen background, and the
cell loop reads it to decide whether the transported carriers are something
this sweep INITIALIZES from the local equilibrium of the seeded state or
something handed to it and imposed (READ, the imposed-fraction block of the
cell loop). It is set at the end of the block loop. With the applications
inside the sweep, the second application therefore behaved like a LATER sweep
and imposed a carrier partition the first application had just produced.

MEASURED on `mol_carrier` at step 0: 185 cells then left the physical simplex
and were clamped onto the element budget, every cell of the wind rested on a
non-root, the coupled source step reached its pass cap of 20 without a fixed
point, and the run stopped at step 50 on the relaxation amnesty (cell 502,
1000 consecutive non-root sweeps). The control has no non-root acceptance and
no clamp at all in 7627 steps.

With `bg_ready` held at the value the sweep was entered with, MEASURED on the
same case at one step: no non-root acceptance, no clamp, and the state differs
from the control only in the lowest ghost row, by 2.1e-10.

The general statement, which is what the comment at the hold says: the
applications are ONE sweep, so nothing that separates one sweep from the next
may turn over between them. `bg_ready` was the only such flag found in
`ioniz_eq`; `ieq_rates_ready` is read outside the sweep, the per-sweep
counters are reset at each application, and the two cross-sweep counts are
taken at the first application.

## 10. What the contract does not settle

- **The base row still rests on a cancellation.** The contract removes the
  ghost's dependence on its seed; it does not change the conditioning of the
  row that reads the ghost. A boundary residual that does not rest on the
  near-cancellation of two face fluxes remains worth having, and step 1b
  section 5 is the measurement that says it would be about the ghost's own
  state and the face areas, not about the interior.
- **The reservoir's prescribed particle count and the ghost's own are still
  two quantities.** Item P6c measured them: they stand at 1.3e-07 in the
  particles per unit mass, which is what the ghost construction shares, and
  the 4.7 per cent is a count at the base level against a count one cell
  below it ([lhs1140b_p6b_p6c_20260920.md](lhs1140b_p6b_p6c_20260920.md)).
- **Six molecular catalog states still refuse**, for reasons the ghost
  composition does not reach (section 8).
- **The atomic ghost is not under the contract.** Its seed dependence has not
  been measured, and every measurement of steps 1, 1b and 2 was made on a
  molecular ghost. Extending it is a measurement first, not an edit.
- **A full stationary solve under the contract has not been run.** What is
  measured here is the evaluate route and the marching route. Whether the
  JFNK iteration sees a different operator, and how the discreteness of the
  application count enters its finite-difference action, is not settled by
  this item; section 2 bounds the discontinuity at 1/44 of one rounding floor
  of the base row, which is an argument and not a measurement of the solve.
