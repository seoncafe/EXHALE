3. WHY THE TWO OFF ARMS ARE OFF -- PATH, NOT CURVATURE
======================================================================
  Two of the nine arms depart from 1.108 by more than 0.05 sigma:
  W1132 (+0.291) and K0 (-0.263).  Both are the NEAREST-ARM seeding, and
  both have a like-for-like partner (W1132p, K0p) that lands on 1.108.
  Two independent tests say the cause is the seed path and not the shape
  of the ladder.

  CURVATURE IS RULED OUT DIRECTLY.  A quadratic through the three arms
  of record nearest each crossing, evaluated at the crossing, differs
  from the chord between the two bracketing arms by

     GJ 699 well mixed    +5.2e-06 %A
     GJ 1132 well mixed   -7.5e-04 %A
     K_zz = 1e9           +8.1e-05 %A
     K_zz <= 1e4 plateau  +1.7e-04 %A
     flux closure         +6.0e-05 %A

  The largest of these is -7.5e-04 %A = 0.025 sigma, and it has the
  WRONG SIGN to explain W1132's +8.7e-03.  Curvature over these brackets
  is a hundredth of the measurement error; it cannot be what moved
  either arm.

  THE OUTER REGION IS WHERE THEY DIFFER.  measure.py's outer-column
  indicator -- the share of the He 2^3S radial column beyond 10 R_p, the
  Update_EXHALE section 83 measure -- is flat within each ladder of
  record and is the one indicator on which the two off arms fall
  outside their own ladder's range:

     ladder of record          its range     confirmation arms
     W2, rs_ (GJ 1132)         0.0260-0.0262  W1132  0.0263  OUTSIDE, high
                                              W1132p 0.0260  inside
     kz0 (plateau)             0.0263-0.0270  K0     0.0257  OUTSIDE, low
                                              K0p    0.0264  inside
     G2, g2_ (GJ 699)          0.0629-0.0631  G699   0.0631  inside
                                              G699p  0.0630  inside
     K1, kz_ (K_zz = 1e9)      0.0262-0.0265  K1e9   0.0264  inside
                                              K1e9p  0.0264  inside
     rw_ (closure)             0.0094-0.0095  C9p048 0.0094  inside

  The sign matches in both cases: W1132 carries MORE metastable column
  beyond 10 R_p than its ladder and its equivalent width is HIGH; K0
  carries LESS and its equivalent width is LOW.  That is the signature
  of a seed-inherited outer state, the same one ../crossings_gm25
  identified in its own first well-mixed lineage (0.0266 and 0.0258
  against 0.0259-0.0262) and in the chained low end of its closure
  ladder.  W1132 was seeded from rs_heh0p425, which is itself an arm of
  the W2 lineage, so it is a second-generation seed; K0 from
  kz0_heh3p79, likewise.  Their like-for-like partners, seeded from the
  same parent as the ladder, do not carry it.

  Every other indicator is clean at every arm.  T4k = 0 everywhere: no
  arm carries a step in n(2^3S) at the T = 4000 K seam of the retired
  Taylor Penning fit.  The eight scalar-base arms flag no departure from
  the local trend anywhere; C9p048 flags 2 cells, at 20.1 R_p where T
  falls, which is the same two-cell pattern every closure arm of record
  carries.  No cell has C+N+O = 0 in the closure arm (0/0, equilibrium
  and advected); the other eight carry no metals at all, so the test
  does not apply.  r_drop is 30.00 (the domain edge, T never falling
  below half of T(12 R_p)) for the eight scalar-base arms and 20.12 R_p
  for the closure arm, which is the value its two neighbours of record
  (9.0 and 9.1) both carry.

  SO: the interpolation is confirmed, and the residual scatter around it
  is the seed path, of order 1 per cent in reservoir He/H -- smaller
  than the 1.7 per cent ../crossings_gm25 measured at K_zz = 1e9 and far
  smaller than the half unit it measured on the closure.  Nothing here
  is evidence of curvature the interpolation missed.

5. THE PHOTOCHEM INTERPRETER
======================================================================
  The closure arm ran on the REPOSITORY environment,
  env/photochem/bin/python -- photochem 0.9.0, the corrected elemental
  closure.  The closure log records it as

     chemistry interpreter .../EXHALE_v1.00/env/photochem/bin/python
     (the repository default)

  and the profile it wrote carries `# source_version photochem 0.9.0`.

  How that was arranged, and why: run_closure.sh builds each arm's
  closure.json out of ../flux_closure/heh9p7/closure.json.  That stored
  file names "python":
  /home/kiseon/.conda/envs/photochem_cmp/bin/python, which is photochem
  0.8.4.  This directory's copy of the script DELETES that line rather
  than rewriting it, and with no "python" key element_flux_closure.py
  resolves the interpreter to env/photochem/bin/python
  (repository_chemistry_python(), src/utils/element_flux_closure.py
  line 96).  0.8.4 cannot be used unqualified any more: the adapter
  default --abundance-tol is 1.0e-10 (src/utils/lower_profile_schema.py
  line 452) and 0.8.4 leaves up to 2e-4 in the deepest-level N/H, so the
  handoff check refuses it.  Photochem 0.9.0 is the build
  ../crossings_pc090 re-measured this same ladder on, arm for arm,
  finding the equivalent width moved by at most 1.2e-06 relative and the
  crossing not at all (9.0484 / 9.0461 on both builds); the arms of
  record this confirmation is judged against, rw_9p0 and rw_9p1, were
  themselves reproduced there to within 3.8e-07 and 1.4e-07 relative.
  So the comparison in section 4 is not a comparison across chemistry
  builds in any way that reaches the line.

  Everything else in closure.json is the stored file unchanged:
  resid_tol 4.0e-4, omp_num_threads 4, tol 0.05, kmax 8, p_match 1e-6
  bar, --climate, --climate-p-deep 20.0, --kzz-const 1.0e9,
  --atoms H,He,N,O,C, the GJ 1132 proxy SED at the b orbit, and the
  starting fluxes phi0_H = 4.7682069882E+06,
  phi0_He = 2.0433648550E+07 that the parent converged on.
