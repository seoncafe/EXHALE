  THE +1-SIGMA END, MEASURED

  Which end this is: the reservoir 8.49 that ../crossings_gm25 could only
  interpolate is the end of the 1-sigma band at EW = 1.108 - 0.030 =
  1.078, which crossing.py labels `+1sig` (a larger equivalent width
  needs a larger reservoir, so the low reservoir goes with the low EW).
  EW = 1.138 is the other end, at reservoir 10.77, and no arm run here
  is anywhere near it -- it was already bracketed by two solved arms,
  10.6 and 11.0, and is unchanged.

     interpolated across the un-runnable band (../crossings_gm25):
         bracket [8.45, 8.75]   chord 8.4923   quadratic 8.5080
     measured, the band now run:
         bracket [8.45, 8.50]   chord 8.4649   quadratic 8.4673

  The interpolation was right to 0.3 percent, and the bracket it was
  taken across, 0.30 wide in reservoir, is now 0.05 wide.  The +1-sigma
  end of the closure is 8.465, and the 1-sigma band of the closure
  crossing becomes 8.465 - 10.77 in place of 8.49 - 10.77.  The crossing
  itself, 9.048, is untouched: none of the new arms lies in its bracket.

  What the new arms also show is that the ladder is not smooth there.
  Between 8.45 and 8.5 the equivalent width rises by 0.0122 %A, and
  between 8.5 and 8.65 by 0.0048 %A over three times the reservoir
  interval -- a local slope eight times smaller.  The step is not a
  lineage artifact of this directory: rep_heh8p45 and rep_heh8p75
  reproduce the two arms of record to every printed digit, so the two
  sides of it are measured the same way.  It appears to be the closure's
  own tol = 0.05 noise: 8.45 through 8.65 satisfy the tolerance at k = 2,
  8.7 and 8.75 at k = 1, i.e. on a lower-atmosphere profile one
  under-relaxed update older, and the arms that stop at k = 1 carry the
  looser residual (0.0447-0.0487 against 0.0218-0.0284).

  Re-closing three of the new arms from the neighbour below instead of
  from the common parent moves them by -1.2, -0.6 and -1.3 percent in
  equivalent width (D8p5, D8p65, D8p7 above), the same sign and the same
  size as the -1.9 percent the 8.75 arm of record moved between seeds.
  On that lineage the band is not monotone and the +1-sigma end reads
  8.563 instead of 8.465.  So the seed systematic on this end is about
  1.2 percent in EW, which at the local slope of the single-parent
  ladder is a large fraction of a unit in reservoir -- the same
  conclusion ../crossings_gm25 reached for the crossing, now measured on
  the band itself rather than across it.
