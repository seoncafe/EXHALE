purpose  put every row of tab:bump of docs/lhs1140b_exhale_vs_pwinds.tex
         on one vintage: the He 2^3S + H total ionization coefficient of
         Garcia Munoz (2025) (Update_EXHALE section 87) and the current
         advection post-process (section 88).  Before this, only the
         He/H = 0.55 row carried the current coefficient, and even there
         the advection-corrected pair predated section 88.

method   resolve_wind.sh, copied from crossings_gm25/resolve_wind.sh and
         generalized for arms that sit one directory level higher (the
         relative SED path gains one "..") and that have no
         lower-atmosphere profile (these six use the scalar base).
         For each arm: its own stored output/Hydro_ioniz.txt and
         output/Ion_species.txt are the restart, the composition and
         every other input line are unchanged, and the wind is re-solved
         with the JFNK steady-state solver (EXHALE_PTC=1,
         EXHALE_PTC_JFNK=1, EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0=1.0) four times, each pass
         seeded from the previous pass's output, so the arm is at its own
         fixed point.  Then one "Do only PP: True" pass writes the
         *_adv.txt profiles, and EXHALE_transit.py synthesizes the line
         with EXHALE_TRANSIT_RES_HETR = 68000 (../../winered_hires_y.sh).
         OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 per arm, six arms at once (12 of 72 cores).
         Convergence tolerance is the arms' own "Resid tol: 1.0e-3".

         The six source directories were read only.  Nothing under
         ../heh0p55, ../solar, ../heh1000, ../heh0p06_gj699,
         ../solar_gj699 or ../heh1000_gj699 was written.

4. THE Python TRANSCRIPTION OF THE RATE COEFFICIENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
LHS1140b/bump_analysis.py carried the retired Taylor et al. (2025)
two-branch Penning power law, and its docstring pointed at a routine
name (penning_HeI_23S) that no longer exists.  Applied to a solution of
the current binary it made the recovered 2^3S photoionization rate
NEGATIVE (-2.15e-4 s^-1), which is impossible.  The two functions of the
current source were transcribed in its place, ioniz_HeI23S_H and
ioniz_HeI23S_H2 (Cool_coeff.f90:1040 and 1076), together with the
branching constant f_penning_HeI23S = 0.9 (Cool_coeff.f90:552).  The
metastable balance takes the TOTAL of the two channels, so the sink is
unscaled; the constant is carried because the heating and proton-source
terms use it.

Checked against the compiled Fortran (a driver linked against
build/Cool_coeff.o), 300 - 10 000 K, max relative deviation:

  ioniz_HeI23S_H     8.1e-16      alpha_23S    1.1e-08
  ioniz_HeI23S_H2    1.8e-16      q13          1.2e-05
  f_penning_HeI23S   exact        q31a         5.1e-07
                                  q31b         7.4e-07
                                  ci_23S       6.3e-08

The five older coefficients agree to 1e-5 or better; the residue is the
single-precision literals the Fortran writes (2.10e-13, 0.06703,
6.41e-21, ...) against full double precision in Python.  Gamma_2^3S
recovered from the corrected balance is now positive on every arm,
5.5e-6 to 1.4e-5 s^-1 at the density maximum.
