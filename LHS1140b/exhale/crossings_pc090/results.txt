5. WHICH RESULTS WERE MADE WITH PHOTOCHEM 0.8.4
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything under ../crossings_gm25, ../flux_closure, ../nh_refusal_
diagnosis/scan and every stored LHS 1140 b run directory that carries a
lower_atmosphere_profile.dat older than 2026-08-29 11:30 was written by
the conda package photochem 0.8.4 in /home/kiseon/.conda/envs/
photochem_cmp.  Each such profile records it in its own header line
"# source_version photochem 0.8.4"; the corrected build writes
"photochem 0.9.0" there, so the two are told apart per file and nothing
has to be inferred from a date.

That environment is unchanged and remains the reproduction path.  To
re-run one of those results, point closure.json's "python" at
photochem_cmp and pass --abundance-tol explicitly: the adapter default
was tightened to 1e-10 on 2026-08-29, and 0.8.4 does not meet it -- its
equilibrium solver scales every elemental residual by the largest
elemental abundance, so it leaves up to 2.1e-4 in N/H and the handoff
is refused.  That refusal is the intended signal, and the explicit
tolerance is how a deliberate reproduction says it accepts it.  The
scans in ../nh_refusal_diagnosis already pass --abundance-tol 1.0 on
their own command lines and are unaffected.

The K_zz ladder (../ladder_gm25, ../kzz_*), the GJ 699 cases and the
well-mixed and K_zz = 1e9 crossings of ../crossings_gm25 sections 1 and
2 use a scalar base, not a profile handoff.  Photochem is not in their
path and they are not affected by any of this.

7. THE 9.4 / 9.5 FAILURES, AND WHAT REMOVING THEM DID TO THIS LADDER
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Measured 2026-08-29, after the arms above.

The two failures of section 4 were not the bracketing scan.  The outer
surface-temperature solve was handing that scan a trial temperature of
6.5e8 K, at which every scan point is an invalid thermodynamic state --
the polynomials stop at 6000 K -- so there was no sign change to find.
It arrived there because its forward-difference Jacobian was numerical
noise: MINPACK's hybrd1 fixes the difference step at sqrt(machine
epsilon), which on log10(T_surf - T_trop) is a 2e-5 K step, and the
normalized flux residual moves over that step by about as much as its
own jitter.  Diagnosis: ../clima_bracket_diagnosis/README.md.

The repair is in the Clima patch EXHALE carries: hybrd1 is replaced by
hybrd so that epsfcn can be set, and it is set to 1e-8 -- a 0.16 K step,
about 1e4 above the jitter and within 0.1-0.5 percent of the derivative.
env/photochem was rebuilt with it on 2026-08-29.  The 61-point He/H grid
8.00-11.00 now solves 61/61, and 9.30-9.60 in steps of 0.01 solves 31/31.

What it did to this ladder: nothing that is visible.  Both arms that
bracket the crossing were re-run end to end on the rebuilt environment,
same run_closure.sh, resolve_wind.sh, measure.py, same seed, same binary,
same transit configuration (R = 68,000, vacuum 10832.60-10834.20 A):

  arm    EW before        EW after         relative   log10 Mdot
  9.0    1.1068538680     1.1068539227     +4.9e-08   7.39 -> 7.39
  9.1    1.1092155021     1.1092155563     +4.9e-08   7.39 -> 7.39

  crossing  chord      9.0484199308 -> 9.0484176252   (-2.3e-06)

Both closure iterations reproduced their stored path exactly: converged
at k=1 on residuals 0.03111 and 0.02567, He/H at the match 9.0100868 and
9.1102042, and info = 0 with ||R|| 2.5e-4 and 2.4e-4 at every re-solve
pass.  Every quality indicator of section 3 is unchanged: flux spread
8.52e-2 and 8.38e-2, T4k = 0, 0/0 metal-drop cells, r_drop 20.121 R_p,
outer-10 metastable share 0.0094.

The handoff profile does move, by much more than the line does, and by
much more than the climate solve moves.  At He/H = 9.0 the two builds
put the deep boundary 2.1e-6 K apart (424.9534068835 vs 424.9534089414 K,
tropopause 1.2e-7 K apart), and out of that the k00 column comes back
differing by up to 2.4 percent in pressure and up to 15 percent in one
trace species, with the elemental O/H the profile hands over differing
by 0.5 percent.  Each build is deterministic -- run twice it reproduces
its own profile bit for bit, and photochem_090_fix reproduces the stored
k00 exactly -- so the difference is real and not run-to-run scatter.
The size looks like the photochemical steady-state solver's own
tolerance rather than an amplification of 2e-6 K, but which it is was
not established here.  The base state EXHALE actually reads agrees to
1e-7 except for that O/H, and the resulting steady H flux agrees to
9.5e-8 relative.  The line does not see any of it.

The arms of this ladder were not re-run beyond those two, and the 0.8.4
comparison of section 3 was not repeated.
