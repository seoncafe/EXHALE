K_zz SCAN OF THE He 10830 CROSSING UNDER THE PROFILE BOUNDARY CONDITION
=======================================================================

What was asked.  `kzz_decision.md` section 6.1 scans K_zz on a SCALAR H/He
base and reports the reservoir He/H that reproduces the measured red-pair
equivalent width, 1.108 +/- 0.030 %A (Cherubim et al. 2026): 4.68 for
K_zz <= 1e5, 4.45 at 1e6, 3.78 at 1e7, 2.87 at 1e8, 2.09 at 1e9, 1.54 at
1e10, 1.11 at 1e11.  This directory repeats that scan with the PHOTOCHEMICAL
PROFILE boundary condition, where the answer at K_zz = 1e9 is already on
record as He/H = 11.73.

Configuration.  Every arm is `flux_closure/heh11p1/closure.json` with only
`--kzz-const` and the reservoir `He=` changed: photochem with `--climate`,
`--atoms H,He,N,O,C`, `p_match = 1 microbar`, `--climate-p-deep 20.0`, the
GJ 1132 proxy SED at the b orbit, the closure driver
`src/utils/element_flux_closure.py` at `--tol 0.05 --kmax 8`,
`resid_tol` 4.0e-4, `EXHALE_PTC=1 EXHALE_PTC_JFNK=1 EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0=1.0`.
The transit synthesis is `EXHALE_transit.py` with
`EXHALE_TRANSIT_RES_HETR=68000` (WINERED HIRES-Y, `winered_hires_y.sh`), and
the equivalent width is the red pair on the vacuum window
10832.60-10834.20 A, the same one `flux_closure/closure_heh_table.py` and
`make_memo_figures.py` use.  Every arm is seeded from the nearest converged
arm; the reservoir step is capped at a factor 1.25 and the K_zz step at half
a decade or less.

Scripts here.  `run_arm.sh` one arm; `ladder_kzz.sh` a K_zz ladder at fixed
reservoir; `ladder_heh.sh` a plain reservoir ladder at fixed K_zz;
`search_heh.sh` a secant search for the crossing; `measure.py` one row per
arm; `crossing.py` the crossing, solved twice; `clima_probe/probe.sh` the
chemistry step on its own; `make_results.sh` rebuilds this file.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ANSWER, SUCH AS IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

reservoir He/H that reproduces 1.108 +/- 0.030 %A, profile boundary
condition, against the scalar base of `kzz_decision.md` section 6.1:

  K_zz     scalar base    profile, cool branch     profile, hot branch
  1e9         2.09        11.72  (10.90-12.50)      1.64  (-- to 1.70)
  1e8         2.87        14.80  (13.62-15.17)      2.26  (-- to 2.33)
  1e7         3.78        not reached (see below)   2.889 (2.81-2.94)
  1e6         4.45        the handoff refuses       the handoff refuses
  1e5         4.68        the handoff refuses       the handoff refuses
  1e4         4.68        the handoff refuses       the handoff refuses
  1e3          --         chemistry runs, closure UNRESOLVED

The cool-branch entries at 1e9 and 1e8 are interpolated inside a bracket of
two solved arms.  The hot-branch entries at 1e9 and 1e8 are extrapolated
from the lowest arm the chemistry would solve, on the local slope measured
between that arm and the one beside it (1.7 -> 1.642 on slope 0.746 at 1e9;
2.3 -> 2.264 on slope 0.893 at 1e8), each about 3 % beyond the end of its
ladder; their +1 sigma ends ARE bracketed.  The match-surface curve is the
reservoir curve to the third digit everywhere (11.731, 14.808, 2.892).

There is no plateau at low K_zz, because the scan cannot get there: the
handoff stops at K_zz ~ 3e6.  Over the range it does cover the profile
curves run the same way as the scalar one -- more helium is needed as K_zz
falls -- and the hot-branch curve tracks the scalar base at a nearly
constant offset (0.79, 0.79, 0.76 of it at 1e9, 1e8, 1e7), while the cool
branch sits 5.2-5.6 times above it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE THREE RESULTS BEHIND THAT TABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  THE WIND HAS TWO CONVERGED STATES AT EVERY K_zz THAT WAS REACHED, and
    the He 10830 line is about three to four times stronger on one of them.
    At K_zz = 1e7 and reservoir He/H = 3.4 the two solutions are

        kzz1e7/heh3p4     log10 Mdot 7.54   red depth 1.64 %   EW 0.4287 %A
        kzz1e7/heh3p399   log10 Mdot 7.87   red depth 4.86 %   EW 1.3248 %A

    at the same K_zz and the same reservoir ratio to 0.03 %, both with
    `info = 0`, ||R|| = 1.3e-4 and 1.1e-4, and flux-window spreads under
    1 %.  Which one is found is set by the seed: `heh3p4` was continued
    upward from He/H = 3.0 on the cool branch, `heh3p399` downward from
    He/H = 4.933 on the hot branch.  The hot state is not confined to low
    K_zz -- it was carried up to 1.78e7, 3.16e7, 1e8 and to the adopted 1e9
    (`kzz1e9/heh13p875hot`, log10 Mdot 7.82, EW 3.970, ||R|| 3.0e-5).  The
    hot state is hotter and more ionized at the same composition: at
    K_zz = 1e7 and He/H = 11.1, T_max 7606 K against 5412 K and x(HII) at
    2 R_p 0.140 against 0.119 (the cool comparison is 1.78e7, the nearest
    K_zz at which the cool state still exists at that reservoir).  log10
    Mdot is bimodal with nothing between 7.60 and 7.82 in any arm measured,
    which is what `crossing.py` splits on.

    The recorded He/H = 11.73 at K_zz = 1e9 is therefore the crossing ON THE
    COOL BRANCH.  Whether the hot state is a second physical state of this
    planet or an attractor of the coupled wind/composition Picard iteration
    is NOT settled by this scan.

2.  THE COOL BRANCH DOES NOT REACH THE MEASUREMENT BELOW K_zz ~ 3e7.  At
    fixed reservoir He/H = 11.1 the cool-branch equivalent width falls
    1.0853 (1e9) -> 1.0251 (1e8) -> 0.9704 (3.16e7) -> 0.9788 (1.78e7),
    so more helium is needed as K_zz falls, and the crossing moves
    11.72 (1e9) -> 14.80 (1e8) -> off the end.  At 3.16e7 the cool branch
    could not be continued past He/H = 13.7 (`kzz3p16e7/heh13p736` and
    `heh13p875` both leave it), and the crossing there needs about 19.  At
    K_zz = 1e7 the cool branch ends at He/H = 3.8 with EW = 0.4661, and the
    He/H = 4.2 continuation is UNRESOLVED with F_He already at the
    hot-branch value -- the cool state ceases to exist, it does not merely
    fail to converge.  So at 1e7 the measurement falls in the gap between
    the cool branch's ceiling (0.4661) and the hot branch's floor (1.0753 at
    He/H = 2.8), and the only crossing at that decade is the hot one,
    He/H = 2.889.

3.  BELOW K_zz ~ 3e6 THE HANDOFF ITSELF REFUSES, for a stated physical
    reason: the top of the photochemical column stops being H2-dominated.
    `photochem_to_lower_profile.py` imposes the whole elemental hydrogen
    escape flux on H2 and refuses when the converged top carries more than
    1 % of its hydrogen nuclei as atomic H.  Measured share at the top
    (chemistry alone, He/H = 11.1, fixed trial fluxes): 4.4e-3 at 1.78e7,
    5.7e-3 at 1e7, 9.4e-3 at 3.16e6, 1.5e-2 at 1e6, 2.1e-2 at 1e5,
    8.1e-3 at 1e4.  With the closure's own (larger) hydrogen flux the
    refusal reaches 1e4 as well (1.6e-2), and it is not a helium-rich
    effect: the same refusal stops the He/H = 3.0 ladder at 1e6 (1.3e-2).
    The decades 1e4, 1e5 and 1e6 of the request are therefore NOT reachable
    with this handoff as it stands.  What would open them is imposing the
    hydrogen flux on the measured H/H2 carrier mix rather than on H2 alone
    -- the adapter already has that branch, it just decides the split on the
    initial composition, which is chemical equilibrium and carries no atomic
    H.  That is a change to a shared tool and was not made here.

    K_zz = 1e3 does produce a profile, but the closure stops UNRESOLVED
    (steady-window spread 0.0995 against tol 0.05, eps_H = 0.89), and that
    profile hands over He/H = 1.42 at the match against a reservoir of 11.1
    together with O/H = 1.2e-15 and C/H = 2.8e-11 -- at that eddy
    coefficient molecular diffusion wins below the match and the profile
    delivers neither the reservoir helium nor the metals.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
RESERVOIR AGAINST MATCH SURFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Over the whole reachable range the two are the same number.  `mtch/res` in
the table below is 1.0011 at 1e9, 1.0006-1.0009 at 1e8, 0.9999 at 3.16e7,
0.9992 at 1.78e7, 1.0007 at 1e7 -- a 0.1 % separation at most, so the
reservoir curve and the match-surface curve differ only in the third digit
(11.717 against 11.731 at 1e9; 14.798 against 14.808 at 1e8; 2.889 against
2.892 at 1e7).  The eddy term beats molecular diffusion below the match
everywhere the handoff will run.  The one place the two do part company is
the K_zz = 1e3 profile of result 3, which is not a converged closure.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
RELAXATIONS, AND WHERE THEY WERE USED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

`run_arm.sh` retries a refused arm in a fixed order and records every
relaxation in that arm's own `closure.json`, so no row can be read without
it: (i) the elemental-conservation check of the handoff at 1e-3 instead of
1e-4 -- hydrogen is a minor species at these ratios and N/H carries the
solver noise on N amplified by 1/q_H; (ii) the wind residual target at
1.0e-3 then 3.0e-3 instead of 4.0e-4; (iii) `EXHALE_DIFF_OMEGA` at 0.25 then
0.125 -- the drift test inside `EXHALE_main` is the undamped distance to the
fixed point, so a smaller omega lengthens the iteration without changing
what it converges to.  The achieved ||R|| of every arm is in the table.
`kzz1p78e7/heh11p1` is the one arm quoted with a composition that had not
co-converged (drift 1.8e-2 after 20 outer passes, against 6e-4 elsewhere).

Half-decade rungs (3.16e7, 1.78e7, 1.33e7) exist because a full decade below
1e8 could not be carried: 1e8 -> 1e7 in one step left the composition
drifting at 1.7e-1 and the next steady solve returned `info = 2`
(`attempts/README.txt` and `kzz1e7/heh11p1_attempt_decade_step`).  1.33e7
and 1e7 at He/H = 11.1 then converged cleanly at the standard settings --
but on the hot branch, which is how the two states were found.

clima's surface-temperature root solve fails on scattered inputs and solves
a per cent away, so a refused reservoir is retried beside itself.  Bands
hit: He/H = 15.5-16.6 at K_zz = 1e8 (18.0 solves, and supplies the +1 sigma
bracket there), and He/H below about 2.2-2.7 on the hot branch, where the
failure is `T_surf` below `T_trop` -- that is what stops the hot-branch
ladders 3 % short of their crossings at 1e8 and 1e9.
