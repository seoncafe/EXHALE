0. WHY THE LADDER HAD TO BE REBUILT AND NOT RE-SOLVED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  The ladder is the five-row table of Update_EXHALE Sect. 78:

    case                    what it adds                  log10Mdot  red
    heh2p13_diff_kzz1e9     scalar base, He/H 2.13, 226 K    7.80   4.396
    control A               HeH_base = 2.0921                7.81   4.393
    control B               + T_base = 185.05 K              7.81   4.389
    control C               + r_base, q_H2_base              7.86   4.985
    control D               + C/N/O_H_base from the profile  7.46   1.517
    profile route           the profile itself               7.46   1.517

  Neither that table nor the memo names a run directory for controls
  A-D, and no directory in the repository carries their base.inp: over
  all 507 base.inp files under EXHALE_v1.00 the scalar keys HeH_base /
  T_base / C_H_base appear in exactly four, all of them the benchmark
  planets (benchmarks/{hd189,hd209,wasp52,wasp121}), and q_H2_base
  appears in none.  The profile route of Sect. 78 has no output either:
  LHS1140b/lower_profile/ holds the profile and its provenance base.inp
  and nothing else.  So the ladder was rebuilt from the profile the
  memo says it was built from, not re-solved from stored output.

  Two further facts fix which kernel and which coefficient the stored
  numbers are on, and both matter for the replacement values:
   - the stored red depth 4.396 for the scalar arm is exactly
     ../heh2p13_diff_kzz1e9/tpm_He10830_metrics_R80k.txt (4.395553,
     written 2026-08-25 22:40), i.e. the ladder was measured at
     R = 80,000, EXHALE_transit.py's built-in He I 10830 default, and
     NOT at the R = 68,000 WINERED HIRES-Y kernel the rest of the memo
     measures through (../../winered_hires_y.sh);
   - 2026-08-25 predates the Penning replacement of Update_EXHALE 87,
     so every rung of the stored ladder is on the retired Taylor et al.
     (2025) two-branch fit.
  Both are therefore re-measured here, and the line is synthesized twice
  per rung so the two effects can be separated: R = 68,000 in
  tpm_He10830*.txt (the memo default) and R = 80,000 in
  tpm_He10830*_R80k.txt (the stored ladder's kernel).

  "if (molecular_base)".  molecular_base is set only by the input.inp
  key "Molecular base: True" or by molecular chemistry being on
  (input_read.f90 F1001); neither the scalar route nor the profile route
  sets it -- ../flux_closure/ref/k00/EXHALE_resolved.out carries
  ntot_bc_per_H = 1.000116, the metals-only value, and so does rung E
  here.  Rung C's ntot_bc is exactly 1.000000: the run echoes
  "base.inp: photochemical q_H2 = 0.19265 is used with He/H = 2.09213"
  and then does not use it.  So the whole of rung C's move is r_base,
  and the memo's "the base radius and molecular fraction" credits the
  step to two keys of which one does nothing.  Size of what is being
  dropped, from the code's own formula and not from a run: with q_H2 =
  0.19265 and He/H = 2.0921, x2 = 0.9990 H nuclei per H nucleus are
  bound into H2 and h2_bound_fraction = 0.16153, so a molecular base
  would give ntot_bc = 0.83858 instead of 1.000116 -- a 16 per cent
  lower base particle count, hence about 19 per cent higher base nuclei
  density at the fixed 1 microbar pressure BC.  NOT RUN: switching it on
  changes both routes and is outside this ladder.

2. CONVERGENCE, RUNG BY RUNG
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  ./resolve_rung.sh <tag> <seed> 4 -- ../crossings_gm25/resolve_wind.sh
  with three changes: the rung directory carries its own input.inp and
  base.inp (they are the experiment, not the seed's), seeding from the
  rung's own output is allowed so a rung can be re-solved at a different
  tolerance, and the line is synthesized at both kernels.  Each rung:
  four JFNK passes (EXHALE_PTC=1 EXHALE_PTC_JFNK=1 EXHALE_PTC_DTAU0=1.0),
  each seeded from the previous pass's output, then one "Do only PP:
  True" pass and EXHALE_transit.py twice.  OMP_NUM_THREADS=4, one rung
  at a time.  The chain is ms_scalar <- ../thermostat_gm25/ms2p13, then
  A <- ms, B <- A, C <- B, D <- C, E <- D.

  THE TOLERANCE IS NOT UNIFORM ACROSS THE LADDER, AND HAS TO BE
  REPORTED.  The scalar arm's own input.inp carries Resid tol 5.0e-3.
  At that value the ladder is worthless on the metal rungs: the JFNK
  accepts the seed on entry without doing anything ("(JFNK) start
  ||R||= 4.806E-03 ... done info=0 ||R||= 4.806E-03" on rung D, with
  ||Fs||2 = 2.32E-01), and the element-diffusion outer loop then walks
  the composition away from a wind nobody re-solved.  Measured that way
  the ladder gives, for ms/A/B/C/D/E, log10Mdot 7.80/7.80/7.80/7.85/
  7.49/7.49 and red depths (R = 68,000) 4.9094/4.8772/4.8713/5.5441/
  1.6644/1.5235 with mass-flux spreads of 0.14 on the scalar rungs and
  0.34 and 0.28 on D and E -- i.e. rho*v*r^2 varying by a third over the
  domain, and D and E disagreeing by 9 per cent.  Those numbers are
  recorded here only so they are not mistaken for the result; the logs
  are kept as <rung>/tol5e3_cont*.log.
  Tightened, every rung was re-solved from that state.  ms/A/B/C reach
  1.0e-4.  D does not: its JFNK plateaus at 3.4-5.1e-4 while driving
  ||Fs||2 from 2.3e-1 to 4.2e-3, so it was given 4.0e-4, the tolerance
  the memo's own flux-closure arms use (../crossings_gm25 arms report
  ||R|| 1.5e-4 to 3.2e-4 at Resid tol 4.0e-4).  E plateaus at 4.09e-4,
  just above that, and was given 5.0e-4; seeded from its own 4.0e-4
  state it then converges to 2.74e-4 with info = 0 (logs
  e_profile/tol4e4_cont*.log for the attempt that returned info = 2).
  After tightening the mass-flux spread is 0.067-0.077 on every rung of
  the ladder, so all six are read at the same quality.

3. HOW THE NUMBERS WERE MEASURED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  ../crossings_gm25/measure.py, unmodified -- the same script and the
      same definitions the thermostat table of ../thermostat_gm25 is
      read with: EW is the red-pair equivalent width over the vacuum
      window 10832.60-10834.20 A of tpm_He10830.txt, "red[%]" is
      red_depth of tpm_He10830_metrics.txt (the three-Gaussian fit of
      he_line_metrics.py, which is also what the Sect. 78 table's "red"
      column is), fluxspread is the fractional radial spread of
      rho*v*r^2 above 2 R_p, and step/T4k/Zdrop/r_drop are the three
      quality indicators.
  ./ladder_table.py -- the same quantities at both kernels, plus the
      rung-to-rung steps.  It reads he_line_metrics.py's own output
      files and adds nothing to the definitions.
  Caveat on one printed column: measure.py reads "He/H number ratio"
      out of input.inp, which is 2.13 on every rung because the
      reservoir is overridden by base.inp or by the profile.  The
      reservoir actually solved is 2.13 on ms and 2.0921 on A-E
      (EXHALE_resolved.out, section 1 above).

8. WHAT WAS NOT MEASURED OR NOT SETTLED

  - The scalar rung here is the same configuration as
    ../thermostat_gm25/ms2p13 but is solved at Resid tol 1.0e-4 rather
    than the 5.0e-3 of the stored run, and gives EW 1.35289 against that
    directory's 1.34021 and red depth 4.9599 against 4.9013, i.e. 1.0
    per cent deeper, with the mass-flux spread halved (0.067 against
    0.145).  The ladder is internally consistent at its own tolerance;
    the thermostat table is internally consistent at the stored one.
    If the memo wants one number for the scalar arm, the two tables
    disagree by 1 per cent and the tolerance is the reason.
  - The tolerance is not uniform across the ladder (1.0e-4 on ms/A/B/C,
    4.0e-4 on D, 5.0e-4 on E; section 2).  Each rung is a converged
    fixed point with a flat mass flux, but the ladder is not a
    single-tolerance experiment and the D -> E 1 per cent difference is
    partly that.
  - The effect of running rung C and the profile route with
    "Molecular base: True" was computed from the code's formula
    (ntot_bc 1.0001 -> 0.8386) but NOT run.  It changes both routes and
    is a question about the profile handoff, not about this ladder.
  - The equivalent widths of the metal-free and the flux-closed 2.09
    solutions (1.129 and 0.421 in the memo) were not re-measured here;
    ../thermostat_gm25/results.txt does that.
  - Only the nominal transit configuration was synthesized, at the two
    kernels; EXHALE_TRANSIT_TURB was not run for any rung.
  - Nothing outside this directory was written.  No stored run, no
    golden, no figure and no document was touched; EXHALE was not
    rebuilt and make check was not run (no code was changed).
