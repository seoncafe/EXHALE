2. HOW THE NUMBERS WERE MEASURED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  ./thermostat.py <label>=<dir>   -- the table rows, the composition and
      ionization fractions, the 2^3S budget and the heating/cooling
      shares.  Structure quantities from the advection-corrected
      profiles (output/Hydro_ioniz_adv.txt, output/Ion_species_adv.txt),
      as the memo states; heating and cooling channels from
      output/Heating_breakdown.txt and output/Cooling_breakdown.txt,
      which are the equilibrium files, the only place the code writes
      the channel split.
  ./extra.py <label>=<dir>        -- the ionization-budget quantities at
      2 R_p (tau(13.6 eV), alpha_B n_e, the Penning and charge-exchange
      shares of case-B removal) and the two candidate definitions of
      heating per particle.
  ../crossings_gm25/measure.py    -- the EW, the line metrics and the
      three quality indicators, unmodified.

  Definitions, all taken from the code rather than assumed:
   - n_tot is calc_ntot (utilities.f90): one particle per species,
     electrons excluded, He 2^3S already inside the He I column.
   - mean mass per particle is the memo's (n_H + 4 n_He)/(n_H + n_He +
     n_e) with nuclei H I + H II and He I + He II + He III.
   - the 2^3S budget is tr_triplet_row (ion_residual_core.f90) with the
     coefficients of Cool_coeff.f90: rec_HeII_23S, coex_HeI_1S_23S,
     coex_HeI_23S_21S, coex_HeI_23S_21P, ci_HeI23S, ioniz_HeI23S_H and
     A31 = 1.272e-4.  The metastable photoionization rate is not written
     to any output file and is obtained as the residual of the balance;
     on the three re-solved arms that residual is 7-9e-6 s^-1, i.e. the
     other five terms alone reproduce the tabulated n(2^3S) to about
     6e-6 relative, which is the check that the reconstruction is right.
   - alpha_B is rec_HII_B = alphaB_HII_new: Badnell RR minus the
     Mao & Kaastra n = 1 term (legacy_hhe_rates is .false.).
   - the He+ + H charge-exchange coefficient is Table-4 row B2,
     1.25e-15 (T/300)^0.25 (charge_exchange.f90).
